### 1. Purpose (2 lines)
The Relationships Dashboard helps users intentionally nurture personal connections by tracking quality time, cadence, and connection health. CIA (the AI coach) acts as a relationship guide—offering warm, non-shaming nudges, activity suggestions, and cross-domain insights to foster human connection rather than metric-heavy guilt.

### 2. Entry and Exit Paths
*   **Entry Paths:**
    *   "Me" tab → Explore (Screen 18) → Relationships Dashboard (Stack push).
    *   CIA Chat deep-link (Screen 09) (Stack push).
*   **Exit Paths:**
    *   CIA Tab (Tap CIA Coaching Note).
    *   Goal Detail (Screen 14) (Via relationship-tagged goals).
    *   RPG Character Screen (Screen 19) (Tap Level badge).
    *   Modals: Add Person Bottom Sheet, Log Quality Time Bottom Sheet.

### 3. Information Architecture (Top-to-Bottom)
1.  **Global Navigation**
    *   Status Bar, Bottom Tab Bar (Today, CIA, Goals, Me).
2.  **Domain Header**
    *   Back button, "Relationships" title, RPG Level Badge, Domain Accent Line.
3.  **CIA Coaching Note**
    *   AI insight card with purple identity accent, avatar, message, and "ask CIA" link.
4.  **Weekly KPI Strip**
    *   Three flat-premium metric tiles summarizing the current week vs. last week.
5.  **Connection Strength Hero Gauge (96px)**
    *   The single visual focal point showing the connection score (0–99) for the focused/top person, accompanied by a status label ("in touch").
6.  **Check In (Reminders)**
    *   List of AI-generated nudges for neglected connections or upcoming dates.
7.  **Key People**
    *   Header containing the "Relationship Portfolio" Donut Chart.
    *   List of key person rows (48px satellite gauge + details).
    *   Inline expansion (on tap) revealing person-specific cadence heatmap, sparkline, and cadence-share donut.
8.  **Recent Quality Time**
    *   Log of recent interactions and "view all" link.
9.  **CIA Suggests**
    *   AI activity recommendation card with action buttons.
10. **Upcoming Dates**
    *   List of approaching birthdays, anniversaries, etc., with countdowns.
11. **Floating Action Button (FAB)**
    *   Persistent "+" button for logging quality time.

### 4. Features and Capabilities
*   **Connection Strength Visualization:** Transforms "days since contact" into a 0–99 composite score (recency, frequency, reciprocity) displayed via a focal hero gauge and per-row satellite gauges.
*   **Portfolio & Cadence Tracking:** 
    *   *Relationship Portfolio Donut:* Splits key people by type (partner, family, friend, colleague).
    *   *Cadence Heatmap:* Shows touchpoints per week inside expanded person rows.
    *   *Cadence-Share Donut:* Shows a person's share of the user's total 6-week outreach.
*   **AI Reminders & Dismissal:** Swipeable reminder cards for neglected connections and approaching dates. Supports swipe-to-dismiss, swipe-to-log, and long-press for an accessible options menu.
*   **Quality Time Logging:** FAB and inline shortcuts open a bottom sheet to log person, activity, duration, and optional reflection/mood.
*   **AI Suggestions:** Contextual activity recommendations from CIA that can be actioned ("do it") or dismissed ("skip").
*   **Motivation Tiering:** 
    *   *Low:* Caps people list at 2, hides suggestions/dates.
    *   *Medium:* Default density.
    *   *High:* Reveals per-person sparklines, full lists, and duration analytics.

### 7. Screen States
*   **Cold-Start / Day-1:** KPIs read `0` with `—` delta. No gauge/heatmap rendered. Prominent "add person" CTA. Ghosted dashed gauge arc for newly added people.
*   **Loading:** Depth-preserving skeletons that morph into drawn data. Gauge tracks visible with radial shimmer; text outlines visible.
*   **Error / Partial Failure:** Per-section inline errors (e.g., "Couldn't load your reminders — pull to refresh.") with visible retry buttons. Network banner appears if offline; cached data shown.
*   **Success / Completed:** Brief green glow on the FAB, buttons, or person row after logging time or marking a reminder done. "Skip" success dims card to 40% opacity.
*   **Disabled / Inactive:** Actions dimmed to 40% opacity (e.g., pull-to-refresh offline).

### 8. Interactions, Gestures, Motion Intents
*   **Gestures:**
    *   *Swipe Left* on Reminder: Dismiss.
    *   *Swipe Right* on Reminder: Mark as done (logs interaction).
    *   *Long-press* Person Row / Quality Time / Upcoming Date: Edit details (Accessible alternative to swiping).
    *   *Tap* Person Row: Expands inline to reveal heatmap/sparkline and retargets the 96px hero gauge to this person.
*   **Motion Intents:**
    *   *Draw-first Hierarchy:* Hero gauge fills (520ms) → KPI strip counts up (280ms) → Person rows stagger in.
    *   *Living Line Draw:* The communication trend chart draws itself left-to-right (1200ms), followed by the dashed CIA projection.
    *   *Expand/ Collapse:* Smooth height expansion (280ms) revealing nested charts.

### 9. Free vs. Premium Gating
*   *Contradiction Flagged:* The source document states in the audit integration that *"Q19 journal keeps basic writing/search free and gates AI/voice features"* and mandates explicit data privacy controls. However, the screen spec does not explicitly assign Free/Premium tiers to the CIA insights, connection strength scores, or automated cadence tracking. By inference, basic logging and people management are Free, while deep AI insights and automated tracking are Premium.
