# 8. Data & honesty states

- **All-Time Completions:**
  - **Real:** `24` + ChipProvenance `via Missions system`.

  - **Honest-null:** `--` (muted) + `Not enough data yet`.
- **Mission Duration:**
  - **Real:** `4 weeks` + ChipProvenance `Missions log`.

# 9. All states
- **Default:** User sees KPI summary, JourneySpine drawn, and cards settled.
- **Skeleton:** JourneySpine draws top-to-bottom (reduced motion bypasses stroke-draw). Cards show text shimmers and thumbnail placeholders. KPI shows shimmer blocks.
- **Empty:** First-use state. Filter chips hidden. Timeline omitted. Centered EmptyState component with Display type.
- **Error:** Network error ListRow banner at top. Defers to cached entries if available; otherwise renders the empty state with recovery options.
- **Success:** On filter application or successful forced refresh: XPToast appears briefly (`Journal refreshed`).
- **Disabled:** No internal buttons are disabled unless data is missing. If premium comparisons are tapped by a free user, a PaywallLock modal overlay appears.

# 10. Motion & interaction
- **Physical easing:** `cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)` used for all entry transitions.
- **Timeline Spine Draw:** On initial screen load, the JourneySpine uses SVG `stroke-dashoffset` to draw downwards over 1200ms. TimelineNodes scale from 0.5 to 1.0 and settle as the line intersects them. Cards simultaneously `translateY(8px)` and fade from 0 to 1.
- **Feedback:** Tapping a journal card triggers a `.98` press scale + glow brighten over `150ms`.
- **Haptics:** Success impact haptic when a filter is applied.
- **Reduced-motion path:** Timeline spine renders instantly. Node scaling is bypassed. Card stagger animations are set to `0ms` duration. 

# 11. Motivation-tier adaptation
- **Low Density (Minimalist):** Hide month-header stats. Hide the XP progress bars inside mission cards. Hide the photo stack counts. Clean focus on the CIA narrative text and mission duration.
- **Medium Density (Default):** Base layout as specified: narrative, core metrics (duration, XP bar), and up to 3 photo thumbnails.
- **High Density (Analyzer):** Adds a micro-donut chart to Month Section headers showing domain distribution for that month. Adds inline `KPIRow` to cards showing session counts or specific cross-domain deltas (e.g., "Runs: 47"). Adds PaywallLock CTA for advanced cross-domain comparisons.

# 12. Accessibility
- **AA+ Contrast:** Paper-100 `#FEFAF3` on `--bg-base` `#0A0A0F` passes easily. Low confidence text (Paper-50 at 64%) maintains minimum contrast on `--surface-2` cards.
- **Targets:** All filters, tap targets, and photo thumbnails meet or expand beyond the 44px minimum. 
- **Screen-reader labels:** The JourneySpine and TimelineNodes are explicitly marked `aria-hidden="true"` as they are decorative. Sub-filter chips read as "Filter by Fitness domain, active." Archive notes read explicitly as "Your note: [text]" to distinguish from CIA summaries.
