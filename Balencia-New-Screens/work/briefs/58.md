# FUNCTIONAL CONTENT BRIEF: Sleep Tracking

## 1. Purpose
Provides a comprehensive command center for sleep analysis, answering "how well am I sleeping and what can I do better?" Surfaces last night's data and long-term patterns to deliver AI-driven, cross-domain coaching.

## 2. Entry and Exit Paths
*   **Entry Points:**
    *   Explore grid card.
    *   Home Screen sleep action/insight card.
    *   CIA Chat deep-link ("let's look at your sleep").
    *   Wellbeing Dashboard.
*   **Exit Points:**
    *   Back button (stack pop to previous screen).
    *   CIA Chat (tab switch via tapping CIA insight cards/links).
    *   RPG Character profile (stack push via RPG badge).
    *   Connected Services (stack push via wearable connect prompt).
    *   Manual Sleep Log (bottom sheet modal via FAB).

## 3. Information Architecture
The screen uses a mixed-content scroll view with a sticky header and bottom tab bar.

1.  **Status Bar:** System-managed.
2.  **Domain Dashboard Header (Sticky):** Back button, "Sleep" title, sleep-indigo accent line, RPG skill badge.
3.  **CIA Coaching Note Card:** Contextual AI message (emotional anchor above the data).
4.  **Last Night Summary Card (Hero):** Sleep score GaugeRing, hours readout, quality stars, 48px recovery ring, sleep reserve ChargeMeter, and bedtime/wake times.
5.  **Sleep Stages Donut Card:** Light/Deep/REM breakdown (conditional, wearable-only).
6.  **Sleep Trend Card:** Duration Living line over 7/14/30 days, target line, and goal adherence MomentumBar.
7.  **Bedtime Consistency Card:** 2D ConsistencyCloud (mints new primitive), showing bedtime and wake time clusters.
8.  **30-Night Calendar Heatmap:** Duration adherence grid.
9.  **Quality Trend Card:** Sleep quality (1-5 scale) living line.
10. **Sleep Hygiene Tips Section:** Collapsible/expandable AI-personalized list.
11. **FAB (Floating Action Button):** "+ Log sleep".
12. **Tab Bar:** Primary app navigation (Today | CIA | Goals | Me).

## 4. Features and Capabilities
*   **Automated Wearable Sync:** Pulls sleep data, stages, and recovery metrics automatically from connected providers (WHOOP/Oura).
*   **Manual Sleep Logging:** Comprehensive bottom sheet to manually log date, bedtime, wake time (auto-calculates duration), quality rating, tags, and notes.
*   **AI-Driven Insights:** CIA analyzes patterns to generate specific, cross-domain coaching notes (e.g., matching late workouts to low HRV) and personalized hygiene tips.
*   **Trend Analysis:** Segmented timeline view (7/14/30 days) for sleep duration and quality, including data-derived target lines and AI projections.
*   **Consistency Tracking:** Visualizes bedtime/wake regularity using cluster tightness, surfacing weekend drift without shaming the user.
*   **Sleep Reserve Tracking:** Segmented gauge showing rolling sleep debt vs. the 7.5h need.
*   **Goal Adherence:** Continuous momentum bar showing progress toward the 7.5h goal.
*   **RPG Integration:** Quality sleep maintains streaks and earns XP, reflected in the header badge.

## 6. Data Fields and Metrics
*   **Sleep Score:** 0–100 composite (Source: derived from `sleep_logs` + `daily_health_metrics`).
*   **Hours Slept:** e.g., "7.2 hrs" (Source: `sleep_logs.duration_hours`).
*   **Recovery Score:** 0–100% with band status (<40% low, 40-70% building, >70% recovering) (Source: `daily_health_metrics.recovery_score`).
*   **Sleep Quality:** 1–5 scale (Source: `sleep_logs.quality`).
*   **Sleep Stages:** Light, Deep, REM times/percentages (Source: `daily_health_metrics` when `provider` present).
*   **Sleep Reserve/Debt:** Rolling 7-night deficit vs 7.5h target (Source: derived from `sleep_logs`).
*   **Goal Adherence:** % vs 7.5h target (Source: derived from `sleep_logs`).
*   **Bedtime/Wake Consistency:** 0–100% score (`100 - normalized(σ)`) with "tight/variable" word (Source: `sleep_logs.bedtime/wake_time`).
*   **RPG Skill Level:** e.g., "Lv.8" (Source: User RPG profile).

## 7. Screen States
*   **Cold-start / Day 1:** Hero ring shows faint empty track (no fake zero). Charts show faint grids with target lines and warm, aspirational copy ("Log a few more nights...").
*   **Loading:** Depth-preserving skeletons. Axis labels/grids visible, chart elements shimmer or draw in. No spinning indicators.
*   **Partial Sync:** Un-synced nights are rendered as ghosted cells or dashed gaps. Missing data is never interpolated as zero.
*   **Error:** Cached data shown with offline banner. "Retry sync" links. Save failures trigger an auto-dismissing error toast; form data is preserved.
*   **Manual Entry Mode:** Wearable-specific metrics (Sleep Stages Donut, Recovery Ring) are completely hidden, not zeroed out.
*   **Offline:** Cached data displayed. FAB is disabled (50% opacity) with an honest aria-label: "Offline — logging will sync when you reconnect."

## 8. Interactions, Gestures, Motion Intents
*   **Gestures:** Swipe right from edge (back), pull down (refresh), drag down (dismiss modal), tap (tooltips, expand/collapse, navigation).
*   **Motion Choreography:** 
    *   **On Mount:** Hero GaugeRing fills first (520ms), numbers count up, cards rise in a staggered sequence (80ms apart).
    *   **Scroll-into-view:** "Living Lines" draw themselves left-to-right (1200ms). ConsistencyCloud dots scale-in (30ms stagger), heatmap cells fade in row-by-row. Green milestone dots "pop" after lines draw.
    *   **Micro-interactions:** Segmented controls slide pill backgrounds, star ratings scale on tap, FAB hides on scroll-down and returns on scroll-up.
    *   **Accessibility:** Honors `prefers-reduced-motion` (instantly renders final states, no drawing animations).

## 9. Free vs Premium Gating
*   **Free Tier:** Manual sleep logging, basic last-night summary, basic trend chart.
*   **Plus Tier:** Unlocks CIA trend analysis and personalized sleep hygiene tips.
*   **Pro Tier:** Unlocks cross-domain correlations (e.g., matching sleep quality against workout intensity, stress, and nutrition data).

## 10. Cross-Domain Hooks (CIA Insights)
*   CIA actively correlates sleep metrics with other logged life areas to provide actionable coaching:
    *   **Fitness/Workouts:** Flags when late-day workouts correlate with lower HRV or delayed sleep onset, suggesting earlier cutoff times.
    *   **Nutrition:** Cross-references afternoon caffeine logs with sleep onset delays.
    *   **Stress/Mindfulness:** Notes when best sleep nights follow evening meditation sessions.
