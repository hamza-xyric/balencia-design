# 2. Purpose
A centralized, checklist-first hub answering: "what do I need to do, when is it due, and what should I not forget?" It bridges intention with execution by tracking health-related tasks and time-based reminders, surfacing CIA cross-domain intelligence, and adapting to the user's motivation tier.

# 5. Components

**NEW: `TaskRow`**
- **Rationale:** The brief demands a complex list unit (checkbox + domain tags + priority indicators + due times). The catalog's generic `ListRow` lacks the structural support for left-aligned interactive checkboxes, swipe-to-complete gestures, and priority warning glyphs required for a premium task manager.

**NEW: `ReminderRow`**
- **Rationale:** Requires right-aligned toggles, specific snooze/trigger metadata in Body-light, and a highly specific "paused/disabled" honest-null visual state that standard list components do not support.

# 8. Data & honesty states
*All metrics ship with 3 distinct states.*

**Metric 1: Today's Completion Ratio (`done ÷ total`)**
- **Real:** "2 of 4 done · 50%" [ChipProvenance: `live status`]
- **Low-confidence:** "2 of 4 done · estimated" [KPIs muted to 64%, label: `estimated · low confidence`] *(Triggered if local time diverges from server sync).*
- **Honest-null:** Hidden entirely [Replaced by `EmptyState` copy: "No tasks for today · check your upcoming list or create one."] (Suppressed on cold-start per brief).

**Metric 2: Active Reminders Roll-up (`N of M on`)**
- **Real:** "3 of 4 on" [ChipProvenance: `system flags`]
- **Low-confidence:** "3 of 4 on" [Value muted, label: `syncing...`] 
- **Honest-null:** "0 of 0 on" [Empty copy: "Set up a reminder for your health activities"]

*Correction Note:* The brief explicitly noted a high priority indicator contradiction (`#F44336` red vs `stalled-amber`). Per the Canon's 60/30/10 color rule and data-viz invariant (no red-vs-green moralizing), urgency is standardized to `--glow-you` (#FF5E00, 60% base) and pairs the warning with a mandatory `⚠` glyph so it remains status-paired, never purely decorative data ink.

# 9. All states
- **Default:** Clean `SolidCard` list with inline active/completed states.
- **Skeleton:** Depth-preserving skeletons. Pill-shaped shimmer for the `ProgressBar`, number-bar shimmers for KPIs, and full-row `--surface-3` shimmer blocks for lists.
- **Empty:** Clipboard icon (quiet, not cartoonish). Display-small copy: "No tasks yet." Body-light sub: "Create your first task or let CIA suggest one based on your goals."
- **Error:** `ErrorState` card: "Couldn't load your tasks." Plain language, no error codes. BtnSecondary: "Tap to retry". Falls back to last cached list if available.
- **Success (Completion):** `ProgressBar` sweeps to 100%, flips to `--glow-done` (Forest Green #34A853). Completed row text is muted/struck through.
- **Disabled (Offline):** `OfflineBanner` (glass-pill): "You're offline · your changes are queued and will sync when you're back online." Controls are dimmed; inline text reads "saving..." upon interaction.

# 10. Motion & interaction
- **Easing & Duration:** Physical easing only (`ease-flow` curves). UI feedback strictly 150-250ms. 
- **Swipe Gestures:** 
  - *Swipe right (>30%):* Complete task. Triggers 200ms green `--glow-done` background reveal.
  - *Swipe left:* Reveal edit/delete quick actions.
- **Long-press (>500ms):** Haptic tick (medium). Triggers drag-and-drop reorder mode within the section.
- **Completion Choreography:** Checkmark stroke draws in (150ms). Text mutes. Row slides down to the Completed section (250ms transform).
- **Glow behavior:** `CIAInsightCard` orb and glow "breathe" (4s ease-in-out) to signal active intelligence. High-priority `TaskRow` glow holds static to avoid anxiety-inducing pulsing.
- **Reduced motion (`prefers-reduced-motion`):** All sweeps and slides bypass transitions. Completion checkmarks fade in instantly (opacity only). Reorders snap to position.

# 11. Motivation-tier adaptation
- **Low motivation (Overwhelmed/Fatigued):** 
  - Past effort and progress stats (`ProgressBar`, KPIs) are hidden to prevent pressure. 
  - List is simplified to strictly 1-2 critical items. 
  - Upcoming sections are collapsed. 
  - CIA card hidden to reduce noise.
- **Medium motivation (Default):** Layout as specified in wireframe. Balanced execution and forward-planning.
- **High motivation (Engaged):** 
  - Expanded metadata visible directly on `TaskRow` (recurring icons, detailed schedules). 
  - Completed stats expand inline at the bottom of 'Today' to celebrate momentum.

# 12. Accessibility
- **Contrast:** Primary text (#FEFAF3) on `--surface-2` (#211008) exceeds AA+ (15:1). Tertiary text stops at paper-40% strictly for non-critical meta-data (maintains 4.5:1).
- **Targets:** All interactive elements (`TaskRow` checkboxes, `FABQuickLog`, swipe action receptors, `ReminderRow` toggles) map to a minimum 44x44px touch target grid, even if visually constrained to 24px.
- **Screen-reader labels:** 
  - Priority `⚠` glyph: `aria-label="High priority"`.
  - Checkbox: `aria-label="Mark task as complete"`.
  - Swipe actions exposed via `accessibilityCustomActions` (iOS) / `AccessibilityAction` (Android) to ensure edit/delete are reachable without gesture navigation.
