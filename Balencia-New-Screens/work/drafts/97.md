# 2. Purpose
Acts as the centralized, unified hub for members to manage, track, and execute structured commitments and plans generated by the CIA. It bridges whole-life areas—such as wellbeing, finance, and career—with accountability and honest progress tracking.

*Correction Note:* The legacy inventory states `/plans` is "not visible in primary nav." For the native mobile app redesign, this route is elevated and accessed via the "Goals" tab to fulfill the unified product vision ("Turn insights into plans...").

# 3. Entry & exit
*   **Entry Paths:**
    *   Global bottom navigation via the "Goals" tab.
    *   Deep-link from the "Today" Dashboard quick actions.
    *   Deep-link from the CIA chat when a new plan is generated.
    *   Deep-link from Accountability Contracts (`/contracts`).
*   **Exit Paths:**
    *   Tapping a `PlanListRow` to view its detailed execution tracker.
    *   Tapping the "Ask CIA" CTA to route to the AI coach.
    *   Standard back gesture/swipe to return to the previous screen.

# 8. Data & honesty states
Every metric in the Plans library renders through the 3-state honesty invariant. 

**Metric: Plan Progress**
1.  **Real:** `70%` (tabular-nums) + `via Google Calendar` provenance chip.
2.  **Low-confidence:** `~70%` (muted, 64% opacity) + label: `estimated · low confidence`.
3.  **Honest-null:** `Not enough data yet — 3 more days` (Body-light) inside the `ProgressBar` track or below it. No fabricated percentage is shown.

**Metric: Milestone Status**
1.  **Real:** `Next: 5k run` + `via Google Calendar`.
2.  **Low-confidence:** `Next: 5k run (est.)` + `estimated · low confidence`.
3.  **Honest-null:** `Schedule your next task` + `BtnGhost: Add date`.

# 9. All states
*   **Default:** `Active` tab selected; CIA Hero card visible; `PlanRowSummary` cards rendered with real or honest-null data.
*   **Skeleton:** Shimmer blocks (`--surface-3` base, 1.2s sweep) replacing the `SolidCards`. The axis of the `ProgressBar` shows a ghost line.
*   **Empty (First-use):** `EmptyState` component. "No *plans* yet. Ask your CIA to generate a plan." with a `BtnPrimary: Open CIA`.
*   **Error:** `ErrorState` component. "We couldn't sync your *plans*. Check your connection and try again." with `BtnSecondary: Retry`.
*   **Success:** Triggered when swiping right to complete a milestone. `CelebrationOverlay` (continuous-stroke line motif draws, respects reduced-motion). Progress bar hits 100% and flips green (`--glow-done`).
*   **Disabled/Locked:** If the free-tier user has 3 active plans, attempting to create a 4th triggers an inline `PaywallLock` tile at the bottom of the list: "Maximum active *plans* reached." + `BtnPrimary: Unlock with premium`.

# 10. Motion & interaction
*   **Easing:** Physical easing (`cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)`) for all transitions. Never linear.
*   **Feedback (150-250ms):** Tapping a `PlanRowSummary` scales the card to .98 opacity and brightens the background to `--surface-3`.
*   **Glow behavior:** The `--glow-cia` on the Hero Card utilizes the 4s "breathe" (opacity 0.7 to 1.0) to simulate CIA presence. 
*   **Haptics:** Light impact haptic on Segmented Tab change. Medium impact haptic when a milestone is swiped-to-complete.
*   **Gestures:** 
    *   *Swipe Left:* Reveals Archive/Delete glass-pill actions.
    *   *Swipe Right:* Marks milestone complete (triggers Success state).
    *   *Pull to Refresh:* Force syncs data.
*   **Reduced-motion path:** `CelebrationOverlay` swaps to a simple 200ms fade-in/scale. CIA hero breathe is disabled; glow remains static. Swipe actions require explicit taps instead of momentum snaps.

# 11. Motivation-tier adaptation
*   **Low (Bare Minimum):** The `CIAInsightCard` is collapsed into a 1-line ghost CTA. Only "Active" plans are shown by default; paused/completed are hidden behind the filter tabs to reduce cognitive load.
*   **Medium (Default):** Hero card is visible. Filters show standard counts (e.g., Active [2]). Standard `PlanRowSummary` layout with text milestones.
*   **High (Execution Mode):** Hero card minimized. `PlanRowSummary` expands to show embedded `GlassStatCard` proof metrics (e.g., the Marathon plan shows a mini `TrendChart` of running pace). Milestone dates include time-of-day countdowns (e.g., "in 4 hours").

# 12. Accessibility
*   **Contrast:** Paper-100 (#FEFAF3) on `--surface-2` (#211008) exceeds 15:1 (AAA). Caption text on surfaces exceeds AA. 
*   **Targets:** All SegmentedTabs, glyphs in the TopBar, and row chevrons maintain a minimum 44x44px touch target.
*   **Screen-reader labels:** The `+` glyph in the TopBar is labeled "Generate new plan with CIA". The FAB is labeled "Quick log". The progress bar has an ARIA label: "Marathon training, 70 percent complete. Next task, 5k run."
