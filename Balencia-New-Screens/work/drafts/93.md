# 2. Purpose
To visualize a user's longitudinal mood data and provide cross-pillar intelligence regarding their emotional patterns. The screen connects raw user check-ins with health metrics (like sleep) and CIA pattern recognition, maintaining strict honesty regarding missing data or low-confidence AI correlations.

# 3. Entry & exit
**Entry paths:**
* Primary: Wellbeing Hub (`/wellbeing`) -> "Mood" module.
* Secondary: Today Dashboard (`/dashboard`) -> Quick Action "Track Mood" -> "View Trends".
* Tertiary: CIA Coach conversational deep-link ("View your mood trends").

**Exit paths:**
* Back chevron: Returns to Wellbeing Hub.
* Bottom nav: Switches app domains.
* `BtnPrimary` (Log mood): Routes to Mood Logging (`/wellbeing/emotional-checkin`).
* `BtnCoach` (Discuss patterns): Routes to CIA Coach (`/ai-coach`).

# 4. Layout anatomy
**Regions top-to-bottom:**
1. **TopBar:** Transparent over atmosphere. Back chevron, H1 "Mood", and a quick-log (+) glyph.
2. **Time selector:** `SegmentedTabs` for 7 Days, 30 Days, 90 Days, 1 Year.
3. **Primary visualization:** `SolidCard` housing the `TrendChart` for the selected time window.
4. **Intelligence layer:** `CIAInsightCard` displaying pattern analysis and cross-pillar correlations.
5. **Historical reflections:** Horizontal scroller (`FeedPostCard` variant) for past check-ins.
6. **Global nav:** `GlassNavBar`.

# 5. Components
* **TopBar:** Transparent header.
* **SegmentedTabs:** Pill track for time-range switching.
* **SolidCard:** Container for chart (preserves legibility).
* **TrendChart:** Line chart for 7-day window. Includes tooltip on drag.
* **GlassStatCard (`metric` variant):** Houses the overall time-range average score with provenance.
* **CIAInsightCard:** Procedural narrative text + insight actions.
* **BtnCoach:** Purple-tinted CTA inside the insight card.
* **FeedPostCard:** Used horizontally for past check-ins/reflections.
* **GlassNavBar:** Floating pill bottom navigation.
* **NEW: ReflectionScrubCard:** A horizontally scrollable `SolidCard` derivative optimized for snapping through qualitative text notes tied to chart data points. *Rationale:* Standard list rows are too tall for horizontal carousels; we need a wide, tile-like text surface that aligns with the chart's timeline scrubbing.

# 6. Visual treatment
* **Glass tiers:** The `CIAInsightCard` and `GlassNavBar` use `.glass-card` / `.glass-pill` to float above the atmosphere. The chart and history use `SolidCard` surfaces for dense data legibility.
* **Semantic glows:** 
  * `--glow-you` (#FF5E00): Applied to the `GlassStatCard` and chart line, anchoring the visualization to the user's direct input.
  * `--glow-cia` (#7F24FF): Applied to the `CIAInsightCard`, signifying projected patterns and AI-derived cross-pillar links.
* **Atmosphere:** Warm dark base `--bg-base` (#0A0A0F) with a soft warm radial glow at top-center. A subtle purple pool anchors the bottom right where the CIA insight card sits.
* **Hero type moment:** "Mood" in H1 (34px NM Medium). The quiet uppercase overline "Overall average" is paired with a large tabular-num KPI.

# 8. Data & honesty states
Every metric honors the 3-state honesty invariant.
* **Metric 1: Overall average (7 days)**
  * **Real:** `7.2` + `via emotional check-in` chip. 
  * **Low-confidence:** `~7` (muted 64%) + `estimated · low confidence` caption. (Triggered if user skipped explicit logging and CIA guessed based on journal sentiment).
  * **Honest-null:** `--` + `Not enough data yet — 3 more days`.
* **Metric 2: Sleep correlation**
  * **Real:** `+ 82% correlation to sleep` + `via WHOOP` chip.
  * **Low-confidence:** `Potential link to sleep` + `estimated · low confidence`.
  * **Honest-null:** `Wear your tracker to unlock sleep links`.

# 9. All states
* **Default:** 7-day chart visible, CIA card populated, history loaded.
* **Skeleton:** Shimmer blocks over chart axes. Pulsing purple orb in CIA card. `--surface-3` base sweeping to `--surface-2`.
* **Empty (Zero Data):** Chart is replaced by `HonestNullState` inside the `SolidCard` ("Not enough data yet"). CIA Card reads: "Log your mood to unlock CIA insights." with a `BtnPrimary` "Log mood".
* **Error:** `ErrorState` in chart frame: "Sync failed. We couldn't load your history." + `BtnSecondary` "Retry".
* **Success:** Upon returning from a check-in, the newest chart node pulses green once, and the line sweeps to include it.
* **Disabled:** Premium tiers (90 Days / 1 Year) in `SegmentedTabs` are visually active but tapping triggers `PaywallLock` (Blurred chart preview + "Unlock with premium"). 

# 10. Motion & interaction
* **Easing:** Physical easing (`cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)`) for all chart rescales.
* **Feedback:** 150ms scale `.98` on button presses. 
* **Glow behavior:** `CIAInsightCard` features a subtle 4s "breathe" on its purple inner-glow. `--glow-you` brightens momentarily when the user drags the chart tooltip.
* **Haptics:** Light selection haptic when dragging the chart tooltip across days. Success haptic on new check-in registration.
* **Reduced-motion path:** Chart line draws instantly (no sweep). CIA glow becomes static. Tooltip crossfades instead of sliding.

# 11. Motivation-tier adaptation
* **Low density:** History scroller hides completely. Only the 7-day chart and a single, concise CIA insight sentence are shown to reduce cognitive load.
* **Medium density (Default):** Layout exactly as specified in Section 4.
* **High density:** History scroller expands into a full list view merging notes, sleep scores, and mood metrics side-by-side per day. Time selector defaults to 30 Days.

# 12. Accessibility
* **Contrast:** AA+ verified. Paper-100 (#FEFAF3) on `--surface-2` (#211008) for primary text. Paper-50 at 64% for axis labels. 
* **Targets:** TopBar quick-log (+), SegmentedTabs, and chart tooltip drag area all exceed the 44px minimum touch target.
* **Screen-reader:** Chart tooltip is rendered as an `accessibilityValue` (e.g., "Mood 8 out of 10, Tuesday"). The horizontal history scroller uses `accessibilityScroll`. Glyph-only buttons possess `accessibilityLabel` (e.g., "Go back to wellbeing hub").
