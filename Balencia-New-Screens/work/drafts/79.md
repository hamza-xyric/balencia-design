### 2. Purpose
Converts a completed CIA voice coaching session into durable action items and correlated insights. Provides a calm, honest debrief rather than a dense transcript archive. Empowers the user to anchor actionable follow-ups directly into their life domains. 

### 3. Entry & exit
- **Entry:** Stack push after Voice Mode [11] ends; accessed via a ListRow from Voice Call History [51].
- **Primary exit:** Down-shifted secondary affordance: Schedule follow-up (opens Sheet from Voice Call History [51] architecture).
- **Secondary exits:** Back chevron (returns to history); Action item tap (routes to Reminders & Tasks [61] or Mission Detail [14]); Transcript highlight tap (opens expanded transcript view).

### 4. Layout anatomy
**Regions (top-to-bottom):**
1. **TopBar:** Sticky, transparent over atmosphere. Back chevron, H1 title, privacy status.
2. **Hero Region:** Call context, emotional baseline, and the single focal visualization (ReputationDial mapping tone).
3. **Metrics Region:** KPIRow displaying duration, action items, and within-call emotion shift.
4. **Call Composition:** SolidCard containing segmented ProgressBar mapping topic allocation.
5. **Call Arc Region:** Horizontal IntelligenceTimeline mapping key decisions.
6. **Action Items:** Tappable ListRows pushing to tasks.
7. **Transcript Region:** CIAChatBubbles highlighting verbatim text.
8. **Bottom Action:** Fixed contextual BtnSecondary for scheduling.

### 8. Data & honesty states
Every metric renders through the 3-state honesty invariant:
- **Call Duration**
  - *Real:* `12m 04s` (Chip: `auto-logged`)
  - *Low-confidence:* N/A (Duration is deterministic)
  - *Honest-null:* N/A
- **Emotion Shift (+15%)**
  - *Real:* `▲ 15%` (Chip: `transcript parsed`)
  - *Low-confidence:* `~▲ 15%` (Label: `estimated · low confidence`)
  - *Honest-null:* `Tone analysis preparing` (Ghosted arc inside SolidCard)
- **Topic Allocation (12m)**
  - *Real:* `Recovery 5m, Fueling 4m, Pace 3m` (Chip: `mapped`)
  - *Low-confidence:* Visual blocks at 64% opacity (Label: `estimated · low confidence`)
  - *Honest-null:* `topic breakdown preparing`
- **Key Moments (Timeline)**
  - *Real:* 4 distinct node dots (`Pacing rule saved`, etc.)
  - *Honest-null:* `Key moments appear when the transcript finishes` (Track remains visible without nodes)
- **Action Items**
  - *Real:* `Check resting HRV` (Checked state)
  - *Honest-null:* `CIA didn't identify action items from this call — that's okay. You can create one manually in Reminders`

### 9. All states
- **Default:** Fully rendered summary, metrics parsed, action items actionable.
- **Skeleton (Mid-generation):** Depth-preserving. Hero shows ghosted dial rings. Topic bars use `SkeletonState` (1.2s sweep). KPIs show `--surface-3` blocks. Timeline shows empty baseline path.
- **Partial:** Sentiment, topics, and duration render normally. Timeline and transcript sections show ghosting with `Transcript is still processing` copy.
- **Error:** `CIA could not summarize this call — try again or review the transcript manually`. Charts default to ghost state; hero replaced with a BtnSecondary `Retry`.
- **Offline:** Cached summary renders normally. OfflineBanner appears: `offline — showing your last sync`. Schedule CTA disabled at 50% opacity with overlay: `connect to internet to schedule`.
- **Success:** Toast drops on action item completion: `+40 XP · Tasks` (if XP is enabled).

### 10. Motion & interaction
- **Motion intent (Draw-first):** Hero dial sweeps clockwise. Topic arcs scale X from left. KPIs count up using `tabular-nums`. Timeline path draws left-to-right. Action and transcript rows stagger in last with a 24ms offset.
- **Easing:** Physical ease-out for inbound elements. 150-250ms feedback for all taps.
- **Tap (Sentiment Gauge):** Expands a one-line explainer (`warm tone detected from vocal pacing`). Never frames low values as failure.
- **Tap (Topic Slice):** Deep-links to anchored transcript section.
- **Tap (Private Pill):** Opens `NEW: GlyphTooltip` overlaying the screen with a subtle scrim.
- **Reduced-motion path:** Draw-first animations swap to simple opacity fades. Dial sets to final state instantly.
