# Functional Content Brief: Progress Photos & Body Composition

## 1. Purpose
A private, encrypted command center tracking body transformation over time via weight trends, measurements, and photos. It connects physical changes to lifestyle habits through contextual AI insights, revealing the "why" behind the numbers.

## 2. Entry and Exit Paths
*   **Entry Paths:** Fitness Dashboard [26] ("progress" link), CIA Chat [09] (deep-link), Home Screen [12] (action card), or Explore Section [18] (grid card).
*   **Primary Exits:** Photo Comparison Mode (modal overlay), Image Viewer [67], CIA Chat [09] (tab switch), RPG Character [19], Connected Services [22], Create/Edit Goal [15].

## 3. Information Architecture (Top-to-Bottom)
*   **Status Bar:** System-managed.
*   **Domain Dashboard Header (Fixed):** Screen title, back navigation, RPG level badge.
*   **CIA Coaching Note:** Contextual AI insight connecting body changes to cross-domain habits.
*   **Weight Trend Chart Card:** Line chart plotting weight over time against a goal target, with range selectors.
*   **Current Stats Row:** Three KPI tiles: Weight, BMI, and Body Fat %.
*   **Measurements Card:** List of circumference metrics (chest, waist, hips, arms, thighs) with change indicators.
*   **Photo Timeline Card:** Vertical progress path of photo checkpoints, AI analysis badge, and a metric sparkline linking weight to photos.
*   **Privacy Notice:** Encryption reassurance cue.
*   **Global Navigation & FAB:** Floating "Add progress" button above primary Tab Bar.

## 4. Features and Capabilities
*   **Weight Tracking:** Visualize weight trends over 1M, 3M, 6M, 1Y, or All timeframes.
*   **Goal Targeting:** View target weight overlaid on the weight trend chart.
*   **Body Composition Snapshot:** Track core metrics (Weight, BMI, Body Fat %) with context-aware trend deltas.
*   **Measurement Tracking:** Log and track 5 distinct body circumference measurements.
*   **Progress Photo Capture:** Take front, side, and back photos with camera pose guides.
*   **Photo Timeline:** View a vertical chronological timeline of photo checkpoints. 
*   **Side-by-Side Comparison:** Drag a slider to compare two photos from different dates across different view angles.
*   **AI Body Composition Analysis:** Automated estimation of body fat percentage and muscle gain from uploaded photos.
*   **Encrypted Storage:** Client-side encryption ensuring photos remain strictly private to the user.
*   **Inline Data Visualization:** Expand measurement rows to view historical micro-trends.

## 6. Data Fields and Metrics
*   **Weight (kg/lbs):** `GET /api/progress` (record_type='weight').
*   **Target Weight:** User-defined goal via Create/Edit Goal.
*   **BMI:** Calculated from weight + profile height.
*   **Body Fat %:** `GET /api/onboarding/body-images` (analysis_result) or wearable integration.
*   **Measurements (Chest, Waist, Hips, Arms, Thighs):** `GET /api/progress` (record_type='measurement').
*   **Progress Photos (Front, Side, Back):** `GET /api/progress/photos` & `GET /api/onboarding/body-images`.
*   **Encryption Status:** `is_encrypted` flag in body_images table.
*   **AI Analysis Status:** `analysis_status` (pending/analyzing/completed/failed) in body_images table.

## 7. Screen States
*   **Cold-start / Day-1:** Invitations to log first data. Chart axes visible with empty containers.
*   **Empty / Partial:** Ghosted or dashed tracks for un-logged metrics (no-data ≠ zero). 
*   **Loading:** Depth-preserving skeletons that morph into data.
*   **Error:** Component-specific failure messages with cached data retained.
*   **Offline:** Cached data displayed with a sync banner; new data queued locally.
*   **AI Analyzing:** Pulsing dot with "Analyzing your photos..." text.
*   **Success:** "Photo saved and encrypted.", "Weight logged. Your trend will update shortly.", "Measurement saved."
*   **Disabled:** Compare function disabled until ≥2 photos exist.

## 8. Interactions, Gestures, and Motion Intents
*   **Chart Scrubbing:** Touch-and-hold on chart displays a vertical crosshair tooltip with exact weight and date.
*   **Row Expansion:** Tap measurement rows to expand height and draw a 7-point sparkline inline.
*   **Comparison Slider:** Drag a circular handle horizontally over side-by-side photos to reveal/hide "before" and "after" states.
*   **Photo Management:** Long-press a photo to reveal a delete floating pill.
*   **FAB Auto-Hide:** The "+ Add progress" button fades out on scroll-down and returns on scroll-up/stop.
*   **Motion Intent:** "Draw-first" choreography. Data curves and the photo timeline path physically draw themselves left-to-right or top-to-bottom on entrance, emphasizing progress as a continuous journey. Past checkpoints permanently settle. 
*   **Motivation Adaptation:** 
    *   *Low Motivation:* UI simplifies (hides BMI/Body Fat, limits chart range to 1M, hides AI badge), FAB changes to "Quick log", CIA tone shifts to pure encouragement ("You showed up. That's what matters.").
    *   *High Motivation:* Surfaces lean mass estimates, all-time min/max ranges, phase grouping labels, and highly specific CIA statistical correlations.

## 9. Free vs Premium Gating
*   **Free Tier:** Photo capture, weight logging, and manual measurements.
*   **Plus Tier:** Unlocks CIA trend analysis (cross-domain lifestyle connections).
*   **Pro Tier:** Unlocks AI body composition analysis (automated BF% and muscle estimation from photos).

## 10. Cross-Domain Hooks
*   **CIA Coaching Notes:** Automatically correlates physical data changes with sleep, nutrition, stress, and workout routines (e.g., linking weight drop to sleep improvement).
*   **RPG System:** Header integrates with the app's overarching gamification via the "Lv.12" badge.
*   **Wearables:** Prompts user to connect external devices (Connected Services [22]) to auto-populate body composition metrics.
