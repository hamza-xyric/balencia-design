# FUNCTIONAL CONTENT BRIEF: Streak Details

## 1. Purpose
The motivational engine of Balencia’s gamification system, making daily consistency visible, rewarding, and strategic. It answers "how consistent have I been, and what do I get for staying consistent?" by visualizing streaks, amplifying XP earnings, and enabling strategic rest planning. 

## 2. Entry and Exit Paths
**Entry Paths (Stack Push):**
*   **RPG Character [19]:** Tapping "day streak" in the stats summary.
*   **Home [12]:** Tapping the streak widget or action card.
*   **Habits [38]:** Tapping the streak flame indicator.
*   **Celebration Overlay [42]:** Tapping "view details" on a milestone achievement.
*   **CIA Chat [09]:** Tapping a deep-link when CIA mentions streak status.

**Exit Paths:**
*   **Back/Source Screen (Stack Pop):** Via top-left back chevron or screen edge swipe. Preserves source tab context.
*   **Leaderboard [39] (Stack Push):** Via "see full leaderboard" link.
*   **Habits [38] (Stack Push):** Via "build your streak" CTA in empty states.
*   **CIA Chat [09] (Tab Switch):** Via compact CIA coaching note.

## 3. Information Architecture
**Layout:** Vertical ScrollView, mixed content modules.
**Hierarchy (Top-to-Bottom):**
1.  **Screen Header:** 44pt height. Back chevron + centered title.
2.  **Current Streak Hero:** Emotional anchor. Flame, streak count, comparison to longest streak.
3.  **Streak Calendar:** Monthly grid visualizing active, frozen, and missed days.
4.  **XP Multiplier Dial:** Current streak-driven XP bonus and path to next tier.
5.  **Recovery Multiplier Dial:** Rested XP bonus status and mechanics.
6.  **Streak Freeze Module:** Available freeze count, rules, and primary "use freeze" CTA.
7.  **Streak Milestones (TimelineAgenda):** Progression ladder of 7 tiers showing earned and locked rewards.
8.  **Streak Leaderboard (PodiumRank):** Top 3 streakers and user's relative rank.
9.  **Streak History:** Record of past streaks with break reasons.

## 4. Features and Capabilities
*   **Streak Visualization:** View current consecutive active days against personal historical bests.
*   **Calendar Navigation:** Browse forward and backward through monthly calendars to view past consistency.
*   **Day Tooltips:** Tap any calendar day to view a brief detail (e.g., "completed 6 of 8 habits", "freeze used").
*   **Multiplier Tracking:** View active XP multipliers (1.0x to 2.0x cap) generated by consecutive daily activity.
*   **Rest Optimization:** Read active/inactive status of the 1.3x Recovery Multiplier earned by taking deliberate rest days.
*   **Streak Protection:** Apply a "Streak Freeze" to retroactively or proactively protect a streak on a missed/rest day.
*   **Reward Pathing:** Visualize progress toward XP milestone tiers (7, 14, 30, 60, 90, 180, 365 days).
*   **Social Comparison:** View rank against friends and globally on the streak leaderboard.
*   **Pattern Recognition:** Review historical break reasons to identify consistency patterns.

## 6. Data Fields and Metrics
*   **Current Streak Count** (e.g., 42) — *Source: `GET /api/streaks/status` (`current_streak`)*
*   **Longest Streak Count** (e.g., 67) — *Source: `GET /api/streaks/status` (`longest_streak`)*
*   **Calendar Day Status** (active, freeze, missed, future) — *Source: `GET /api/streaks/calendar`*
*   **XP Multiplier Level** (1.0x, 1.5x, 2.0x) — *Source: `user_xp_transactions` (derived from `current_streak`)*
*   **Recovery Multiplier Status** (Active Boolean, 1.3x) — *Source: `GET /api/streaks/status` (`recovery_multiplier_active`, `next_active_bonus`)*
*   **Available Freezes Count** (0-3) — *Source: `GET /api/streaks/status` (`available_freezes`)*
*   **Milestone Progress** (Earned vs. Locked, XP values) — *Source: `GET /api/streaks/rewards`*
*   **Leaderboard Rank & Names** — *Source: `GET /api/streaks/leaderboard`*
*   **Streak History Logs** (Length, Date Range, Break Reason) — *Source: `GET /api/streaks/history`*

## 7. Screen States
*   **Active/Established:** UI displays standard metrics; user has an active streak > 1 day.
*   **Day 1 / Empty (New User):** Hero shows "0", milestones locked, calendar blank. Highly aspirational framing.
*   **Broken Streak (Restart-Kindly):** Streak resets to 0. *No loss-aversion alarms, no red UI.* Earned milestones remain locked in/earned. CIA compact coaching note appears above the hero.
*   **Loading:** Skeleton elements that morph into drawn data. Copy: "CIA is reading your streak — one moment."
*   **Partial Sync:** Calendar data ghosted; cached data shown elsewhere. Copy: "syncing your calendar… — showing cached data"
*   **Error / Offline:** Sections display independently with "could not load [section] — retry". Offline displays cached data and disables freeze CTA.
*   **Confirmation Sheet State:** Modal bottom sheet appears when "use freeze" is tapped.
*   **Motivation Variants (Dynamic):**
    *   *Low Motivation:* Hides Leaderboard, Multipliers, and reduces history/milestones to avoid overwhelming or shaming the user.
    *   *High Motivation:* Displays secondary stats (avg daily XP, 14-day XP sparklines, projected milestone dates).

## 8. Interactions, Gestures, and Motion
*   **Gestures:** Pull-to-refresh (re-fetches all data), Tap (calendar days, CTAs, rows), Vertical scroll, Drag down (dismiss bottom sheet).
*   **Motion Chorography (Draw-First):**
    *   Hero card fades in; `GaugeRing` fills via `ring-animate` while the count ticks up.
    *   Content below fades in sequence (staggered).
    *   Multiplier `ArcGauges` sweep from base to current value.
    *   Milestone `TimelineAgenda` path draws itself top-to-bottom via `stroke-dashoffset`.
    *   Calendar cells stagger appearance left-to-right, top-to-bottom.
*   **Reduced Motion (`prefers-reduced-motion`):** All UI instantly renders at final static state without sweeping or pulsing. Essential data is fully preserved.

## 9. Free vs Premium Gating
*   **Free Tier:** Access to current streak visualization, the monthly calendar, and standard history.
*   **Plus (Premium) Tier:** Unlocks the XP Multiplier system, Recovery Multiplier mechanics, Streak Freeze management (earn/use), and the Streak Leaderboard.

## 10. Cross-Domain Hooks & CIA (Coach Persona) Insights
*   **CIA Coaching Integration:** If a user breaks a streak, the UI does not trigger a shaming notification. Instead, a compact CIA note is injected into the UI: "streaks break. what matters is starting again." Tapping this deep-links to CIA Chat for a supportive conversation.
*   **Habits Domain Sync:** The calendar intensity on this screen is directly fueled by daily habit completion loads from the Habits [38] domain.
*   **RPG/XP System Sync:** Streak multipliers directly cap and scale the global 2.0x XP cap, routing data to the RPG Character [19] profile and Celebration Overlays [42].
