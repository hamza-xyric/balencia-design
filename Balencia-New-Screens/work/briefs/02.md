# Functional Content Brief: Motion Carousel

## 1. Purpose
A 5-10 second cinematic, pre-authentication onboarding hook that uses motion graphics to pitch the app’s core value propositions. It establishes an emotional arc (curiosity → warmth → intelligence → aspiration) to drive the user toward account creation.

## 2. Entry and Exit Paths
*   **Entry:** Auto-advances from the Splash screen (`[01]`) via a 280ms crossfade. 
*   **Primary Exit:** Navigates to the Welcome / Sign Up screen (`[03]`) via a standard stack push when the user taps "Get started" on the final panel or taps "Skip."
*   **Secondary Exits:** None.

## 3. Information Architecture
The layout is a horizontal pager (swipeable or auto-advancing). The top-to-bottom structural hierarchy is persistent across all 4 panels:
1.  **System Status Bar:** Light/transparent style.
2.  **Skip Control (Top-Right):** Text button to bypass the carousel.
3.  **Motion Graphic Area (Hero):** The dominant visual centerpiece occupying ~60% of the viewport. Renders directly on the background (no container card).
4.  **Text Area:** Center-aligned Headline (with one distinct accent word) + supporting Subtext.
5.  **Pagination Indicator:** Dots indicating the current slide out of 4.
6.  **Primary CTA:** A full-width pill button (labeled "next" for panels 1-3, "get started" for panel 4).
7.  **System Home Indicator.**

## 4. Features & Capabilities
*   **4-Panel Value Communication:** Cinematic storytelling conveying distinct value propositions:
    *   *Panel 1 (Curiosity):* Unified life tracking (9 domains connecting into one system).
    *   *Panel 2 (Warmth):* Introduction of CIA (the coach persona) as a responsive, non-robotic presence.
    *   *Panel 3 (Intelligence):* Cross-domain correlation (how one life area directly impacts another).
    *   *Panel 4 (Aspiration):* Gamification (XP, leveling up, and quests).
*   **Auto-Advance Mechanism:** Automatically progresses to the next panel after 5 seconds of inactivity. Stops on the final panel.
*   **Bypass Capability:** Users can instantly skip to the authentication flow.
*   **Responsive Progression:** Users can navigate forward via swipe gestures or button taps.
*   **Accessibility Fallbacks:** Keyboard navigation (arrows/enter), long-press context menus for motor-impaired users, and screen-reader live-regions.

## 6. Data Fields & Metrics
This is a pre-auth screen; all data is static or illustrative rather than user-specific.
*   **Animation Assets:** Local Lottie/Rive files (Source: Pre-bundled in app binary).
*   **Fallback Keyframes:** Static PNG images (Source: Pre-bundled in app binary).
*   **XP Counter (Panel 4):** Displays an illustrative `142 / 500 XP` using tabular numerals (Source: Hardcoded static asset).
*   **Pagination Index:** Current slide state out of 4 (Source: Derived from carousel component state).

## 7. Screen States
*   **Default (Active):** Auto-advancing or idle navigation through the 4 panels.
*   **Empty (Established User):** Valid sessions skip this screen entirely (Splash → Home).
*   **Loading (Asset Download):** Displays a depth-preserving skeleton (pulsing shapes) for the motion graphic and text. The primary CTA is visibly disabled.
*   **Cold-Start / Fallback:** If the animation bundle fails to load, a warm-toned static keyframe PNG replaces the animation. Text and controls remain fully functional.
*   **Partial Failure (Stutter):** If an animation freezes, the last rendered frame persists. The screen does not lock.
*   **Error (Navigation Timeout):** If the stack push to `[03]` fails, a brief error toast appears. The CTA remains visible and tappable.
*   **Offline:** CTA dims and displays a network icon. Navigation is deferred until connection returns.
*   **Reduced Motion (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`):** All animations instantly snap to their final resting frame. Text enters immediately without delays or translating. Pagination updates instantly.

## 8. Interactions, Gestures & Motion Intents
*   **Swipe Left / Right:** Snaps to the adjacent panel. Boundary limits apply (cannot swipe left past Panel 1, or right past Panel 4).
*   **Tap (Skip / CTA):** Triggers forward navigation or panel advancement.
*   **Long-press (Fallback):** Opens a context menu with "Next" and "Skip" options.
*   **Auto-advance Interruption:** Any user touch cancels auto-advance for the remainder of the session.
*   **Motion Intents:**
    *   *P1:* Magnetic convergence and continuous-stroke line drawing.
    *   *P2:* Organic emergence, settling, and subtle breathing/pulsing loops.
    *   *P3:* Pulsing correlation lines and sequential fade-ins for insight cards.
    *   *P4:* Synchronized count-up numbers and progress ring fills.
    *   *Pagination:* Active dot morphs geometrically from a circle to an elongated pill.
    *   *Text Choreography:* Headlines and subtexts fade in and translate upwards upon panel snap.

## 9. Free vs. Premium Gating
Not applicable. This screen exists entirely outside the authentication and paywall flow.

## 10. Cross-Domain Hooks & AI Insights
*   **Unified Life System (P1):** Foreshadows the app's core architecture by visually representing 9 separate domains (e.g., fitness, finance, sleep, stress) magnetically pulling together into a single ecosystem.
*   **CIA Coach Persona (P2):** Introduces CIA not as a static chatbot, but as a responsive, omnipresent entity.
*   **Cross-Domain Correlation (P3):** The "intelligence" panel explicitly hooks into the app's analytics engine by demonstrating how inputs in one area dictate outputs in another (e.g., the direct correlation between low sleep metrics and increased financial spending). 
*   **Gamified Progression (P4):** Hooks into the user's future XP economy, leveling system, and domain-tagged weekly quests.
