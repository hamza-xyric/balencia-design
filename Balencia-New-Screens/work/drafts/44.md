# 8. Data & honesty states
Every metric ships 3 states. No fabricated numbers.
- **Metric: Water Consumed / Target**
  - **Real:** 5 / 8 glasses · 1250 ml (Provenance: `via Apple Health`)
  - **Low-confidence:** 4 / 8 glasses · 1000 ml (`estimated · low confidence` label)
  - **Honest-null:** 0 / 8 · building capacity (`Not enough data yet`)
- **Metric: Weekly History (TrendChart)**
  - **Real:** 7-day bars rendered with green dots for target hits (Provenance: `synced 2h ago`)
  - **Low-confidence:** Missing days rendered as dashed outline bars (`partial sync`)
  - **Honest-null:** Empty chart frame (`Log water to see your week`)
- **Metric: Stats (Streak/Avg/Best)**
  - **Real:** 12 day streak · 6.5 avg · 9 best (Provenance: `all-time stats`)
  - **Low-confidence:** 12 day streak · ~6 avg (`estimated`)
  - **Honest-null:** — · — · — (`Your hydration streak starts today.`)

# 9. All states
- **Default:** Real data populated, ring sweeps to current progress.
- **Skeleton:** Ring track pulses (opacity 4%→8%). Bar charts show axis + ghost line. Stat numbers show `--surface-3` shimmer blocks.
- **Empty (Cold Start):** Ring ghosted. Log shows `Your drinks will appear here`. Chart shows empty grid. Optional CIA note: `Start hydrating`.
- **Error:** Cached data loaded. `OfflineBanner` reads: `Could not load latest data. Showing cached from 2h ago.` BtnGhost `retry` attached to failed charts.
- **Success:** Ring crossfades to green (`--glow-done`). Pulsing green glow loop. Text changes to: `Goal achieved. +25 XP at 3:45 PM`. 
- **Disabled:** Quick-add buttons dim to 40% opacity momentarily during the 2s celebration animation to prevent double-logging.

# 10. Motion & interaction
- **Physical easing:** Standard `cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)` for all entrance and feedback curves. 
- **Feedback timing:** 150-250ms scale/sweep on button press and ring updates.
- **Entrance draw:** Ring fills first → MomentumBar fills → Quick-adds settle → Log entries rise L→R → Bar chart bars grow L→R.
- **Glow behavior:** Ring breathes slowly (4s ease). On 100% goal hit, flashes bright green and pulses continuously for 5s.
- **Interactions:** 
  - Tap center ring: crossfades glasses ↔ ml (150ms).
  - Tap quick add: flashes green checkmark, ring re-sweeps.
  - Swipe left on log: reveals red delete.
- **Haptics:** Light impact on quick log. Medium impact on 100% goal achievement.
- **Reduced motion path:** Skips confetti burst and 5s green glow loop. Replaces with a 280ms static green-glow settle. Ring updates via instant opacity fade instead of sweep.
