# 4. Layout Anatomy
The screen is a horizontal pager. The top-to-bottom structural hierarchy is persistent across all 4 panels:
1. **System Status Bar:** Transparent, light style.
2. **Top Bar Region:** Contains the transparent `Skip` ghost button aligned right.
3. **Motion Graphic Area (Hero):** ~55% of viewport height. Renders directly on the background atmosphere. 
4. **Text & Insight Region:** Center-aligned Headline (with Tiempos italic accent) + supporting subtext. Panel 3 replaces subtext with an `InlineArtifactCard`.
5. **Pagination Indicator:** Dots indicating slide state out of 4.
6. **Primary CTA:** A full-width pill button anchored bottom.
7. **System Home Indicator:** Standard OS glyph.

# 9. All States
- **Default (Active):** Auto-advancing or idle navigation through the 4 panels.
- **Empty (Established User):** Valid sessions bypass this screen entirely (Splash → Home).
- **Skeleton:** If assets download slowly, a depth-preserving skeleton renders (pulsing `--surface-3` shapes matching the motion graphic and text). Primary CTA renders visibly disabled (40% opacity).
- **Error (Asset Failure):** A warm-toned static PNG keyframe replaces the motion canvas. Text remains fully functional. If animation stutters, the last rendered frame persists without locking the UI.
- **Success:** Swiping or tapping `next` triggers a satisfying 200ms snap to the adjacent panel.
- **Disabled:** If network required for auth flow is lost, CTA dims to 40% opacity, displays a network glyph, and locks. Error toast shows.

# 10. Motion & Interaction
- **Physical Easing:** Snaps use native `spring (stiffness: 300, damping: 30)`. Text choreography fades in and translates +12px upward over 250ms (`ease-out`).
- **Feedback:** Buttons yield a `.98` scale transform on press (150ms).
- **Glow Behavior:** The background atmosphere subtly shifts hue (Orange → Purple → Green) over 800ms upon snapping to a new panel. The active CarouselDots morph from circle to pill.
- **Haptics:** Light `selection` tick on panel snap. `success` notification haptic on hitting the final panel.
- **Reduced Motion:** Text enters instantly. Lottie/Rive snap instantly to final resting frame. Pagination updates immediately. Glows are static.

# 11. Motivation-Tier Adaptation
- **Low Density (Minimalist):** Hides the subtext and pagination dots entirely, relying purely on the Motion Graphic and Headline.
- **Medium Density (Default):** Exactly as designed in the wireframe. Balances text, hero, and controls.
- **High Density (Comprehensive):** Surfaces the `InlineArtifactCard` and `ChipDomainTag` overlays on Panels 1, 2, and 4 (e.g., showing the 9 domains as labeled chips on Panel 1) to provide immediate structural context.

# 12. Accessibility
- **AA+ Contrast:** Primary text is `#FEFAF3` over `#0A0A0F` (Ratio: 19.5:1).
- **Targets:** The `skip` button and pagination dots maintain a minimum 44x44px invisible bounding box to prevent mis-taps.
- **Screen Reader:** Carousel is encoded as a `pager`. Swiping right/left reads the new Headline and Subtext via live-regions. Glyph-only icons possess explicit `accessibilityLabel` values (e.g., "Next panel", "Skip onboarding").
