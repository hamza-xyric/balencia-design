# VISUAL-001 X1 — cross-system audit

Date: 2026-07-10 PKT
Scope: `balencia-screens/src/app/globals.css`, current hifi kit and 104 canonical screen modules, current canon/specs/ledgers, fresh local/Railway baselines.
Method: embedded Design Auditor 19-lens rubric at developer-handoff / WCAG AA, current Balencia source hierarchy, static inventories, and manual contact-sheet review. The skill's separate `references/` bundle is absent; no unavailable appendix was inferred.

## Verdict

The prototype has a recognizable, coherent warm-dark Balencia shell, but it is not yet visually or interaction-system final. The strongest foundation is composition: 104/104 canonical routes render in the intended 390×844 frame, local and Railway structure agree, orange/green/purple roles are broadly recognizable, and repeated shell/card conventions make the product feel related across families.

The release boundary is systemic rather than route availability. Shared controls are frequently visual-only, active CTA contrast fails, semantic text is often too small or too translucent, the CIA orb does not visually encode state, the CTA family lacks a complete state/art contract, no signature icon registry exists, image slots remain placeholders, and one-state screenshots often stand in for multi-state specs. Final orb/CTA/icon art direction is explicitly blocked until Image 1 and Image 2 are supplied.

## Positive foundation worth preserving

- The official logo assets, ink/paper base, warm-brown glass and constrained orange/green/purple roles already make the prototype recognizably Balencia.
- The 390×844 composition is stable across 104 routes; no missing phone frames, visible SIA token or console-error screen appeared in fresh scans.
- Shell rhythm, top bars, bottom navigation, card radii and general spacing are coherent enough to support a shared-system remediation rather than 104 unrelated redesigns.
- Global reduced-motion support is present and should be extended, not replaced.
- The product usually distinguishes CIA/purple from action/orange and success/green; the remediation should sharpen this grammar rather than add more colors.
