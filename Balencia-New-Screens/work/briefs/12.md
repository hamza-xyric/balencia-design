# Functional Content Brief: Home / Today Screen

### 1. Purpose
The daily command center and primary entry point for returning users. It answers: "What is worth my attention today?" by curating a cross-domain, AI-driven action list and visualizing whole-life balance.

### 2. Entry and Exit Paths
*   **Entry Paths:** Splash screen (returning users with valid session) or Initial Plan Summary (first-time users completing onboarding).
*   **Exits:** Stack pushes to Goal/Mission Detail, RPG Character, Schedule/Calendar, Create Mission, and Domain Dashboards. Tab switches to CIA Chat, Goals List. Deep-links from action cards to specific feature screens (e.g., Breathing, Water, Journal).

### 3. Information Architecture (Top-to-Bottom)
1.  **Sticky Header:** Time-contextual greeting, user name, current date, and RPG level badge.
2.  **CIA Greeting Card:** Emotional anchor; conversational daily tone-setter. Includes optional mood chips. Demoted to a quiet visual preamble (no heavy glow).
3.  **Life Balance Hero Card:** The primary focal point. Contains the Constellation Radar, a daily energy companion tile, average balance metrics, and a CIA read.
4.  **Metric Cards Row:** 3 horizontally aligned cards showing live vitals (Heart Rate, Steps, Sleep) with trend sparklines.
5.  **Today’s Actions Section:** A continuous momentum header followed by a vertical stack of cross-domain action cards.
6.  **Pinned Missions Section:** A vertical stack of up to 3 priority user missions.
7.  **Coming Up Section:** Schedule preview for upcoming time-sensitive commitments.
8.  **Connection Spotted (Insight) Card:** Intermittent module showing high-value cross-domain correlations.
9.  **Recent Activity Feed:** Collapsed list of recent XP events and completions.

### 4. Features and Capabilities
*   **Constellation Radar Visualization:** A 12-domain radar chart with a central "sun" hub displaying Life Power. Features star dots for domains, honest ghosted spokes for un-synced data, and tap interactions to view specific domains or expand the breakdown.
*   **Charge Meter:** A segmented daily energy/readiness gauge sized as a companion below the radar.
*   **CIA Action Engine:** Curates a prioritized list of daily actions across all life domains (fitness, finance, wellness). Supports deep-linking to native features.
*   **Action Interactions:** Swipe right to complete, swipe left to skip, tap checkbox to complete, tap body to reveal CIA’s reasoning ("Why this matters").
*   **Pinned Mission Tracking:** Vertical cards showing progress rings, mission type, domain, next step, XP, and streaks.
*   **Correlation Engine:** CIA discovers and displays real cross-domain correlations (e.g., "Sleep drops 30% when you skip exercise").
*   **Mood Capture:** Optional mood chips within the CIA card to log emotional state.
*   **Quick Actions:** Horizontal scroll of 1-tap shortcuts to high-frequency features (Breathing, Water, Journal, Check-in).
*   **Pull-to-Refresh:** Triggers a branded spinner to fetch updated data across all modules.

### 6. Data Fields and Metrics
*   **User Level/Life Power:** RPG system (overall character power, logarithmic XP curve). Source: `user.lifePower`.
*   **Domain Stats:** 10 domain scores (0-99 scale). Source: `domainStats`.
*   **Average Balance:** Weekly domain average and delta. Source: `domainProgress[].weekDelta`.
*   **Vitals (HR, Steps, Sleep):** Wearable sync APIs (Apple Health, WHOOP) or manual entries. Includes 7-point historical trend array.
*   **Today's Momentum:** Completion percentage of daily actions and XP earned. Source: `todayActions`, `xp`.
*   **Mission Progress:** Percentage to goal, next action text, streak count. Source: Missions system.
*   **Schedule Items:** Time and event names. Source: Calendar sync or manual entries.
*   **Insight Correlations:** Text descriptions of data relationships. Source: CIA correlation engine.

### 7. Screen States Mentioned
*   **Motivation Tiers:** 
    *   *Low:* Simplified layout (1-2 actions, no radar, gentle tone).
    *   *Medium (Default):* 4-6 actions, collapsed activity feed.
    *   *High:* Full data, schedule conflict flags, expanded activity feed.
*   **Cold-Start / Day 1:** Radar renders as a faint full polygon with ghosted spokes (hub reads "Building your balance"). Displays 1-2 starter actions and a "Create your first mission" prompt. Schedule and insights hidden. Genesis XP event shown in feed.
*   **Loading:** Depth-preserving skeletons (rings/spokes/axes visible, drawing into data). Metric cards show skeleton lines morphing into sparklines.
*   **All-Done (Success):** Action cards replaced by a centered green checkmark and completion messaging. Momentum bar arrives fully green.
*   **Partial Sync / Empty:** Metrics show "Connect a device" affordance. Radar uses ghosted spokes for missing domains (no-data ≠ 0).
*   **Error / Offline:** Cached data retained. Network error banner appears below sticky header. Sync failures show quiet "tap to retry" affordances.
*   **Component Micro-States:** Pressed, Focus-visible (offset orange ring), Disabled (0.4 opacity), Swiping (proportional reveal).

### 8. Interactions, Gestures, and Motion Intents
*   **Gestures:** Swipe right (>30%) to complete action (green reveal). Swipe left (>30%) to skip (gray reveal). Snap-back if under threshold. Pull down (from top) for branded spinner refresh. Tap mood chips to log state.
*   **Motion (Choreographed Entrance):** 1) Radar polygon draws itself (L→R stroke animation, not scale). 2) Star dots stagger in. 3) Hub number counts up. 4) Metric sparklines draw. 5) Momentum bar fills continuously. 6) Cards fade-up on scroll.
*   **Haptics:** Light impact on presses/checkbox taps. Medium impact on mood chip selection and pull-to-refresh release. Success/error notifications on action completion.
*   **Accessibility Gestures:** Swipe-to-complete is backed by an explicit tap checkbox alternative. Pull-to-refresh available via assistive tech rotor.

### 10. Cross-Domain Hooks (Correlations, AI Insights, Life Areas)
*   **The Constellation Radar:** The core cross-domain hook. Visualizes all 10-12 life areas simultaneously as a constellation, with the central Life Power hub aggregating overall performance.
*   **Cross-Domain Actions:** The CIA Action Engine actively curates tasks across disparate domains (e.g., mixing "Morning run" (Fitness) with "Review budget" (Finance) in the same action stack).
*   **Connection Spotted Insights:** The CIA actively surfaces multi-domain correlations (e.g., linking Sleep quality to Exercise consistency).
*   **Cross-Domain Tagging:** Action and Mission cards display multiple domain tags inline, highlighting overlapping life areas.
