# Functional Content Brief: Intelligence Dashboard

## 1. Purpose
The CIA Intelligence Dashboard is the app’s analytical command center, synthesizing cross-domain life data into a unified health score. It serves as a premium, non-shaming briefing that surfaces AI-driven insights, contradictions, and behavioral correlations to build user trust and prompt positive action. 

## 2. Entry and Exit Paths
*   **Entry Paths:**
    *   Explore Section [18] (Module card stack push)
    *   CIA Chat [09] (Deep-link via referenced insight)
    *   Home Screen [12] (Proactive insight card stack push)
    *   Life Areas Overview [16] ("See insights" link stack push)
*   **Exit Paths:**
    *   **Back/Explore [18]:** Standard stack pop.
    *   **CIA Chat [09]:** Tab switch with context pre-loaded (accessed via insight, correlation, best day, or prediction taps).
    *   **Goal Detail [14]:** Stack push (via pillar sparkline taps if referencing a goal).
    *   **Domain Dashboards [26-36]:** Stack push (via pillar sparkline or weekly report domain pills).
    *   **Knowledge Graph [72]:** Stack push via "Explore your health knowledge graph".
    *   **Weekly Report Detail:** Stack push via "see full report".
    *   **Contradiction Resolution Flow:** Modal bottom sheet via "resolve".

## 3. Complete Information Architecture
The screen is a vertically scrolling `ScrollView` (~1400-1800pt). 

1.  **Sticky Header (88pt expanded → 48pt collapsed):** Back chevron, "Intelligence" title, 3pt royal-purple accent line, and overflow menu `[...]`.
2.  **Daily Score Hero Card (~280pt):** Focal anchor. Houses the 120pt score gauge, trend delta text, pillar sparkline row (Fitness, Nutrition, Wellbeing), and last updated timestamp.
3.  **Active Contradictions Section (Variable height):** Structural eyebrow + count badge. Stacked alert cards containing the contradiction, source tags, severity dial, and action chips.
4.  **Correlations Section (~180pt+):** Section hero. Houses the N×N Correlation Matrix (Tier 1) followed by ranked coaching rows (Tier 2), and a "see all" link.
5.  **Score Trend Chart Card (~220pt):** Structural eyebrow, time range selector (7d/14d/30d), and the line chart with projected forecast.
6.  **Best Day Formula Card (~260pt):** Structural eyebrow, factor checklist with daily statuses, and overall progress bar.
7.  **Weekly Report Summary Card (~160pt collapsed/320pt expanded):** Structural eyebrow, date range header, summary text, expandable domain score metrics, and report link.
8.  **Predictions Card (~160pt):** Predicted score mini-gauge, basis text, accuracy badge, and feedback row.
9.  **Recent Insights Section (~160pt):** AI eyebrow. Stacked insight cards featuring the CIA indicator dot, text, timestamp, and feedback buttons.
10. **Knowledge Graph Link Card (~64pt):** Navigational card to the visual graph sub-screen.

## 4. Features and Capabilities
*   **Score Comprehension:** View unified daily intelligence score (0–100) broken down by pillars.
*   **Contradiction Resolution:** Detect and act on data mismatches (e.g., self-reported sleep vs. wearable data) via "resolve" or "dismiss" actions, promoting transparency.
*   **Cross-Domain Matrix:** Interact with an N×N correlation grid surfacing the strength and direction (reinforcing/competing) of relationships between life domains.
*   **Temporal Tracking:** Scrub historical score data across 7, 14, or 30 days, paired with AI predictive forecasting.
*   **Behavioral Blueprinting:** Track daily progress against a personalized "Best Day" formula checklist.
*   **Report Summarization:** Expand/collapse weekly domain performance summaries.
*   **Predictive Insights:** View tomorrow’s predicted score, accuracy confidence, and the behavioral basis for the prediction.
*   **AI Feedback Loop:** Provide explicit thumbs up/down feedback on individual correlations and insights to tune CIA’s recommendations.
*   **Accessibility & Tailoring:** Low/Medium/High motivation states dynamically hide/show sections to reduce cognitive load.

## 7. Screen States
*   **Cold-start (Day 1-3):** Score ring shows faint full track with "--" (never 0%). Sparklines hidden. Contradictions hidden. Matrix and trends show "analyzing" copy.
*   **Loading:** Depth-preserving skeletons. Ring tracks and ticks visible with radial shimmer. Trend outlines animate draw-in.
*   **Empty/Partial:** Unsynced pillars render ghosted/dashed lines (distinct from real zero values). Contradictions hide entirely (absence = positive state). 
*   **Error:** Inline section errors: "couldn't load [section name] — tap to retry". Failures degrade gracefully without crashing the screen.
*   **Offline:** "you're offline — showing cached data" banner. Pull-to-refresh dimmed.
*   **Motivation Variants:** 
    *   *Low:* Hero card only (no sparklines) + Best Day (top 3 factors).
    *   *Medium:* Default view (7d trend, collapsed weekly).
    *   *High:* All expanded + "Deep Patterns" section (second-order correlations).

## 8. Interactions, Gestures, and Motion
*   **Gestures:** Pull-to-refresh, Tap to expand (score ring, best day factors), Tap to navigate (pillars, knowledge graph), Swipe left (dismiss contradiction), Press-hold (scrub trend chart with haptics).
*   **Motion Choreography (Entrance):** 
    1. Score GaugeRing arc draws, count-up animates simultaneously.
    2. Pillar Sparklines draw Left-to-Right (120ms stagger).
    3. Structural cards fade up (80ms stagger).
    4. Below-fold: Trend line draws, Matrix cells scale-in row-by-row, MomentumBar fills.
*   **Micro-interactions:** "Resolve" chip shows inline spinner then brief green glow. Dismissing cards slides left and collapses height. Thumbs feedback buttons scale 1.0 to 1.3 and settle. Confetti bursts (purple/green particles) when all Best Day factors are matched.
*   **Reduced Motion:** All charts render instantly at final state; Living Lines remain fully drawn, loops disabled.

## 9. Free vs Premium Gating
*   **Premium / Pro Tier Required:** This entire screen requires Pro. Composite intelligence scores, AI-mode visualizations, active contradictions, cross-domain correlations, and future predictions are advanced AI analytics gated behind the Pro tier.

## 10. Cross-Domain Hooks
*   **Correlation Engine:** The N×N Correlation Matrix visualizes direct quantified relationships across multiple domains (Fitness, Sleep, Nutrition, Wellbeing, Finance, etc.). Example: Meditation ↔ Stress.
*   **Deep Patterns (High Motivation):** Surfaces second-order cross-domain correlations (e.g., "When you meditate AND sleep 7+ hours, your fitness score is 25% higher").
*   **Goal Detail & Domain Dashboards:** Tapping pillar sparklines or weekly report pills deep-links the user directly into specific life-domain tracking screens.

### ⚠️ Source Contradiction Flags
1. **Elevation / Correlation Layout:** The design spec text states the `CorrelationMatrix` (Tier 1) appears *beneath* readable rows. However, the "Design Elevation" and final ASCII wireframes explicitly *promote* the matrix as the section hero above the ranked rows. **Resolution applied:** Prioritized the "Design Elevation" pass (Matrix as Tier-1 Hero above Tier-2 rows).
2. **Empty State Wording:** The *State Craft* table dictates cold-start copy as "SIA is getting to know you — your score appears in a few days" (sentence case). The older *Empty States* section lists it as "SIA is getting to know you. Your health intelligence score will appear in a few days." Both are retained as functional variants.
3. **Persona Renaming:** Explicitly renamed all occurrences of "SIA" (Standard Intelligence Assistant) to "CIA" per project directive.
