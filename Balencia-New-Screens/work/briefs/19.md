# Functional Content Brief: RPG Character Screen

## 1. Purpose
A premium, gamified "profile card" that visualizes a user’s whole-life progression, answering "how far have I come?" through overall level, XP, 10-domain stat scores, and mission history. It is a read-only, celebratory, non-shaming summary of the user's RPG stats.

## 2. Entry & Exit Paths
*   **Entry Paths:** Me Main [17] (via RPG level badge tap, stats row tap, or quick links grid).
*   **Primary Exit:** Back button / edge swipe → Me Main [17].
*   **Secondary Exits:** 
    *   Avatar → Edit Profile modal.
    *   Domain Sub-Stats sheet → "view dashboard" link → Domain dashboard.
    *   Mission row → Archived Goal Detail [14].
    *   Achievement badges → Celebration Overlay [42] / Achievement Gallery [71].
    *   Streak card → Streak Details [59].
    *   "view full journal" link → Mission Journal [73].

## 3. Information Architecture
The screen is a vertical scroll view containing the following modules, top-to-bottom:
1.  **Navigation Header:** Back button + "your character" title.
2.  **Character Card:** Avatar, user name, overall level diamond, rank title, and overall XP progress bar. Acts as an identity preamble.
3.  **Constellation Radar Hero:** A focal chart mapping the "shape" of all 10 life domains. Absorbs the standalone "Life Power" number into its center sun-hub. Includes a one-line cross-domain insight.
4.  **Domain Skills Section:** "DOMAIN SKILLS" eyebrow followed by a 3-column grid (4 rows: 3-3-3-1) of 10 compact domain cards showing individual stat scores and XP.
5.  **Ranked Magnitude Read:** "RANKED" eyebrow with a grouped bar chart showing all 10 domains side-by-side for quick magnitude comparison.
6.  **Stats Summary Row:** A 4-cell text-led row showing streak, missions done, active missions, and Life Power (echoing the radar hub).
7.  **Streak & Rewards Section:** "STREAK & REWARDS" eyebrow with active streak, XP multiplier, streak freezes, and a mini badge wall of recent rewards.
8.  **Mission History Section:** "MISSION HISTORY" eyebrow with a list of up to 5 recent completed goals and a link to the full journal.

## 4. Features & Capabilities
*   **RPG Identity Display:** View user level, rank, avatar, and XP progression.
*   **Cross-Domain Shape Visualization (Constellation Radar):** Visualizes the balance/spikiness of the user's life domains at a glance.
*   **Life Power Calculation:** View overall life proficiency score (calculated via active domain stats, consistency, and balance multiplier).
*   **Domain Drill-Downs:** Tap any domain card or radar axis to open a bottom sheet showing 3-5 sub-stats, tier labels, and a link to the specific domain dashboard.
*   **Stat Scrubbing (Micro-interaction):** Long-press a radar spoke to "scrub" or explore that domain's stat.
*   **Hub Expansion:** Tap the radar sun-hub to expand into a full 10-domain ranked breakdown.
*   **Honest Deltas:** View week-over-week metric changes (e.g., streak, Life Power) via fixed-window delta arrows.
*   **Mission Tracking:** View recent mission completions, including domain tags, dates, and XP earned.
*   **Streak Mechanics:** View active streaks, XP multipliers, and streak freeze availability.
*   **Reward Tracking:** View rarity-graded achievement badges (earned, in-progress, locked).
*   **Profile Editing:** Tap avatar to edit profile details.

## 6. Data Fields & Metrics
*   **Life Power:** `sum(active_domain_stats) * (1.0 + 0.15 * (1 - coeff_variation(active_stats)))`. Source: RPG Stats API.
*   **Overall Character Level:** `floor(overall_power / 4) + 1`. Source: RPG Stats API.
*   **Domain Stat Score (0-99):** `(consistency * 0.40) + (depth * 0.35) + (trend * 0.25)`. Source: RPG Domain Stats API.
*   **Domain Level XP:** `xp_required(level) = 100 * level^1.5`. Source: RPG Domain Stats API.
*   **Sub-stats:** 3-5 per domain mapping to tiers (Newcomer → Mastery). Source: RPG Domain Stats API.
*   **Mission History:** Mission name, type badge, domains tagged, date completed, XP earned. Source: Goals API.
*   **Streak Status:** Current streak count, multiplier tier, freeze count. Source: `GET /api/streaks/status`.
*   **Rewards:** Earned, in-progress, and locked achievements. Source: `GET /api/streaks/rewards`.
*   **KPI Deltas:** `weekDelta` metric for honest arrow comparisons. Source: `domainProgress`.

## 7. Screen States
*   **Cold-start / Day 1:** Character card is Lv.1/Beginner. Radar shows a faint full polygon with dashed spokes; hub reads "Building your balance" (no number). All domains are Lv.0/Stat 0, visibly dimmed/ghosted but tappable. Mission history shows prompt text + genesis "+10 XP Welcome" item.
*   **Loading:** Depth-preserving skeletons. Radar rings/spokes remain visible with radial shimmer. Domain GaugeRings show tracks. Stats and Mission rows show skeleton lines. Copy: "Building your character — one moment."
*   **Empty / Partial:** Domains with no data are rendered as ghosted/dashed spokes on the radar + dimmed gauges in the grid (no-data ≠ a real 0).
*   **Error:** Per-section failures. Cached data is retained where possible. A network banner names the failed zone (e.g., "Couldn't load mission history") with a "try again" link.
*   **Offline:** Cached data retained. Action buttons are dimmed with a reason. Pull-to-refresh is disabled with an "offline" affordance.
*   **Level-up Celebration:** A temporal state where the XP Living Line completes its draw to green, avatar glow pulses green, and a subtle confetti particle burst occurs.
