# FUNCTIONAL CONTENT BRIEIF: Medication Tracking

## 1. Purpose
Acts as the user's daily medication management hub, answering "what do I need to take today and have I been consistent?" Tracks adherence, manages schedules, and integrates safely with broader health and coaching ecosystems.

## 2. Entry and Exit Paths
*   **Entry Paths:** Wellbeing Dashboard (via "Medications" card); Explore screen; CIA Chat (via deep-link from medication reminder); Home Screen (action card); Settings > Health.
*   **Exit Paths:** Back button (stack pop); CIA Chat (tab switch via coaching note tap); Add Medication (modal); Medication History (stack push); RPG Character screen (via RPG badge tap).

## 3. Complete Information Architecture
**A. Global Navigation & Headers**
*   **Sticky Screen Header:** Back chevron, Screen Title, Domain accent line, RPG Skill Level Badge. 

**B. Main Scrollable Content (Top-to-Bottom)**
*   **CIA Coaching Note:** Contextual AI preamble introducing the daily status.
*   **Today’s Adherence Hero:** Primary focal point showing daily completion metrics.
*   **Safety Warning Banner:** Persistent, non-dismissible medical disclaimer.
*   **Today’s Schedule (Timeline):** Time-grouped list (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, As Needed) of medications scheduled for the current day, rendered as a temporal path.
*   **All Medications List:** Roster of all active medications including historical adherence and reminder status.
*   **Adherence History Grid:** 4-week calendar heatmap visualizing daily consistency.
*   **Privacy Notice:** Reassuring text regarding data encryption and visibility.
*   **Persistent FAB:** "Add medication" floating action button.

**C. Overlays & Modals**
*   **Add/Edit Medication Modal:** Bottom sheet form containing inputs for Medication Name, Dosage, Frequency, Time of Day, Start/End Dates, Notes, and Reminder configurations.

## 4. Features & Capabilities
*   **Daily Checklist & Temporal Tracking:** Visualizing the day's doses as a continuous drawn timeline rather than a flat checklist. 
*   **Adherence Analytics:** Real-time daily completion percentage and 4-week historical consistency grid.
*   **Medication Management (CRUD):** Add, edit, soft-delete, and view details of medications.
*   **Custom Dose Scheduling:** Configurable frequencies (daily, twice daily, weekly, PRN) and specific time anchors.
*   **Reminders:** Toggleable local notifications with customizable lead times (5min, 15min, 30min, 1hr).
*   **Drug Interaction Alerts:** Server-side check upon saving a medication that triggers a contextual warning banner if interactions exist.
*   **Midnight Rollover:** Automatic reset of daily checklists while permanently preserving historical adherence data.
*   **Motivation-Based UI Adaptation:** Screen complexity and data density scales down or up based on user's current motivation tier (Low/Medium/High).
*   **Offline Mode:** Cached viewing, action queuing, and graceful degradation when network is unavailable.

## 7. Screen States
*   **Cold-start / Day 1:** FAB-led empty state; charts and lists hidden; CIA note and Privacy notice remain to build trust.
*   **None-taken-yet (0%):** Hero displays ghosted-0 (not a failure state); first node on timeline pulses as "next-due".
*   **All-taken (100%):** Hero shifts to arrival green; CIA displays consistency affirmation.
*   **Loading:** Depth-preserving skeletons (rings, cells, outlines) that morph into drawn charts. Never blank discs.
*   **Error / Partial Failure:** Chart-specific honesty. Cached data shown; missing zones get explicit "couldn't load - tap to retry" copy without nuking the whole screen.
*   **Offline:** Dimmed actions; "showing last sync" copy; local action queuing.
*   **Midnight Rollover:** Previous day's checks freeze historically; today's timeline resets cleanly.
*   **Motivation Tiers:** Low (minimal text, next dose only, no charts), Medium (standard spec), High (extra sparklines, expanded stats, deeper analytics).

## 8. Interactions, Gestures & Motion
*   **Tap:** Checkbox nodes to toggle taken; FAB to open modal; RPG badge for nav; heatmap cells for tooltips; CIA card for chat nav.
*   **Swipe:** Left on check-row for skip/edit; Left on med-card for edit/delete.
*   **Long-press:** Medication rows for context menus.
*   **Pull:** Down on scrollview to force-sync and refresh.
*   **Drag:** Modal handle down to dismiss (with unsaved changes trap).
*   **Motion Intents:** Heavy emphasis on "draw, don't fade". The timeline literally draws top-to-bottom as the day progresses. Toggling a dose re-sweeps the adherence arc and progresses the path. Missed/lapsed states avoid red flashing or alarm-shaking; they animate calmly to muted "open door" states.

## 9. Free vs Premium Gating
*   **Free Tier:** Access to the daily checklist, basic medication list, adding/editing medications, and the adherence heatmap.
*   **Premium (Plus) Tier:** Unlocks CIA coaching notes, automated reminder scheduling, and drug-drug interaction warnings.

## 10. Cross-Domain Hooks
*   **CIA (AI Coach):** Full bidirectional integration. CIA reads adherence patterns to inform coaching tone (non-shaming, affirmative), and the user can deep-link straight from the screen into a contextual CIA chat.
*   **RPG / Avatar System:** Consistent tracking earns RPG XP, visually anchored by the Wellbeing skill badge in the header.
*   **Life Areas Radar:** Daily adherence metrics feed directly into the broader "Wellbeing" score on the user's life dashboard.

### ⚠️ Source Contradictions Flagged
*   **CONTRADICTION 1 (Checkmark Visuals):** The general component spec states checkboxes shift to `wellbeing-teal` fill when checked. However, the `Visualization` and `Design Elevation` modules explicitly revoke this, stating that teal-as-data-ink is a defect, mandating that checkmarks/nodes shift to `forest-green`. **Brief resolution:** Forest-green supersedes teal for data/completion states.
*   **CONTRADICTION 2 (Adherence Bar Component):** The ASCII wireframe and UI Copy sections reference a flat text "Adherence Summary Bar". The `Visualization` module explicitly mandates replacing this flat bar with a 96px `GaugeRing` hero. **Brief resolution:** The GaugeRing supersedes the flat bar.
*   **CONTRADICTION 3 (Checklist vs Timeline):** Layout sections refer to "Today's Medications Section" as a time-grouped checklist. The `Design Elevation` section mandates replacing this with a drawn `TimelineAgenda` day-track. **Brief resolution:** TimelineAgenda supersedes flat checklist.
