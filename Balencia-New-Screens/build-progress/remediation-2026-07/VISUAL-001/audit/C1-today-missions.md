## Verdict

The family has a strong warm-dark foundation, but it is not release-ready. No screen is acceptable wholly as-is. Screen 14 is closest to a conditional pass; screens 12, 13, 15, 41, 45, 59, 61, and 97 have blocking local or systemic defects. The fresh scanner catches only target/role symptoms on 15, 41, 45, 61, and 97; screen 12 reports `interactiveCount: 0` and screen 13 only `1`, despite many visible affordances.

## What is working

- All 11 frames render at 390×844 without runtime/console failures; `ScreenShell.tsx:28-30` provides a real scroll boundary, so below-fold content on 14, 44, 59, 61, 73, and 97 is not itself clipping.
- Orange effort, green completion, and purple Cia/projection are generally disciplined; charts usually pair color with text, icons, or accessible summaries.
- Screen 14 has excellent focal hierarchy and progressive disclosure; 44 has strong source/null treatment; 73 uses humane pivot language; 97 keeps Dismiss visible and does not coerce acceptance.
- `ConsentRail` controls are native buttons, and global reduced-motion CSS freezes `area-in`, `quiet-pulse`, radar, chart, skeleton, and related motion (`globals.css:797-842`).
