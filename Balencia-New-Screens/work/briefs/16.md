# Functional Content Brief: Life Areas Overview

## 1. Purpose (2 lines)
Provides a holistic visual snapshot of the user's progress across 12 life domains (10 active, 2 un-started) to answer "where am I strong, where do I need attention?" It serves as the cross-domain hub, fusing the Life Power proficiency score, temporal trajectories, and direct drill-downs into specific domain dashboards.

## 2. Entry and Exit Paths
**Entry Paths:**
*   **Goals List [13]:** Tap life areas preview (Stack push).
*   **Home Screen [12]:** Tap domain tags (Stack push).
*   **CIA Chat [09]:** Deep-link (Tab switch + push).
*   **Other:** Bottom of various screens with domain tags.

**Exit Paths:**
*   **Primary:** Tap domain (chart axis, star dot, or list row) → Domain dashboard [26-36] (Stack push).
*   **Secondary:** Tap CIA insight card → CIA Chat [09] (Tab switch with context pre-loaded).
*   **Tertiary:** Tap Back button / iOS swipe-from-left-edge → Goals List [13] (Stack pop).

## 3. Complete Information Architecture
**Top-to-Bottom Module Stack:**
1.  **Navigation Header (44pt):** Back chevron (left) + "life areas" title (center).
2.  **Constellation Radar - Hero (≥96pt):** 12-spoke radar chart. Life Power score fused inside the center "sun" hub. 10 active domain axes + 2 ghosted axes for un-started domains (Creativity, Learning). Tappable axes/star dots.
3.  **Balance Trajectory Pair:** 
    *   *Balance Sparkline:* 7-point curved line showing Life Power over the last 7 weeks.
    *   *KPI StatTile:* Average stat score + completeness ring (active domains / 12) + weekly delta.
4.  **CIA Insight Card (~72pt):** Contextual text coaching based on the user's radar shape. Purple left-border indicator.
5.  **Time Range Selector (36pt):** Segmented control (Current / vs week / vs month).
6.  **Domain StatBars List (56pt per row):** Ranked list of all 12 domains. Contains domain color dot, name, 0-99 stat score, progress bar, goal count, and optional delta swatch.

## 4. Features and Capabilities
*   **Life Balance Visualization:** Shape of the radar polygon tells the user's holistic story (spiky = specialist, circular = balanced).
*   **Life Power Hub:** Single competitive proficiency score calculated with a balance multiplier (up to 15% bonus for perfectly balanced stats).
*   **Expandable Hub:** Tap the radar hub to expand the card in place to the full 12-domain breakdown.
*   **Temporal Comparison:** Toggle between current stats, last week, or last month. Overlays a dashed "ghost" polygon of the prior period over the current one. Outward growth and inward decline are visually marked.
*   **Trajectory Sparkline:** View the 7-week history of the overarching Life Power score.
*   **CIA Coaching:** AI-generated insights adapting to motivation levels and actual data (highlights strengths, frames weaknesses constructively).
*   **Domain Drill-down:** Direct navigation to individual domain dashboards.
*   **Goal Tracking:** Visibility of active goal counts per domain.
*   **Motivation Adaptation:** UI adapts to user state (e.g., hiding 0-goal domains for low motivation; showing deep analytical trends for high motivation).

## 6. Data Fields and Metrics
| Field / Metric | Data Source | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Life Power Score** (e.g., 487) | `user.lifePower` (`mock.ts`) | `sum(active stats) * balance_multiplier`. Max 15% bonus for perfect variance. |
| **Domain Stat Score** (0-99) | Domain stat scores API / `domainStats` | Auto-calculated from consistency, depth, trend. |
| **Active Goal Count** | Domain goals API | Integer count per domain. |
| **7-Week Life Power History** | `domainProgress[].weekDelta` / weekly snapshots | Used for the sparkline trajectory. |
| **Average Stat** | Derived | Mean of all active domain stats. |
| **Completeness Ratio** | Derived | Active domains with data ÷ 12. |

## 7. Screen States
*   **Cold-start / Day-1:** Radar shows a faint full polygon at ~10% on all axes. Hub reads "Building your balance" (never a 0). All 12 spokes visible (creativity + learning ghosted). Sparkline ghosts missing weeks. CIA shows onboarding note. Time range selector locked to "Current". 
*   **Partial / Empty (Un-started Domain):** Radar shows ghosted/dashed spokes at the origin for un-started domains. Domain list row text dims; stat shows `—` (not 0); goal count reads "Tap to explore".
*   **Loading:** Depth-preserving skeletons. Radar shows rings + 12 spokes with a radial shimmer pulse that morphs into the drawn fill. Domain bars animate width 0 → value. CIA card shows a 2-line shimmer. No spinners.
*   **Error (Partial Domain Failure):** Radar successful vertices remain; failed domains are dashed spokes. Failed domain rows show `—` stat, empty bar, "Data unavailable — tap to refresh". No alarm-red colors.
*   **Error (Total Network / API Timeout):** Radar shows grid rings/axes only. Domain list skeletons. CIA card hidden/collapsed. Toast: `"Couldn't load latest data."`
*   **Offline:** Time range selector locked to "Current". Pull-to-refresh disabled. Banner: `"You're offline. Cached data shown."`
*   **Success (Comparison Toggle):** Ghost polygon draws itself. Growth/decline glow and glyphs fade in, hold, then fade out. 

## 8. Interactions, Gestures, Motion Intents
*   **Tap Targets (≥44x44pt):** Chart axis labels, chart star dots, domain list rows, back button, time range segments, CIA card.
*   **Gestures:** 
    *   Tap domain (chart/list) → Domain dashboard.
    *   Tap radar hub → Expand 12-domain breakdown.
    *   Tap CIA card → CIA Chat.
    *   Pull-to-refresh → Retry data fetches.
    *   Swipe-from-left-edge → Stack pop.
*   **Motion Choreography:**
    *   *Entrance:* Radar polygon stroke "draws itself" (1200ms, left-to-right). Star dots fade-in/stagger. Life Power counts up 0 → value. Sparkline draws L→R. Domain bars rise 0 → width.
    *   *Comparison Toggle:* Vertices morph old → new values (280ms). Ghost polygon draws itself.
*   **Accessibility Motion (`prefers-reduced-motion`):** All animations bypass. Radar is fully drawn, sparkline is static, numbers are at final state.

## 9. Free vs. Premium (Tier) Gating
*   **Free Tier:** Access to "Current" time range only. 
*   **Premium (Plus) Tier:** Unlocks "vs week" and "vs month" comparison overlays, the 7-week Balance Sparkline, and the weekly delta KPI. 
*   **Gate Prerequisite:** Comparison unlocks only after 14 days of history. 
*   **Gate UI:** Locked segments render at 40% opacity with a lock icon and "Plus" label. Pre-threshold (Plus user with <14 days history) replaces the sparkline delta with a calm "building history" hint—no manufactured scarcity.

## 10. Cross-Domain Hooks (Correlations, AI Insights)
*   **CIA AI Coaching:** The CIA insight card analyzes the radar shape to generate holistic observations, explicitly correlating multiple domains (e.g., *"Fitness up 12% this month, Career down 8% — the correlation may be time allocation"*).
*   **Navigation Hooks:** Acts as the central hub routing to all 12 individual life domain dashboards.

## 11. Resolved Contradictions & Spec Conflicts
*(Per Audit Integration, the following conflicts in older source documents were resolved):*
*   **COMPONENT HIERARCHY:** Older specs listed the CIA card first in visual priority. **Resolution:** The Constellation Radar is the singular hero focal point (≥96pt, heavy glow). The CIA card is strictly a secondary, quieter preamble.
*   **LIFE POWER PLACEMENT:** Older specs placed Life Power as orphaned text below the chart. **Resolution:** Absorbed directly into the center "sun" hub of the radar chart.
*   **AXIS COUNT:** Older specs defined a 10-axis radar. **Resolution:** Upgraded to 12 axes to match the canonical Constellation spec; Creativity and Learning are included as ghosted/dashed spokes until activated.
*   **NEGATIVE DELTA STYLING:** Older specs allowed alarm-red for declines. **Resolution:** Superseded by non-shaming `stalled-amber` paired with a visible `▼` glyph. Declines are framed as "needs attention," never failure. 
*   **DELTA VISUALIZATION:** Older specs used inline text deltas. **Resolution:** Upgraded to low-prominence end-cap `ArcGauge` swatches on progress bars for at-a-glance readability.
