# Premium Benchmark Matrix — per cluster

A screen is graded against the **right** best-in-class app for its kind, not a single bar. This **reuses** the viz-audit's per-cluster data-viz matrix and **extends** it to the content/LOW clusters the viz-audit skipped. Always-on baselines apply to every screen.

> Naming a benchmark is **for calibration, not cloning.** The methodology requires one sentence per screen on *how it stays unmistakably Balencia* (warm ink / burnt-orange / forest-green / royal-purple, warm-glow surface craft, the Living Line / Constellation signature, the brand period) rather than a screenshot of the benchmark. Borrowing a competitor's signature device 1:1 caps Signature (dim 4) and Anti-generic (dim 14) at **B**.

## Always-on baselines (every screen)

| Baseline | What it sets |
|---|---|
| **Apple Health / iOS** | the honest, accessible, restrained floor — never below this on clarity or a11y |
| **Linear · Things · Stripe** | editorial restraint, spacing discipline, motion craft, microcopy precision — the "calm premium" floor for any content screen |
