Provides a distraction-free, immersive conversational voice interface where the user and the CIA interact naturally. Simulates a premium, face-to-face coaching experience by replacing dense datadashboards with state-reactive ambiance, direct transcription, and warm cross-pillar intelligence.

### 9. All states
- **Default:** Avatar breathing (4000ms sinusoidal scale), idle state.
- **Skeleton:** `SkeletonState` block pulse on the avatar container and transcription text area while audio engine initializes (<500ms).
- **Empty (Mic Permission Denied):** `HonestNullState` utilizing `SafetyResourceCard` layout principles. Calm `--surface-2` card with the copy and an orange `Open settings` BtnPrimary.
- **Error (Network/API):** `ErrorState` sub-zone. Avatar freezes, waveform dims to 20% opacity, conversational recovery copy is displayed.
- **Success:** Upon completed voice turn, avatar flashes `--glow-done` (#34A853) for 250ms before returning to idle.
- **Disabled:** Mute toggle (Mic off). Button visually loses glow, drops to 40% opacity.

### 10. Motion & interaction
- **Physical Easing:** `cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)` for screen entry and avatar scaling.
- **Feedback:** `150-250ms` press scale (.98) on mic toggle and close (✕).
- **Glow Behavior:** "Draw, don't fade." Screen slides up; avatar scales up and glow expands radically as a signature entrance. Glow transitions use `280ms ease-out-soft` color crossfade between User (`--glow-you`) and CIA (`--glow-cia`).
- **Haptics:** Light `HapticImpact` on state transition (Listening -> Processing). Success `HapticNotification` when payload is captured.
- **Reduced-motion path:** Breathing pulse disabled (static orb). Particles frozen. Transcription words fade in as blocks (rather than sequentially). Swipe-to-dismiss replaced by ✕ tap target.

### 11. Motivation-tier adaptation

- **Low Density:** CIA uses simpler vocabulary, confirms basic context ("I hear you saying you're tired"), limits cross-domain referencing.

- **High Density:** CIA speaks with high data specificity, seamlessly weaving multi-domain data into coaching ("Your sleep consistency is down, which correlates with your skipped morning workouts and dip in focus scores. Let's adjust.")
