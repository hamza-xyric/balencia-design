## Authority constraints that affect all three screens

- The native reference is 390×844 and dark-only (`Balencia-New-Screens/canon/COMPACT-CANON.md:7-8`); all controls require AA+ contrast, 44px targets, glyph labels, and reduced-motion variants (`Balencia-New-Screens/canon/COMPACT-CANON.md:83-87`). - Royal purple means Cia/AI synthesis, projection, or premium—not generic recovery (`Balencia-New-Screens/canon/COMPACT-CANON.md:32-42`). Real chart history is solid orange, projections dashed purple, and milestones green (`Balencia-New-Screens/canon/COMPACT-CANON.md:74`; `Balencia-New-Screens/canon/COMPONENT-CATALOG.md:66`).
- Health data, photos, and third-party sources require consent state plus revoke/delete entry points (`Balencia-New-Screens/canon/COMPACT-CANON.md:83`); the canonical `ConsentCard` additionally states data, purpose, retention, accept/decline, and revoke path (`Balencia-New-Screens/canon/COMPONENT-CATALOG.md:114`). Locked features require a blurred preview, value copy, and one orange unlock CTA (`Balencia-New-Screens/canon/COMPACT-CANON.md:84`; `Balencia-New-Screens/canon/COMPONENT-CATALOG.md:112`).
- The life model is ten domains scored 0–99 (`RPG_SYSTEM_DESIGN.md:33-48`). The correlation authority is a ten-node, 45-pair weighted graph (`LIFE_CORRELATION_MATRIX.md:38-59`); weights are co-movement, explicitly not causality (`LIFE_CORRELATION_MATRIX.md:55-57`). Personal correlations begin with no confidence and grow with observations (`LIFE_CORRELATION_MATRIX.md:79-81`), with a default 60-point full-confidence threshold (`LIFE_CORRELATION_MATRIX.md:270-286`). These facts make sample/window/freshness/confidence and confirmed-vs-estimated provenance mandatory for user-specific claims.
