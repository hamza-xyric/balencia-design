# Functional Content Brief: Reputation & Trust Score Profile

## 1. Purpose
Provides members with a transparent, quantifiable view of their standing, reliability, and influence within the Balencia community. Showcases reputation tiers, historical progression, and the core metrics that build interpersonal and systemic trust. 

## 2. Entry and Exit Paths
**Entry Paths:**
*   Primary Navigation: `Community` Tab -> `Reputation` module (`/reputation`).
*   Deep-link via User Profile (`/profile/[id]`): Clicking a user's "Trust Score" badge.
*   Deep-link via Leaderboards (`/leaderboard`): Tapping a ranked member's score.
*   Deep-link via Community Feed (`/feed`): Tapping an interaction metric (e.g., upvotes) on a user's post.

**Exit Paths:**
*   Back/Chevron: Returns to the referring screen (Leaderboard, Feed, or Profile).
*   "Learn How to Earn": Deep-links out to `/wiki` (Reputation guidelines).
*   "Resolve Flags": Deep-links to `/contracts` (Accountability).
*   "Connect with Mentor": Deep-links to `/people` (Relationships).

## 3. Complete Information Architecture (Mobile-Inferred)
*Inferred from route groupings (`social`, `knowledge`, `accountability`) and standard gamification patterns.*

*   **Header Region:** Screen Title, User Profile Snippet (Avatar, Name).
*   **Module 1: Trust & Reputation Hero:** Overall composite score, current tier badge, progress indicator to next tier.
*   **Module 2: Score Breakdown:** Horizontal scroll or grid of core contributing metrics (consistency, helpfulness, engagement, accountability).
*   **Module 3: Reputation History:** Chronological log or trend chart of score changes over time.
*   **Module 4: Tiers & Privileges:** Horizontal scroll of tier ladders (Current, Next, Locked).
*   **Module 5: CIA Insights:** AI-generated qualitative summary of the user's social standing.
*   **Module 6: Cross-Domain Impact & Moderation:** Trust flags, accountability links, and domain correlations.

## 4. Features and Capabilities
*   **Composite Trust Score Calculation:** Real-time aggregation of community interactions into a single trust metric.
*   **Tiering System:** Categorization of users into stratified levels (e.g., Newcomer, Trusted, Mentor) with associated platform privileges.
*   **Reputation History Tracking:** Visual timeline showing point gains (upvotes, streaks, resolutions) and point losses (flags, unmet contracts).
*   **Metric Breakdown:** Transparent isolation of different scoring categories (e.g., Helpfulness vs. Accountability).
*   **AI Insight Generation:** CIA synthesis of recent reputation shifts.
*   **Moderation & Trust Transparency:** Visibility into accountability flags or reporting metrics.
*   **Actionable Guidance:** Prompts directing the user to activities that will positively impact their score.

## 6. Data Fields and Metrics
| Field Name | Data Source |
| :--- | :--- |
| Trust Score (Composite) | Computed BFF metric (Aggregated from `/community`, `/contracts`, `/feed`) |
| Current Tier Level | Computed BFF metric (Mapped from Trust Score) |
| Progress to Next Tier | Computed BFF metric (Point delta) |
| Helpfulness Metric | `/feed` and `/community` (Upvotes, accepted answers, likes) |
| Consistency Metric | `/activity-status` (Streaks, daily log completion) |
| Accountability Metric | `/contracts` (Commitments met vs. broken) |
| Trust Flags | `/admin/moderation` & `/admin/trust` (Reports, unresolved strikes) |
| Score Trend Graph | Historical BFF metric (Time-series of reputation points) |

## 7. Screen States
*   **Loading:** Skeleton states for the Trust Score hero and history chart.
*   **Empty (New User):** "You haven't generated a reputation score yet. Start by participating in the Community!"
*   **Error:** "Something went wrong fetching your score." (General BFF failure state).
*   **Syncing/Partial:** Real-time score updating (e.g., "+10 points added") without full page reload.
*   **Flagged/Warning:** Account under review; features temporarily limited by moderation rules.
*   **Disabled/Locked:** User lacks the premium entitlement required to view deep-dive reputation analytics.

## 8. Interactions, Gestures, Motion Intents
*   **Tap:** Selecting a specific metric (e.g., "Helpfulness") to open an explanatory tooltip or modal.
*   **Swipe (Horizontal):** Scrolling through the "Tiers & Privileges" ladder.
*   **Swipe (Horizontal):** Scrubbing the "Reputation History" timeline to see specific past events.
*   **Pull to Refresh:** Force re-fetch of the latest trust score from the BFF.
*   **Motion Intent:** Subtle animation or color shift on the Trust Score ring when a new positive score event is received from the backend.

## 9. Free vs. Premium Gating
*   **Free:** Basic Trust Score number, Current Tier name, and basic History (events list).
*   **Premium:** CIA Insights on reputation (AI analysis of *why* the score changed), granular metric breakdowns, predictive scoring ("You are 3 days away from leveling up"), and full trend graphs. (Represented via `/locked/[pageKey]` or `/upgrade` flow triggers).

## 10. Cross-Domain Hooks
*   **CIA (Coach):** The AI coach (`/ai-coach`) uses the Trust Score to tailor its tone and suggest community interventions ("Your helpfulness metric dropped this week, consider answering a question in the feed").
*   **Health & Wellbeing:** Correlation between "Consistency Metric" and `/wellbeing/habits` or `/activity-status` streaks.
*   **Accountability:** Direct tie-in to `/contracts` (e.g., breaking a behavioral contract with a pod negatively impacts the Trust Score).
*   **Knowledge Graph:** High-reputation users' inputs in `/wiki` or `/quick-notes` are weighted heavier in search algorithms.
