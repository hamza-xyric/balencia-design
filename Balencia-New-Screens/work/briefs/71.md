# FUNCTIONAL CONTENT BRIEF: Achievement Gallery

## 1. Purpose
The Achievement Gallery is the user’s visual trophy room, displaying all badges and milestones earned across all 9 life domains alongside progress toward undiscovered achievements. It closes the gamification feedback loop by reinforcing engagement breadth (spanning all life areas) and depth (progressive tiers and rarity), driving aspirational pull without using shaming or punishing mechanics.

## 2. Entry and Exit Paths
*   **Entry Paths (Arrives from):**
    *   Me Main screen via "Achievements" quick link card.
    *   RPG Character screen via achievement badge tap in Streak & Rewards section.
    *   Celebration Overlay via "view all achievements" link (upon earning a new badge).
    *   Push Notification tap (new achievement earned).
*   **Exit Paths:**
    *   Previous screen (via standard stack pop/back button).
    *   Domain Dashboards (via "go to [domain]" CTA in the Detail Bottom Sheet).
    *   Native OS Share Sheet (via "Share this achievement" in Detail Bottom Sheet).

## 3. Complete Information Architecture (Top-to-Bottom)
1.  **Navigation Header:** Back chevron + "Achievements" title.
2.  **Achievement Summary Strip (Hero Region):** A single composite card displaying at-a-glance progression metrics. 
    *   *Left zone:* Hero completion ring showing total earned vs. total possible.
    *   *Center zone:* KPI tile showing badges earned this month.
    *   *Right/Bottom zone:* Current streak status and forward momentum toward the next streak milestone.
    *   *Secondary Viz:* Compact donut chart showing domain coverage spread (earned badges distributed across the 9 life domains).
3.  **Domain Filter Chips:** Horizontal, single-select scrollable row to filter the grid (All, Fitness, Nutrition, Finance, Career, Relationships, Spirituality, Learning, Creativity, Wellbeing, General). Each chip includes a micro-arc showing the earned/total density for that specific domain.
4.  **Achievement Grid (Body Region):** Virtualized 2-column grid of achievement cards. Sorted: Earned (newest first) -> In-progress (highest % first) -> To-discover (closeness-to-unlock).
5.  **Achievement Detail Bottom Sheet (Modal):** Overlay triggered by tapping any card, displaying full badge details, requirements, tier ladder, and CTAs.

## 4. Every Feature and Capability
*   **Summary Progress Tracking:** View total completion percentage, monthly earned velocity (with month-over-month delta), and current streak momentum.
*   **Domain Filtering:** Isolate the gallery to view achievements belonging to one specific life domain.
*   **Grid Browsing:** Scroll through homogeneous achievement cards categorized by Earned, In-progress, and To-discover.
*   **Achievement Detail Viewing:** Tap any card to open a modal sheet containing the badge description, rarity tier, earned date, unlock requirements, or progress specifics.
*   **Tier Progression Tracking:** Visualize progression through multi-tier achievements (Bronze -> Silver -> Gold).
*   **Action/Deep-linking:** Navigate directly from a "To-discover" badge's requirements list straight to the relevant domain dashboard to begin working on it.
*   **Social Sharing:** Share earned achievements externally via the native OS share sheet.
*   **Motivation Adaptation:** The UI dynamically adapts based on user motivation levels (e.g., hiding locked/unearned tiles entirely for low-motivation users to celebrate existing progress).

## 7. Screen States
*   **Cold-start / Day 1:** Gauge is ghosted at 0% (faint full track visible, center "0"). KPI shows 0 with "—" delta. Streak bar text reads "Your streak starts today." Motivational banner appears above grid. All tiles are "to discover" (ghosted rarity-outlined silhouettes, not padlocks) sorted by closeness-to-unlock.
*   **Loading (Full & Partial):** Depth-preserving skeleton shimmer that matches layout (rings, bars, circles). Morphs seamlessly into drawn data. Partial load renders successful tiles normally; failed tiles temporarily render as skeleton before resolving to text-only cards.
*   **Empty (Filtered):** When a domain with zero badges is selected, the grid displays a centered domain icon and constructive messaging instead of a blank void.
*   **Error (List, Summary, Detail, Navigation, Share):** Graceful degradation. Cached data is prioritized. Error toasts/banners are specific, warm, non-alarming, and provide explicit recovery actions (e.g., "retry"). Individual badge image load failure falls back to a domain-colored circle.
*   **Offline Mode:** Cache renders normally. Banner appears below filter chips. Pull-to-refresh is visibly disabled with a calm explanatory reason.
*   **Low-Motivation Adaptation:** Undiscovered/locked badges are entirely hidden. The grid emphasizes currently "in-progress" (>75% complete) badges at the top with green progress rings to highlight attainability rather than gaps.

## 8. Interactions, Gestures, and Motion
*   **Gestures:**
    *   *Tap:* Achievement Card (opens detail sheet), Filter Chip (toggles filter, crossfades grid), "go to [domain]" (navigates away), Share row (opens native sheet).
    *   *Drag Down:* Dismisses the Detail Bottom Sheet.
    *   *Scroll:* Standard vertical scroll on the grid (virtualized).
    *   *Pull-to-Refresh:* Manually retries data fetch (disabled if offline).
*   **Motion Choreography (Draw-First Order):**
    *   On mount: Hero completion ring draws (0 -> final %) while the center number counts up.
    *   KPI tile numbers and streak momentum bar fill subsequently (left-to-right).
    *   Grid tiles cascade in with a staggered fade-up. In-progress micro-arcs fill *after* their host tile lands. Rarity glows fade in from 0 to calibrated alpha (warm arrival, no flashing).
    *   On filter change: Grid executes a fast crossfade.
    *   On new badge arrival (from Celebration Overlay): The specific tile scales from 0.8 -> 1.0 and emits a small particle burst colored identically to the badge's rarity tier (not generic gold).
*   **Haptics:** Light impact on card/chip press; medium impact on CTA button press.

## 9. Free vs Premium Gating
*There are no explicit paywalls, premium locks, or CIA-gated features native to the Achievement Gallery interface itself.* The gallery acts as a pure-gamification feedback loop accessible to all users. (Note: Data inputs that *generate* achievements, such as advanced progress photo AI analysis, may be premium elsewhere, but the gallery displays all data universally).
