## Dimension notes
1. Spec conformance — issue: `SafetyResourceCard` ("emergency contact and crisis rules") is under-built — no crisis-rules content and no call/text action at all; `Toggle` ("CIA intervene per trigger") is substituted with a full-size `BtnSuccess` primary-CTA button, not a toggle; the spec's "Contracts" tab has no matching content section (it was reframed as "Shared missions," and the spec's contract-fulfillment framing/honesty states have no home under any visible label). Everything else (ConsentCard, tabs, partner rows, CIAInsightCard, trigger row, bottom CTAs) is present and in spec order.
2. Canon fidelity — issue: the on-screen Partners/Contracts/Triggers switcher doesn't match the catalog's `SegmentedTabs` definition (glass pill track, active = `--surface-3` + orange label, plus tab ARIA semantics); `SafetyResourceCard` renders as `GlassCard` (glass) instead of the catalog-mandated solid `--surface-2` tier; the ConsentCard (a permissions/privacy gateway, not an AI insight) is the dominant visual element on the fold via a CIA-purple glow, inverting the spec's stated focal hierarchy where the consent banner is an "Operational layer" element and the accountability surface itself is the singular dominant focal moment. Glass-vs-solid split elsewhere and 60/30/10 role usage (orange = you/effort, green = domain dot, purple = CIA) is otherwise correct apart from the ConsentCard glow (see known-finding sightings).
3. Data honesty — ok apart from one gap: partner rows, shared-mission progress, CIA synthesis card and trigger row all read as real values with provenance (`Aisha Khan · 2 days`, `Run mission`, domain dots), no invented numbers. But the spec's three "Contracts" honesty states (signed terms/progress, sync-delayed, no-contract) aren't represented under any visible label since that section was retitled "Shared missions" with generic goal-progress framing instead.
4. Cross-cutting — issue: consent/revoke entry is present (ConsentCard + ConsentRail exposes Source/Retention/Export/Revoke/Delete) but the "Emergency contact" safety module is a fully static, non-interactive block — there is no tap target to actually reach the contact, which is the entire point of the canonical component. Touch targets otherwise ≥44px.
5. Composition craft — issue: the ConsentCard (shield icon, 9 total chips, purple glow) dominates the fold and crowds the partner list into secondary space, contrary to the spec's stated focal hierarchy; the Triggers row's oversized `BtnSuccess` will visually overpower the 40px icon + two-line text next to it; Shared-missions card double-encodes the same percentage via both a `ProgressRing` and a `ProgressBar` per mission, which is redundant. Otherwise hierarchy/spacing/order matches the spec's module sequence and reads cleanly above the fold.
6. Copy voice — ok apart from known "CIA" all-caps casing (A24-008, see below); sentence case, second person, no exclamations elsewhere, one Tiempos-italic emphasis word ("accountability").
