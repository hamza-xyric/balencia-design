### 2. Purpose
Delivers a sub-2-second premium brand handshake that covers cold-start initialization (auth checks, asset preloading). Creates emotional impact through a brand-mark reveal where a continuous stroke line draws itself around the Balencia symbol, setting the visual tone of the app.

### 3. Entry & exit
- **Entry paths:** System app launch (cold-start or warm-start).
- **Primary exit:** Crossfade to Motion Carousel (Screen 02) — *Condition: First-time user or no saved session.*
- **Secondary exit:** Crossfade to Home Screen (Screen 12) — *Condition: Returning user with a valid authenticated session.*
- **Timeout exit:** Forced transition after 4 seconds (network timeout) or 5 retries (auth fail) to fallback screens.

### 4. Layout anatomy
The screen is entirely passive and structurally minimalist, centering the brand mark over the mandatory atmosphere.

**Regions top-to-bottom:**
1. **Status Bar Zone:** Transparent, blends with `--bg-base`.
2. **Upper Spacer:** Flexible spacing.
3. **Brand Cluster:** The focal point, containing the Balencia symbol, the continuous stroke line reveal, and the wordmark, all nested within the warm atmosphere and CIA purple pool.
4. **Lower Spacer:** Flexible spacing (slightly larger than upper spacer to maintain optical center).
5. **System Safe Area:** Home indicator zone.

### 5. Components
This screen is intentionally devoid of standard UI components to preserve the premium brand handshake.
- **NEW: BrandClusterReveal** — A bespoke composite for this screen only. Contains the wordmark, Balencia symbol, and animated stroke line. Rationale: The catalog intentionally omits marketing/brand-specific assets to remain UI-focused.
- **NEW: GlobalAtmosphere** — Background layer applying the mandatory `--bg-base`, radial warm glow, and soft grain overlay. Rationale: Standardized app background logic that exists as a base style rather than a discrete component.
- **NEW: SystemSyncToast** — A quiet `.glass-pill` banner for offline state alerts. Rationale: The catalog's `OfflineBanner / SyncStatus` is intended for data staleness within app flows; this is a pre-auth system state requiring a more minimal implementation.

### 6. Visual treatment
- **Glass tiers:** None. No `.glass-card`, `.glass-frost`, or `.glass-pill` elements are rendered on this screen. The composition relies entirely on the atmospheric base.
- **Glow color & meaning:** 
  - **Warm radial glow** (top-center): Uses `#FF5E00` at 18% opacity to establish the base identity of the app (you/effort).
  - **Purple pool** (right-center): Uses `#7F24FF` to establish the presence of *CIA* (AI coach).
- **Background atmosphere:** `--bg-base` (`#0A0A0F`), overlaid with the mandatory soft warm radial glow and a 3% soft-light grain overlay.
- **Hero type moment:** The "Balencia." wordmark. Uses Neue Medium 500 at 34px, letter-spacing 0.5px, colored paper-100 (`#FEFAF3`). Sentence case, with the period acting as a grounded visual anchor.

### 7. Content & copy
- **Wordmark:** "Balencia."
- **Screen reader on mount:** "Balencia. Loading."
- **Auth retry tooltip (voice-over only):** "Checking your session… will retry"
- **Offline toast (returning user):** "No connection — continuing with saved data"
- **Offline toast (new user):** "No connection — starting fresh"
*(Note: CIA voice rules apply strictly to in-app coaching; the splash screen is purely systemic, using sentence case and omitting exclamation marks.)*

### 8. Data & honesty states
The Splash Screen contains **zero** data-bearing metrics, chart primitives, or user data fields. It is a pure pre-auth brand handshake. No fabricated numbers are used.
- **Timer Durations (System):** Total animation duration 1.8s. Network timeout threshold: 4s. Auth retry interval: 3s.
- **Auth State Token:** *Data Source: Local device cache / Auth server.* (Used invisibly to determine exit path).

### 9. All states
- **Default:** Warm dark background with atmospheric glow. Brand cluster centered. Stroke line draws itself organically.
- **Loading / Partial:** Not applicable (no data-driven content).
- **Skeleton:** Not applicable (no UI components to scaffold).
- **Empty:** Not applicable.
- **Error (Auth Fail):** Brand cluster remains fully visible. Symbol executes a subtle opacity pulse (80%–100% loop). No red colors or error icons shown.
- **Offline (No Network):** Holds splash, pulses symbol. After 4 seconds, forces transition based on cached state while showing the offline toast.
- **Success:** App launch sequence initializes silently in the background, triggering the primary or secondary exit crossfade.

### 10. Motion & interaction
- **Interaction:** None. The screen is entirely passive; no user input is accepted.
- **Physical easing:** `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (standard physical ease-out) for the stroke draw and wordmark fade.
- **Timing:**
  - **0.0s:** Symbol fades in. Hero glow begins fading in.
  - **0.2s–1.4s:** Stroke line draws itself (using `stroke-dashoffset`, never opacity-fading).
  - **1.2s:** Wordmark fades in and translates upwards (+8px to 0px).
  - **1.8s:** Exit crossfade initiates if auth/session resolves.
- **Glow behavior:** The atmospheric warm glow breathes subtly (4s loop, ease-in-out) to signify life.
- **Haptics:** None (passive screen).
- **Reduced-motion path:** Skips the 1.2s stroke draw completely; shows the final settled frame instantly, holding for 1.2s to preserve the total 1.8s timing contract.

### 11. Motivation-tier adaptation
