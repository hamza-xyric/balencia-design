# 2. Purpose
Provide a transparent, honest, and quantifiable view of a member's standing within the Balencia community. By isolating core metrics (consistency, helpfulness, accountability) and tying them directly to cross-pillar behavior, the screen connects community participation to whole-life growth.

# 3. Entry & exit
* **Entry Paths:**
  * Primary: `Me` Tab -> `Reputation` module.
  * Deep-link: Tapping a "Trust Score" badge on a user profile or leaderboard.
* **Exit Paths:**
  * Chevrons return to the referring screen.
  * Ghost buttons route to `/wiki` (Learn How to Earn), `/contracts` (Accountability), and `/people` (Connect with a Mentor).
  * **Correction to Brief:** The brief specified a "Community" tab. As defined in the Balencia Glass Canon, the native app architecture uses 4 tabs (`Today`, `CIA`, `Goals`, `Me`). Entries from social or community modules route through the `Me` profile or direct deep-links.

# 4. Layout anatomy
**Top-to-bottom regions:**
1. **TopBar:** Transparent header with back chevron, Title ("Reputation"), and privacy glyph.
2. **Hero Region:** `ReputationDial` displaying the composite score via `GlassCard`. 
3. **Breakdown Region:** `SolidCard` containing a `KPIRow` of 3 core contributing metrics.
4. **CIA Insight Region:** `CIAInsightCard` offering qualitative synthesis of social standing.
5. **History Region:** `SolidCard` with a `TrendChart` visualizing score changes over time.
6. **Tiers Region:** Horizontal scroller of `BadgeTile` and `LockedBadgeTile` showing progression.
7. **Navigation:** `GlassNavBar` floating globally.

# 9. All states
* **Default:** Fully populated real data states as outlined in the layout anatomy.
* **Skeleton:** Shimmer blocks (`--surface-3` base, 1.2s sweep) matching the geometry of the Hero Dial, the 3-column `KPIRow`, and the `TrendChart` ghost axis.
* **Empty (New User):** Replaces Hero & Breakdown. "You haven't generated a *reputation* score yet." Sub: "Start by participating in the community." Action: `BtnPrimary` ("Explore community").
* **Error:** `ErrorState` component. "Something went *wrong* fetching your score." Action: `BtnSecondary` ("Retry").
* **Success (Live Update):** Ring briefly pulses orange (250ms) and a ghost "+10" floats up and fades without full page reload.
* **Disabled (Premium Gate):** Deep analytics (Predictive scoring, full trend graphs) obscured by a `PaywallLock` blur overlay. Copy: "Unlock *predictive* scoring." + `BtnPrimary` ("Unlock with premium").

# 10. Motion & interaction
* **Feedback:** All interactive targets scale to `.98` with 150ms physical ease on press.
* **Hero Glow:** The `ReputationDial` (`glow-you`) uses a continuous 4s "breat" (opacity 0.8 to 1.0) to feel alive. 
* **Chart Scrub:** Horizontal swipe on the `TrendChart` scrubs a vertical reticle, popping a tabular-nums tooltip for that day's score (200ms slide-up).
* **Haptics:** Light impact haptic on chart reticle snap to milestone dot.
* **Reduced-motion:** Disables ring breathe and chart reticle spring. Reticle snaps instantly with a 150ms opacity crossfade.

# 11. Motivation-tier adaptation
* **Low Density:** Viewport shows only the Hero Score and CIA Insight card. User must scroll for metrics and history. Spacing increased to 32px rhythm.
* **Medium Density:** Default layout. Hero card, KPIRow, CIA card, and TrendChart visible in a single viewport scroll.
* **High Density:** Toggles an "Advanced Analytics" mode. CIA card compresses; TrendChart expands vertically; KPIRow expands to show exact upvote/strike counts via `Sheet` expansion on tap. 

# 12. Accessibility
* **Contrast:** Paper-100 (#FEFAF3) on `--surface-2` (#211008) exceeds AA+ (16.5:1). Caption text maintains 40-64% opacity but stays above 4.5:1 on dark backgrounds.
* **Targets:** All chevrons, glyphs, and tier tiles adhere to the 44px minimum target footprint. 
* **Screen-reader:** The reputation dial glyph exposes `accessibilityLabel="Trust score 924 out of 1000, trusted tier"`. The privacy glyph is labeled "Privacy and safety menu". Charts expose data points as ordered lists via VoiceOver/TalkBack.
