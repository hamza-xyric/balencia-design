# 14-goal-detail — Hi-Fi Design Spec

## 1. Header
- **Screen ID:** 14-goal-detail
- **Name:** Mission detail
- **Route(s) covered:** `/goals/:id`
- **Tab:** Goals
- **Source:** Functional Content Brief: Mission Detail
- **Batch:** 6

## 2. Purpose
To answer "am I on track?" at a glance, and "what is the complete picture?" via expandable progressive disclosures. The screen provides a single, focused view of a specific objective, grounding high-level metrics in immediate, physical next actions.

## 3. Entry & exit
- **Entry paths:**
  - Mission Board [13]: Stack push via mission card tap.
  - Home Screen [12]: Stack push via pinned mission card tap.
- **Exit paths:**
  - Back/swipe-edge: Stack pop to origin.
  - Edit icon: Modal present to Create Mission [15].
  - "Ask CIA" CTA: Tab switch to CIA Chat [09].
  - Domain tags & Cross-domain links: Stack push to Domain Dashboards [26-36] or Other Mission Details [14].

## 4. Layout anatomy
**Regions top-to-bottom:**
1. **Atmosphere & Nav:** Base warm gradient, floating glass TopBar with Pin/Edit controls.
2. **Hero Ring:** Large ProgressRing communicating overall completion.
3. **Mission Identity:** Center-aligned mission name, type badge, domain tags.
4. **KPI Row:** Compact solid tile showing Actions, Streak, XP.
5. **CIA Intelligence:** Contextual coaching card.
6. **Next Action:** Primary CTA card displaying the immediate next step.
7. **Expandable Sections:** Collapsible blocks for All Actions, Milestones, CIA Reasoning, Cross-Domain Links, and Progress Over Time.
8. **Ask CIA:** Persistent conversational escape hatch.

## 5. Components
- **TopBar** (transparent → scroll to glass): Back chevron, title, pin toggle, edit glyph.
- **ProgressRing** (hero): Large 6-8px stroke, tabular-nums center.
- **ChipDomainTag**: Tappable pills for associated life areas.
- **KPIRow**: 3 inline mini-stats (Actions, Streak, XP) sharing one SolidCard.
- **CIAInsightCard**: Purple-tinted glass for coaching text and CTA.
- **BtnPrimary**: Full-width CTA inside the Next Action card.
- **SectionHeader**: Overline title + trailing chevron/glyph for expandable blocks.
- **NEW: ActionCheckCard**: A SolidCard integrating a large tap-target checkbox, action title, and CTA. *Rationale: Bundling the immediate action and its completion mechanism into a single, tappable premium surface reduces cognitive friction.*
- **ExpandableList**: Standard accordion wrapper for deep-dive sections.
- **TrendChart**: Living line chart for XP progress and projection.

## 6. Visual treatment
- **Glass tiers:** 
  - TopBar: `.glass-pill` on scroll.
  - CIA Card: `.glass-card` (hero).
  - KPI Tile & Action Card: `SolidCard` (data/legibility priority).
- **Semantic inner-glow (one per card):**
  - **Hero Ring & Next Action Card:** `--glow-you` (#FF5E00) — Signifies active user effort, streaks, and immediate momentum.
  - **CIA Coaching Card:** `--glow-cia` (#7F24FF) — Signifies AI-derived insight and projected intelligence.
  - **Milestones Section:** `--glow-done` (#34A853) — Signifies completion and positive growth.
- **Background atmosphere:** Soft warm radial glow top-center (`rgba(255,94,0,.18)` to transparent 60%) over `--bg-base`, with a 3-4% soft-light grain overlay.
- **Hero type moment:** The Mission Name (e.g., "Hyrox Relay Prep") set in Neue Montreal Medium 500, 34px, paired with the single Tiempos italic emphasis word in the CIA card.

## 7. Content & copy
- **CIA Coaching (Standard):** "Strong momentum this week. Your tempo runs are paying off. Keep the *momentum*."
- **CIA Coaching (Stalled):** "No action logged in the past 7 days. Ready when you are — adjust the timeline, reduce scope, pause for now, or archive. Sometimes priorities *shift*." *(Note: Legacy "SIA" corrected to CIA per directive).*
- **Chain Extension:** "CIA suggests:" / "Add to my missions" / "Customize first" / "Not now" *(Overrides older accept/modify copy).*
- **Cold Start Copy:** "Every mission starts at 0%. Your first action is ready below. Begin your *whole* ascent."
- **Completion Copy:** "Mission accomplished. You stayed consistent for 24 days. Ready for a new *challenge*?"
- **Error/Offline Copy:** "Couldn't load this mission. Try pulling to refresh." / "You're offline. Your progress is saved locally and will sync when you're *back*."

## 8. Data & honesty states
*(Every metric ships with 3 states: real, low-confidence, honest-null.)*
- **Mission Completion %:**
  - *Real:* 72% (`via WHOOP`)
  - *Low-confidence:* 72% (`estimated · low confidence`)
  - *Honest-null:* "Not enough data yet — 3 more days to calibrate."
- **Action Count:**
  - *Real:* 12 (`you logged`)
  - *Low-confidence:* N/A (Exact count requires user log).
  - *Honest-null:* "No actions logged yet."
- **Streak (Days):**
  - *Real:* 8 (`you logged`)
  - *Low-confidence:* 8 (`estimated · low confidence`)

- **XP Earned:**
  - *Real:* 450 (`system calculated`)

  - *Honest-null:* "Calibrating — your XP trend builds here."

## 9. All states
- **Default:** Active progress, hero ring sweeps on mount, expandable sections collapsed.
- **Skeleton:** Shimmer blocks (`--surface-3` base, 1.2s sweep) replace text/rings. Chart axis renders with ghost line.
- **Empty (Cold Start):** 0% ring with ghosted track, future milestones dashed, stats zeroed with honest-null copy.
- **Error:** `ErrorState` SolidCard inline: "Couldn't load this mission. Try pulling to refresh." Ring retains last cached state.
- **Success (Completion):** Ring flips green (`--glow-done`), CelebrationOverlay triggers continuous-stroke line motif, Next Action card swaps to chain progression.
- **Disabled:** Next Action button shows spinner (label→spinner, width locked) if network is validating the tap.

## 10. Motion & interaction
- **Hero Ring:** Sweeps 0 → 72% on mount (520ms physical easing). Re-sweeps briefly upon action completion.
- **Next Action Completion:** Old card slides down/fades out; new card slides up (250ms, ease-in-out). 
- **Chart Drawing:** TrendChart solid orange line draws left-to-right; dashed purple CIA projection draws *after*. Long-press triggers crosshair scrub for weekly metrics.
- **Glow behavior:** `glow-you` and `glow-cia` utilize a subtle 4s "breathe" (opacity shift) to feel alive without distracting.
- **Haptics:** Light impact feedback on checkbox completion; medium impact on milestone unlock.
- **Reduced Motion:** Drawing/stagger animations bypass completely. Final static states render immediately. Glow "breathe" is static.

## 11. Motivation-tier adaptation
- **Low Density:** Expandable sections start fully collapsed. KPI Row uses plain numbers without trend arrows. Clean, quiet dashboard.
- **Medium Density:** (Default) Next Action card expanded. TrendChart loads inside the collapsed "Progress Over Time" header as a peekable mini-sparkline.
- **High Density:** All expandable sections open by default. Full TrendChart and Milestone timeline drawn on mount. Sub-goal StatBars fully exposed.

## 12. Accessibility
- **Contrast:** Paper-100 (#FEFAF3) on `--bg-base` (#0A0A0F) and `--surface-2` exceeds AA+ (16:1). 
- **Targets:** Checkbox, Edit, Back, and Pin controls mapped to 44x44px minimum invisible bounding boxes.
- **Screen-reader:** Glyph-only controls (Pin, Edit, Back) equipped with `accessibilityLabel` (e.g., "Pin to home screen", "Edit mission"). ProgressRing announces "72 percent complete".

## 13. Premium checklist
1. **Connects:** Yes. Cross-domain links show recursive tappable mission cards. CIA reasoning breaks down cross-pillar strategy.
2. **Honest:** Yes. Source spec contradictions resolved (SIA→CIA, old chain copy discarded). 3 metric states enforced.
3. **Premium:** Yes. Solid/glass separation respected. 1 hero type moment, 1 primary CTA.
4. **60/30/10:** Burnt orange (user/effort), Forest green (milestones), Royal purple (CIA) strict adherence.
5. **Semantic glow:** One glow per card, explicitly mapped to meaning.
6. **Glass tiers:** Data = solid, CIA = glass. Clean separation.
7. **Type:** Neue Montreal + one Tiempos italic word per CIA moment.
8. **Voice:** CIA persona. No exclamations. Sentence case.
9. **Shape/Spacing:** 8pt grid. 28px radii on cards.
10. **Motion:** 150-250ms physical easing. No linear transforms.
11. **A11y:** AA+ contrast. 44px targets met.
12. **Icons:** 2px rounded outline, filled active state.
13. **Bottom Nav:** Floating glass pill respected (hidden in wireframe for brevity, present in hi-fi).
14. **Data Viz:** Solid user line, dashed CIA projection, green milestones. No multi-color gradients.
