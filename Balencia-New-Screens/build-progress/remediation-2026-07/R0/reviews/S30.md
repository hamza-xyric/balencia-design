## Dimension notes
1. Spec conformance — issue: spec's Components list ("TrendChart: Spending line and projected trend") and Motion section ("Trend scrub: Long-press displays exact day amount and source") call for a spending trend chart, but no chart or scrub interaction exists anywhere in the build — it is also absent from the spec's own Final Composition ASCII, so either the module is missing or the spec's ASCII vs. Components/Motion sections are internally inconsistent and need reconciling.
2. Canon fidelity — issue: "Finance"/"Stress" domain-tag chips render in semantic `tone="cia"` (purple) rather than canon §4's per-domain hex colors, and a second glass+glow "hero-tier" card (Emergency Fund Mission) duplicates the Focal Hierarchy's singular-hero instruction; the glass/solid split is otherwise correct (SolidCard for KPI tiles/budgets/transactions, GlassCard reserved for the donut hero and the CIA note).
3. Data honesty — issue: the donut's 4th segment (37% ≈ $800, the single largest slice in the ring) has no legend row, category name, amount, or provenance chip; the three labeled categories only sum to $1,350 against the card's own stated "$2,150 Total." Everything else (KPI tiles, budget bars, transaction rows, provenance chips) is real+provenance per spec with no fabricated numbers.
4. Cross-cutting — ok apart from known/plan-wide items: `ComplianceFooter` (Source/Retention/Export/Revoke/Delete) satisfies §8 consent+revoke for third-party finance data; no crisis layer needed (finance, not wellbeing/mood); touch-target and chrome-semantics gaps are already scheduled (see sightings below).
5. Composition craft — issue: `CIAInsightCard` stacks domain chips + a full `Provenance` row + an "Ask CIA" action link (5 pill/action elements total) in one card, denser than the spec's own ASCII composition (`[finance] [stress] ask cia`) and partly redundant with the spec's stated long-press-to-sheet source-disclosure pattern.
6. Copy voice — ok apart from known Title-Case drift (S-04: "Adjust Or Roll," "Over By $50," "Net Change This Month") and CIA all-caps casing (A24-008); the CIA note sentence itself is sentence-case, no exclamation marks, coach-voiced observation.
