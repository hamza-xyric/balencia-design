# 9. All states
- **Default:** Fully populated UI showing an active, established streak with modifiers and history.
- **Skeleton:** Shimmer blocks (`--surface-3` base, 1.2s sweep) mirroring the layout geometry. The calendar skeleton renders as an empty grid; charts render as axis + ghost lines.
- **Empty (Day 1):** Hero shows "0". Aspirational framing. Calendar cells are blank. Milestones display the `HonestNullState` for future goals.
- **Error / Offline:** `OfflineBanner` pinned under TopBar. Leaderboard and Multipliers gracefully hide or show cached data. Freeze CTA (`BtnPrimary`) enters disabled state (40% opacity, no glow).
- **Success (Freeze Applied):** ModalOverlay dismisses. A `CelebrationOverlay` sweep quickly flashes the freeze icon, retires to a small `XPToast` (`streak protected`), and the calendar cell visually animates from missed to frozen (`❄`).
- **Disabled:** If offline, the "use freeze" button is disabled to prevent false sync assumptions.

# 10. Motion & interaction
- **Physical Easing:** strictly cubic-bezier `(0.4, 0.0, 0.2, 1)` for standard UI, `(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)` for slight overshoots on stat rings.
- **Feedback:** 150-250ms scale (.98) and glow brighten on all `BtnPrimary` and interactive cards.
- **Draw-First Choreography:** 
  - Hero `ProgressRing` sweeps to 63% while the "42" count ticks up numerically.
  - `TimelineAgenda` path draws itself top-to-bottom via `stroke-dashoffset`.
  - Calendar cells stagger appearance left-to-right, top-to-bottom (30ms delay each).
- **Glow Behavior:** The Hero `--glow-you` slowly breathes (4s ease infinite alternate) to signify active vitality.
- **Haptics:** Light selection feedback on calendar days; medium success haptic on hitting a milestone; heavy impact strictly reserved for `CelebrationOverlay` completion.
- **Reduced-motion path:** (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`): All sweeping and ticking instantly snap to final static values. Glows remain static. Line draws are replaced by solid 1px lines.
