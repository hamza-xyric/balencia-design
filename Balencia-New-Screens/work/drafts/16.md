## 9. All states
- **Default:** Radar renders 10 active axes. Creativity & Learning ghosted (dashed). Life Power score displayed. Sparkline shows 7-week path.
- **Skeleton:** Rings + 12 axes display with radial shimmer pulse. Domain bars animate width 0 → value. No spinners.
- **Empty (Cold-start):** Radar polygon faint at ~10% on all axes. Hub reads `Building your balance`. CIA shows onboarding note. Time range locked to Current.
- **Error (Total Network):** Radar shows grid rings/axes only. Domain list skeletons. CIA card hidden. Toast: `Couldn't load latest data.`
- **Error (Partial Domain):** Successful radar vertices remain; failed domains are dashed spokes. Domain rows show `Data unavailable — tap to refresh`.
- **Success (Comparison Toggle):** Ghost polygon draws itself (280ms). Growth marks green, decline marks amber. Glow holds, then fades.
- **Disabled:** Time selector segments `vs week` and `vs month` rendered at 40% opacity with lock glyph for Free users.

## 10. Motion & interaction
- **Physical Easing:** Standard cubic-bezier (never linear). 150–250ms feedback for taps.
- **Glow behavior:** Hero radar uses a slow "breathe" (4s ease) to feel alive. 
- **Haptics:** Light tick (medium impact) on comparison toggle switch and domain star-dot taps.
- **Comparison Toggle:** Radar vertices morph old → new values (280ms). Ghost polygon draws itself.
- **Accessibility (`prefers-reduced-motion`):** Radar stroke draws instantly. Sparkline renders static. Count-up logic bypassed; final numbers display immediately.
- **Gestures:** Tap domain axis → Stack push to dashboard. Tap Hub → Expand card in place. Pull-to-refresh → Retry fetch.

## 11. Motivation-tier adaptation
- **Low density:** Radar hub remains large, but the 12-domain list below the radar defaults to *collapsed*. Shows only the top 3 active domains. 0-goal domains are hidden entirely to reduce cognitive load. 
- **Medium density:** Default state. Top 3 domains shown, with a `Show all 12 domains` ghost action. Radar and sparkline remain visible.
- **High density:** Radar condenses vertically. The full 12-domain list expands by default. Includes supplementary analytical copy and weekly delta swatches on every bar. 

## 12. Accessibility
- **AA+ Contrast:** Primary text `paper-100` (#FEFAF3) on `--surface-2` (#211008). Muted text (paper-64%) strictly adheres to 4.5:1.
- **Targets:** All radar star-dots and axes are ≥44x44pt (expanded invisible bounding boxes). Segmented tabs and list rows meet 44pt minimum height.
- **Screen-reader:** 
  - Glyph-only toggle buttons include `aria-label="Toggle historical comparison"`. 
  - Radar chart exposes data via an invisible `CalendarStrip` fallback at the bottom of the scroll view (Announces: `"Fitness, 82 out of 99, up 4 points"`).
- **Non-shaming visuals:** Negative deltas use `stalled-amber` (#f59e0b) paired with a visible `▼` glyph. Never alarm-red.
