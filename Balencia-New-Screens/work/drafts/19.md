# 2. Purpose
A premium, read-only visual anchor that answers "how far have I come?" by mapping the user's whole-life progression into a unified RPG identity. It celebrates consistency without toxic gamification, accurately visualizing the "shape" of the user's life across 10 domains while upholding absolute data honesty.

# 5. Components
*Standard catalog components used: `TopBar`, `GlassCard`, `BentoGrid`, `SectionHeader`, `SolidCard`, `ProgressRing`, `KPIRow`, `ListRow`, `GlassNavBar`, `FABQuickLog`, `ChipProvenance`, `ChipDomainTag`, `BtnGhost`, `HonestNullState`, `SkeletonState`.*

- **NEW: `ConstellationRadarHero`**
  *Rationale:* A standard line chart fails to capture the spatial "shape" of multi-domain balance. This custom 10-axis radar renders the user's life as a unified geometry, with a tap-to-expand center sun-hub serving the dual purpose of focal anchor and data ingest for the "Life Power" calculation.
- **NEW: `MagnitudeRankChart`**
  *Rationale:* The brief asks for a "Ranked Magnitude Read" to compare domains 1:1. Standard bar charts lack the tabular tabular-nums precision and honest domain-tag coloring required by the canon.

# 6. Visual treatment
- **Background atmosphere:** `--bg-base` with the mandatory soft warm radial glow at top-center. A faint purple pool (`rgba(127, 36, 255, 0.08)`) is introduced behind the radar hero to establish CIA presence.
- **Glass tiers:** 
  - `.glass-card` (hero): Used for the Character Identity card and the Constellation Insight card.
  - `.glass-pill`: Used for the floating bottom navigation and the quick-log actions.
  - `--surface-2`: Used for all data-dense modules (Domain Skills gauges, Ranked Chart, Summary Row, Badge Wall, Mission History).
- **Semantic inner-glow (one per card, meaning-driven):**
  - **Character Identity Card:** `--glow-you` (Orange). *Meaning:* Anchors the user's active effort and current baseline XP.
  - **CIA Insight Card:** `--glow-cia` (Purple). *Meaning:* denotes the cross-domain intelligence engine reading the user's stats.
  - **Streak Card:** `--glow-done` (Green). *Meaning:* represents established consistency, recovery, and completion.
- **The one hero type moment:** The "Living Line" XP progress bar in the Identity Card. It starts as `--glow-you` (Burnt Orange) and sweeps into `--glow-done` (Forest Green) upon crossing the 80% threshold to the next level, visually climaxing during the Level-up Celebration temporal state.

# 9. All states
- **Default:** Fully populated dashboard, scroll-enabled, radar polygon drawn.
- **Skeleton:** Depth-preserving `SkeletonState`. Radar rings/spokes remain visible with a radial shimmer. The Identity card and `KPIRow` display axis blocks and ghost lines. Copy: `Building your character — one moment.`
- **Empty (Cold-start / Day 1):** Identity card is Lv.1. Radar shows a faint full polygon with dashed spokes. All domains are Lv.0/Stat 0, visibly dimmed/ghosted but tappable. Mission history shows the prompt text and a genesis `+10 XP Welcome` item.
- **Error (Per-section failure):** Cached data retained where possible. A quiet network banner sits below the TopBar: `Couldn't load mission history` with an inline `try again` ghost button. Charts fall back to cached axes.
- **Success (Level-up Celebration):** Temporal overlay. The "Living Line" completes its draw to green, the avatar glow pulses green, and a restrained continuous-stroke particle burst occurs. Respects reduced-motion (fade-only).
- **Disabled (Offline):** Cached data is retained. `FABQuickLog` and tap-interactions on mission rows are dimmed at 40% opacity. Pull-to-refresh is disabled. `OfflineBanner` reads: `offline — showing last sync 2h ago`.

# 10. Motion & interaction
- **Physical easing:** Standard 150-250ms ease-out for UI feedback (taps, sheet transitions). 
- **Glow behavior:** Radar hub pulses softly (4s breathe). Tapping a domain spoke brightens its specific `--glow-you` bleed and dims the polygon outline locally to highlight the selection.
- **Interactions:** 
  - Tap radar sun-hub: Expands the ranked magnitude chart in-place using a 250ms physical spring.
  - Long-press radar spoke: Scrubs the domain stat (haptic tick + `IntelligenceTimeline` caption: `checking [Domain] stats…`).
- **Haptics:** Light tick on domain tap; medium impact on level-up success state.
- **Reduced-motion path:** Radar polygon and XP bar render fully formed instantly (opacity fade in). Particle bursts are disabled. Glow breathing is static.

# 11. Motivation-tier adaptation
- **Low density (Minimalist):** Radar hero centers the screen. The Domain Skills grid, Rank Chart, and Badge wall are hidden behind a `BtnSecondary` ("show details"). Focuses entirely on identity and the CIA insight.
- **Medium density (Default):** Exactly as laid out in the ASCII wireframe. Balance of identity, shape, and linear history.
- **High density (Power user):** The 3x4 BentoGrid transitions to a 4x3 grid. The center radar shrinks slightly to make room for inline sub-stats rendered directly under each domain gauge, negating the need to open the drill-down sheet for basic viewing.
