### 2. Purpose
To provide a high-speed, intelligent command line that collapses Balencia’s 9 life domains and 73 screens into a single interface. It deep-links users to specific content while the CIA engine injects predictive, behavioral intelligence directly into the search flow, prioritizing what matters right now.

### 3. Entry & exit
*Note: Pull-down to open is removed. Modal overlays in native mobile must intercept gestures cleanly, and a pull-down on the Today tab conflicts with scroll/refresh dynamics. Search is opened via tap to guarantee 100% reliability.*
- **Entry Paths:** Tap the search icon on the TopBar, tap the search bar in Settings, or tap the "search for that" deep-link inside CIA Chat.
- **Exit Paths:** Tap a result row (stack-pushes to destination), tap "Cancel", swipe down from top, or hardware back (Android).

### 4. Layout anatomy & wireframe
**Regions (top-to-bottom):**
1. **Search Header:** Auto-focused input field with inline clear button and "Cancel" escape hatch.
2. **Category Filter Chips:** Horizontal scroller for scoping results (All, Goals, Habits, etc.).
3. **CIA Suggestion Row:** Dynamic AI prediction card (appears after 2+ characters).
4. **Results/Recent Body:** Grouped `ListRow` items based on query state.
5. **Bottom Nav:** Global navigation (fades out when search is active to preserve focus).

### 5. Components
*   **GlassPillInput** (Variant: search): Autofocused, clear button.
*   **SegmentedTabs** (Variant: chips): Used for horizontal category filtering.
*   **CIAInsightCard**: Top AI prediction row with cross-domain tags.
*   **SolidCard**: Grouping container for data-dense result categories.
*   **ListRow**: Base unit for flat result items.
*   **ChipProvenance**: Attached to metrics to show data origin.
*   **ChipDomainTag**: Inline domain labels (Fitness, Nutrition).
*   **NEW: ResultListCard:** A SolidCard variant strictly for grouping ListRows of varying heights in search results. *Rationale: The catalog lacks a structured container for mixed-type query results that handles dynamic height and internal hairline dividers cleanly.*

### 6. Visual treatment
- **Glass tiers:** 
    - Header Input: `.glass-pill` 
    - AI Insight: `.glass-card` (reserved for hero/AI elements)
    - Result Groupings: `SolidCard` (--surface-2). *Rationale: Search results are highly data-dense and text-heavy. Using glass here would create visual noise and hurt legibility.*
- **Semantic inner-glow:** 
    - `CIAInsightCard` uses `--glow-cia` (Royal Purple `#7F24FF`). Meaning: Predicted by AI, cross-domain intelligence.
    - Zero decorative glows on result cards to maintain honest, blunt utility.
- **Background atmosphere:** The global warm dark base (`#0A0A0F`) with the top-center radial orange glow. When the CIA suggestion card appears, a subtle purple pool (`rgba(127, 36, 255, 0.15)`) fades in behind it to anchor the AI presence.
- **Hero type moment:** The placeholder text *"everything"* inside the search bar renders in Tiempos Medium italic when empty, smoothly transitioning back to NM Regular upon typing.

### 8. Data & honesty states
Every metric displayed in the search results renders through the honesty invariant:
1.  **Goal Progress (Real):** `60%` · Chip: `via Goals`
2.  **Goal Progress (Low-confidence):** `~60%` (muted 64% opacity) · Chip: `estimated · low confidence`
3.  **Goal Progress (Honest-null):** `Start tracking to see progress` (Text replaces metric) · Chip: `no data yet`
4.  **Habit Streak (Real):** `12 day streak` · Chip: `via Habits`
5.  **Habit Streak (Honest-null):** `Not enough data yet` · Chip: `no data yet`
6.  **Recipe Calories (Real):** `450 cal` · Chip: `via server`
7.  **Community Members (Real):** `120 members` · Chip: `via Community`
8.  **Community Members (Offline/Honest-null):** Hidden entirely from local index when offline.

### 9. All states
- **Default / Empty-Query:** Shows up to 10 recent searches or the Day-1 prompt.
- **Typing / Live Suggestions:** 300ms debounce triggers. CIA card slides in if confidence > 0.7. Grouped `SolidCard` results fade in below.
- **Skeleton:** Inline loading text `Searching for 'query'...` with 4 shimmer skeleton `ListRow` blocks matching target geometry.
- **Empty / No Results:** Centers a quiet search glyph with: `No results for 'query'. Try a different spelling or search term.` Filter chips disable (40% opacity).
- **Error / API Failure:** Quiet inline banner at top: `Some results may be missing. Pull down to retry.` Server-dependent categories (Community, Recipes) hide gracefully; local categories remain visible.
- **Offline:** Informational glass-pill banner: `Searching offline — some results may be outdated.`

### 10. Motion & interaction
- **Physical easing:** Overlay slides up in 280ms (ease-out-soft). 
- **Feedback:** Result rows scale to `.98` and background shifts to `--surface-3` on press (150ms).
- **Glow behavior:** The `--glow-cia` on the CIAInsightCard breathes (4s ease-in-out) to signify active AI processing.
- **Haptics:** Light tower tap when CIA prediction appears. Medium impact tap on successful deep-link navigation.
- **Reduced-motion path:** Slide-up overlay drops to 0ms fade. CIA glow breathing freezes. Staggered result fade-ins render instantly.

### 12. Accessibility

- **Screen-reader:** Glyph-only clear buttons and domain icons possess explicit `aria-labels` (e.g., "Clear search", "Fitness domain").
