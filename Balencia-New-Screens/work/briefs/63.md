# Functional Content Brief: Energy Tracking

## 1. Purpose
The Energy Tracking screen serves as the user's personal energy observatory, enabling frictionless logging of energy levels throughout the day. It leverages this data to detect chronotypes, identify peak productive windows, and reveal cross-domain correlations, answering: "How is my energy right now, and what can I do about it?"

## 2. Entry and Exit Paths
**Entry Paths (Stack Push):**
*   **Explore Screen [18]:** Via the "Energy Tracking" module card.
*   **Home Screen [12]:** Via the "log your energy" quick action card.
*   **CIA Chat [09]:** Via deep-link (e.g., "let's check your energy patterns").
*   **Wellbeing Domain Dashboard:** Via the "energy" section tap.

**Exit Paths:**
*   **Back Button / iOS Swipe:** Stack pop to Explore or previous screen.
*   **CIA Chat [09]:** Tab switch (with energy context pre-loaded) via Coaching Note, Peak Hours card, or "ask CIA more" chip.
*   **Fitness Dashboard [26]:** Stack push via Correlation row ("workout").
*   **Nutrition Dashboard [28]:** Stack push via Correlation row ("meals").
*   **Stress [52] / Sleep:** Stack push via Correlation row ("stress" / "sleep").

## 3. Complete Information Architecture
**Scroll Behavior:** Vertical ScrollView (mixed content, ~1200-1400pt). Tab bar visible.

**Top-to-Bottom Stack:**
1.  **Domain Header (Sticky):** Back button, Screen Title, Domain Accent Line, RPG Skill Badge.
2.  **CIA Coaching Note:** Observational insight preamble.
3.  **Current Energy Display:** The visual hero featuring the ArcGauge and nested ChargeMeter.
4.  **Quick Log Card:** Primary action zone containing slider, context chips, text input, and submit CTA.
5.  **Today’s Energy Timeline:** Sparkline chart visualizing today's intra-day energy arc.
6.  **Energy Trend Section:** Segmented control (7d/14d/30d) and multi-day trend area chart.
7.  **Energy × Time of Day:** ScatterPlot mapping individual logs across hours.
8.  **Peak Hours Card:** AI-detected high/low energy windows with intensity bars.
9.  **Chronotype Badge:** User's identified biological rhythm profile.
10. **Correlations Card:** Horizontal impact bars detailing cross-domain effects.
11. **CIA Insight Card:** Prescriptive, deeper coaching insight.

## 4. Features and Capabilities
*   **Sub-5-Second Quick Logging:** Discrete 1-10 slider with floating value label, single-select auto-detected context tags, and optional notes.
*   **Energy Charge Visualization:** Open 240° ArcGauge dial showing current energy level, supported by instrument ticks and a nested segmented charge reserve ladder.
*   **Intra-Day Arc Tracking:** Curved 7-point sparkline showing energy fluctuations with milestone dots and interactive tooltips.
*   **Multi-Day Pattern Analysis:** Time-segmented (7d/14d/30d) trend chart with horizontal scrubbing for exact values.
*   **Time-of-Day Cloud Mapping:** Scatter plot clustering logs by hour and energy level to visualize rhythm reliability.
*   **Optimal Window Detection:** AI-driven detection of peak productive hours and low-energy dips, complete with scheduling recommendations.
*   **Chronotype Identification:** Automatic assignment of biological rhythm profiles based on logging patterns.
*   **Cross-Domain Impact Analysis:** AI-computed correlations linking energy levels to external factors (sleep, meals, workouts, stress).
*   **Offline Support & Rate Limiting:** Local queueing of logs when offline and submission cooldowns to prevent spam.

## 6. Data Fields and Metrics
*   **Current Energy Value (1-10):** `energyTracking.current.value`
*   **Current Context Tag:** `energyTracking.current.context`
*   **Current Note:** `energyTracking.current.note`
*   **Time since last log:** Derived from `energy_logs.logged_at`
*   **Slider Value (1-10):** Local UI state.
*   **Selected Context Tag:** Local UI state (auto-derived from device time or proximity to meal/workout logs).
*   **Today's Average Energy:** Aggregated `energy_logs` for the current day.
*   **Today's Log Count:** Count of `energy_logs` for the current day.
*   **Trend Average (7d/14d/30d):** Aggregated `energy_logs` grouped by time-of-day.
*   **Peak Hours / Low Hours:** `timing_profile.peak_hours`, `timing_profile.low_hours`
*   **Chronotype:** `timing_profile.chronotype`
*   **Correlation Impacts (+/-):** `energyTracking.correlations.impact` / `.positive`

## 7. Screen States
*   **Cold-Start / Day-1:** Hero gauge shows `--` with ghosted empty track. Sparkline is a flat dashed line. Trend, ScatterPlot, Peak Hours, Chronotype, and Correlations sections display constructive "needs more data" skeletons or are hidden entirely.
*   **No Logs Today (Established User):** Hero gauge shows `--`. Quick Log card pulses. History/trend sections remain fully populated with prior data.
*   **Loading:** Skeletons with radial/linear shimmer morph into drawn data. CIA copy reads: `"CIA is reading your week — one moment."`
*   **Empty / Partial:** Dashed lines and ghosted UI represent missing data without implying a "zero" energy level.
*   **Error:** Specific error toasts/banners with retry options. CTA flashes red on submission failure. Charts cache old data or display failure text.
*   **Offline:** Cached data retained. Banners inform the user; actions are dimmed; logs queue locally.
*   **Success:** Log CTA text changes to "logged" with a checkmark and green glow flash. ArcGauge re-sweeps to the new value and timeline updates instantly.
*   **Low Motivation Mode:** Screen truncates to essential logging actions only, hiding complex historical analytics.
*   **High Motivation Mode:** Adds an expanded "Energy Analytics" section with day-of-week breakdowns, streaks, and expanded CIA confidence bands.

## 8. Interactions, Gestures, and Motion Intents
*   **Screen Enter/Exit:** Standard iOS stack push/pop slide (280ms).
*   **Energy Slider:** Drag to adjust. Thumb scales (1.2x), floating value label appears. Haptic light impact on integer snaps.
*   **Context Chips:** Tap to single-select. Press animation (scale 0.97) + light haptic.
*   **Log CTA:** Press animation (scale 0.97) + light haptic. Success state triggers 600ms green glow and 520ms gauge re-sweep.
*   **Trend Chart Scrub:** Horizontal drag over the chart reveals an exact value tooltip at the finger position.
*   **Chart Enter Animations:** ArcGauge fills 0→value (520ms); TrendChart line draws left-to-right (1200ms); ScatterPlot dots stagger in (40ms delay per dot); Correlation bars rise (60ms stagger).
*   **Tooltips:** Tap dots on Sparkline/ScatterPlot for fade-in tooltips. Tap elsewhere to dismiss.
