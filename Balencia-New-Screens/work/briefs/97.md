# Functional Content Brief: 97-Plans Library (/plans)

## 1. Purpose
Serves as the centralized hub for members to manage, track, and execute structured commitments and plans generated by the CIA (Coach). It bridges actionable goals across life areas—such as wellbeing, finance, and career—with accountability and progress tracking.

## 2. Entry and Exit Paths
*Inferred from related app IA (Goals, Wellbeing, Dashboard)*
*   **Entry Paths:**
    *   Global member navigation menu (primary entry, though historically "not visible in primary nav" on legacy web, it is promoted to the main app shell in the redesign).
    *   Deep-link from the "Today" Dashboard (`/dashboard`) timeline or quick actions.
    *   Deep-link from the CIA (`/ai-coach`) chat when a new plan is generated and saved.
    *   Deep-link from Goals (`/goals`) or Accountability Contracts (`/contracts`).
*   **Exit Paths:**
    *   Tapping a plan card to view its detailed execution tracker.
    *   "Back" gesture to return to the previous screen (Dashboard, Goals, etc.).
    *   Global bottom navigation to other domains (Community, Profile).

## 3. Complete Information Architecture
*Inferred mobile IA based on product capabilities.*

*   **Screen Header:**
    *   Title: "Plans"
    *   Action: Create/Generate New Plan (+)
*   **Contextual AI Strip:**
    *   CIA Insight Banner (Active plan progress summary or next task suggestion).
*   **Segmented Control (Filter Bar):**
    *   All / Active / Completed / Paused
*   **Content Scroller (Plan List):**
    *   *Plan Card Module 1..N*
        *   Plan Name & Associated Domain Icon (Fitness, Career, Finance, etc.)
        *   Status Badge (Active, Completed)
        *   Progress Indicator (Linear or circular)
        *   Next Milestone / Task Due Date
        *   Quick Actions (Edit, Mark Complete)
*   **Empty State Module:**
    *   Placeholder graphic and prompt to ask CIA to generate a plan.
*   **Modals & Sheets (Invoked):**
    *   Plan Creation/Detail Sheet (Tasks, milestones, reminders).
    *   Delete Confirmation Dialog.

## 4. Features and Capabilities
*   **Plan Generation & Sourcing:** Ability to view plans created directly by the CIA based on conversational context or goal creation. *(Inferred from CIA capabilities)*
*   **Plan Tracking:** Visual tracking of plan status (Active, Paused, Completed).
*   **Task Management:** View and edit journey items/tasks associated with a specific plan.
*   **Reminders & Accountability:** View due dates for upcoming plan milestones. Integration with Accountability Contracts (`/contracts`) for external commitment.
*   **Filtering & Sorting:** Filter the plan library by status or life area domain.
*   **Deep Linking:** Tap-to-action from CIA recommendations directly to the specific plan.

*   **Associated Commitments:** Links to `/contracts` (Source: Accountability module)

## 7. Screen States
*   **Empty State:** "No plans yet. Ask your CIA to generate a plan" (with a deep link to the CIA chat).
*   **Loading State:** Skeleton placeholders for plan cards during initial data fetch.
*   **Error State:** Generic error message with "Retry" button if plan synchronization fails.
*   **Disabled/Locked State:** Premium plan templates or features within a plan locked behind the subscription paywall.
*   **Success State:** Visual confirmation (e.g., confetti or checkmark) when a plan is marked "Completed".

## 8. Interactions, Gestures, Motion
*   **Tap:** Select a plan card to open the detail view.
*   **Swipe (Left):** Reveal quick actions on a plan card (Archive, Delete).
*   **Swipe (Right):** Mark the next milestone/task as complete.
*   **Pull to Refresh:** Force sync plan data and progress across life domains.
*   **Long Press:** Open context menu to edit, pause, or share a plan.
*   **Motion Intent:** Smooth expansion animation when transitioning from the plan list card to the full-screen plan detail.

## 9. Free vs Premium Gating
*   **Free Tier:** Basic plan creation, standard task tracking, limited active plans (e.g., max 3 active plans).
*   **Premium Tier:** Unlimited plan generation by CIA, advanced analytics on plan adherence, and integration with complex Accountability Contracts.

## 10. Cross-Domain Hooks
*   **CIA Intelligence (AI Insights):** The CIA actively monitors plan progress and surfaces "Obstacles" or "Reconnections" on the Today Dashboard (`/dashboard`). The CIA may suggest modifying a plan based on stale data or missed milestones.
*   **Goals Linkage:** Every plan is intrinsically linked to the Goals (`/goals`) module; completing a plan contributes to overarching metric goals.
*   **Finance / Wellbeing / Career:** Plans are tagged by domain. A "Finance" plan will sync tasks to the Money Map (`/money-map`), while a "Wellbeing" plan syncs tasks to the Wellbeing hub (`/wellbeing`) schedule.
*   **Accountability:** Direct bridge to the Contracts module (`/contracts`) to assign a commitment or penalty to a specific plan's success.
