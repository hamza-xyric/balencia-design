# Screen 48: Intelligence Dashboard

- **Screen ID:** 48
- **Name:** Intelligence dashboard

## 2. Purpose
To provide a premium, non-shaming analytical command center that synthesizes cross-domain life data into a unified health score. It surfaces AI-driven insights, contradictions, and behavioral correlations to build user trust and prompt positive, whole-life action.

*Design Correction Note:* The entire dashboard is gated for Premium users. The full screen renders behind a `PaywallLock` for free users to maintain the "funded product, no dead ends" premium invariant.

## 4. Layout anatomy
The screen is a vertically scrolling `ScrollView` (390x844pt frame). Regions top-to-bottom:
1. **TopBar:** Sticky, transparent over atmosphere, gains `.glass-pill` on scroll.
2. **Hero Card:** Daily score gauge, trend delta, pillar sparklines, timestamp.
3. **Active Contradictions:** Structural eyebrow, stacked interactive alert rows.
4. **Correlations:** Matrix hero + ranked coaching rows.
5. **Trend Chart:** Time-selector (7/14/30d) + line chart with projection.
6. **Best Day Formula:** Factor checklist + progress bar.
7. **Weekly Report:** Collapsed/expandable domain metrics.
8. **Predictions:** Predicted score gauge + basis.
9. **Recent Insights:** AI insight cards with feedback loops.
10. **Knowledge Graph:** Navigational link card.

## 6. Visual treatment
- **Glass tiers:** `GlassCard` is used selectively for primary immersive containers (Hero, Contradictions, Best Day, Insights). `SolidCard` (`--surface-2`) wraps data-dense, fine-detail modules (Correlation Matrix, Trend Chart, Weekly Report Grid) to guarantee legibility over atmosphere.
- **Semantic glows (one per card, meaning-driven):**
  - Hero Card: `--glow-you` (#FF5E00) — represents *your* current state and active effort.
  - Contradiction Card: `--glow-cia` (#7F24FF) — represents *CIA* identifying an analytical mismatch.
  - Best Day Card: `--glow-done` (#34A853) — represents positive *growth* and completion.
- **Background atmosphere:** Soft warm radial glow top-center (`radial-gradient(90% 60% at 50% -10%, rgba(255,94,0,.18), transparent 60%)` over `--bg-base` #0A0A0F) with a 4% grain overlay (soft-light). The Hero card breathes 1.0 to 1.02 scale over 4s.
- **Hero type moment:** The global composite score ("87") in Display Neue Medium 52pt, emphasizing the unification of complex data into a quiet, confident metric.

## 9. All states
- **Default:** Data populated, ambient breathing glow on Hero card, background atmosphere visible.
- **Skeleton:** Shimmer blocks matching layout. ProgressRing shows full faint track with sweeping radial shimmer. Trend chart axis visible with a ghost-line draw-in.
- **Empty / Partial (Day 1-3):** Sparklines hidden. Matrix and trends show "analyzing" honest-null copy. Contradictions hidden (absence = positive state, no empty UI noise).
- **Error:** Inline section degradation. `GlassCard` or `SolidCard` renders normal borders but interior swaps to ErrorState component: `Couldn't load [section name] — tap to retry`. Rest of dashboard remains perfectly functional.
- **Success (Action Resolved):** Tapping "resolve" triggers a 150ms inline spinner, followed by a brief green sweep across the card border (`glow-done`), then the card collapses out of the stack.
- **Disabled:** N/A. (Free users see `PaywallLock`; premium features cannot be manually disabled).

## 10. Motion & interaction
- **Easing & Durations:** Physical easing (ease-out) universally. Cell/UI feedback = 150-250ms. Entrance stagger = 80ms.
- **Glow behavior:** Hero card breathes continuously (`box-shadow` alpha 0.8 to 1.0, 4s loop). Glow brightens instantly on press-down (150ms).
- **Haptics:** Light impact tick on Trend Chart press-and-hold scrubbing. Success impact on checking a "Best Day" factor.
- **Interactions:** Matrix cells scale 0.95 on tap. Contradiction cards support swipe-left to dismiss (drag 80px past threshold = collapse). Thumbs-up/down buttons scale 1.0 to 1.3 and settle.
- **Reduced Motion (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`):** Entrance count-up and line-draw animations fire instantly at final state. Hero glow freezes at static alpha. Confetti particles disabled (replaced by static gold checkmark overlay on the Best Day list).

## 11. Motivation-tier adaptation
- **Low density:** View collapses to Hero card (sparklines hidden) + Top 3 Best Day factors. Matrix, Correlations, and Predictions are hidden.
- **Medium density (Default):** Hero + Contradictions + 7d Trend + Best Day formula + collapsed Weekly report.
- **High density:** All sections fully expanded. 30d trend enabled by default. Matrix adds a "Deep Patterns" quadrant surfacing second-order cross-domain correlations (e.g., `When you meditate AND sleep 7+ hours, your fitness score is 25% higher`).

## 12. Accessibility
- **AA+ contrast:** Paper-100 (#FEFAF3) over warm dark surfaces (#0A0A0F, #211008) exceeds WCAG AA. Overline utilizes Paper-64% for structural hierarchy while maintaining legibility.
- **44px targets:** All chips (`resolve`, `thumbs`), TopBar chevrons, and SegmentedTabs honor 44pt minimum vertical/horizontal touch zones.
- **Screen-reader:** Glyph-only buttons (`[...]`, `<`, `✓`) include dynamically assigned `aria-labels`. ProgressRing and TrendChart encapsulate grouped data summaries (e.g., "Intelligence score 87, up 3 points from yesterday").
