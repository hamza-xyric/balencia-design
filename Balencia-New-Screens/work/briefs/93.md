# Functional Content Brief: 93-mood-trends (Longitudinal Mood Tracking)

## 1. Purpose
Displays historical mood data through interactive longitudinal charts and AI-generated narratives. It helps users identify emotional patterns, triggers, and correlations over time to support self-awareness and mental wellbeing.

## 2. Entry and Exit Paths
**Entry Paths:**
*   **Primary:** Wellbeing Hub (`/wellbeing`) -> "Mood" module.
*   **Secondary:** Today Dashboard (`/dashboard`) -> Quick Action "Track Mood" -> "View Trends".
*   **Tertiary:** CIA Coach conversational deep-link ("View your mood trends").

**Exit Paths:**
*   Back arrow/hardware back -> Returns to Wellbeing Hub.
*   Bottom navigation -> Switches app domains.
*   CTA Buttons -> Routes to Mood Logging (`/wellbeing/emotional-checkin`), Insights (`/wellbeing/insights`), or CIA Coach (`/ai-coach`).
*   Unlocked overlay -> Closes modal/sheet to return to previous screen.

## 4. Features & Capabilities
*   **Longitudinal Graphing:** Visual plotting of mood scores over variable time horizons (7D, 30D, 90D, 1Y).
*   **Detailed Reflection Access:** Ability to scroll through historical mood entries and view the contextual notes/journal entries tied to specific data points.
*   **AI Pattern Analysis:** Automated scanning of mood variance to surface temporal trends (e.g., "Your mood dips on Tuesday afternoons").
*   **Cross-Factor Correlation Engine:** Cross-references mood data against other logged life areas to identify statistical or observed triggers.
*   **Quick Capture:** Instant routing to the daily emotional check-in flow.
*   **CIA Conversational Handoff:** One-tap export of the current mood graph context into the CIA Coach chat to "discuss" the data.

## 6. Data Fields & Metrics
| Field | Description | Data Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mood Score** | Quantitative value representing emotional state (e.g., 1-10 or scale). | User Input (`/wellbeing/emotional-checkin`) |
| **Reflections** | Qualitative text notes attached to a mood log. | User Input (Journal/Check-in) |
| **Time Range Filter** | The active chronological window. | System/UI State |
| **Correlation Factors** | Tags/metrics showing relationships (e.g., low sleep + low mood). | Derived via CIA (Cross-referencing `/whoop`, `/nutrition`, `/wellbeing/stress`) |

## 7. Screen States
*   **Loading/Syncing:** Skeleton states for charts; pulsing placeholders for CIA insight generation.
*   **Empty/Zero Data:** "Log your mood to unlock CIA insights." (Graph hidden or shown as flat baseline).
*   **Partial Data:** Chart renders sparsely; dashed lines or neutral markers used for days with missing check-ins.
*   **Locked/Paywall (Free users):** Heatmap/graph blurred with a Premium upgrade CTA overlay.
*   **Error:** "Failed to sync mood data. Retry." (Inferred standard network error).
*   **Success:** Chart smoothly animates/plots upon new data entry or time-range switch.

## 8. Interactions, Gestures & Motion
*   **Time Segmented Control:** Tap to instantly re-render the chart with easing animations.
*   **Chart Scrubbing:** Drag finger horizontally across the chart line to reveal a tooltip showing the exact date and mood score/reflection preview.
*   **Drill-In Tap:** Tap a specific data node on the chart to deep-link to that day's journal/check-in detail.
*   **Horizontal Scroll:** Swipe left/right through the "History" list modules.
*   **CIA Launcher:** Long-press or tap the floating CIA icon to initiate a context-aware conversation ("Let's talk about my recent mood trends").

## 9. Free vs. Premium Gating
*   **Free Tier:** Access to basic 7-Day and 30-Day historical views. Basic mood logging.
*   **Premium Tier:** Unlocks 90-Day, 1-Year trends, advanced correlation factors, longitudinal pattern recognition, and the "Discuss with CIA" deep-analysis handoff.

## 10. Cross-Domain Hooks
*   **CIA (Coach):** Direct hook to pass trend context to the AI.
*   **Health Data (`/whoop` / Health Connections):** Correlation between mood and Sleep/Recovery scores.
*   **Wellbeing (`/wellbeing/stress` & `/wellbeing/energy`):** Trivariate correlation to explain emotional dips.
*   **Nutrition (`/nutrition`):** Links between meal timing/dietary scores and mood fluctuations.
