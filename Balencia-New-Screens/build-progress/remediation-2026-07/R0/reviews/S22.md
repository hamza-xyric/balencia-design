## Dimension notes
1. Spec conformance — issue: Wearables & Fitness ships only 4 `IntegrationCard`s (WHOOP, Apple Health, Fitbit, Garmin) against the spec's ASCII composition, which draws 3 named cards plus "(4 more wearables)" (7 total), feeding the header evidence note's "11-card architecture." Built total across all 4 sections is 9 cards, not 11. A "Route: /calendar/connected" implementation string also leaks into visible button copy on the Google Calendar card, and an un-spec'd "Save preferences" `BtnPrimary` appears at the foot of the screen with no basis in Components/Layout/States. Otherwise all named modules (TopBar, CIA note, 4 section groups, Consent controls, nav) are present and in the right order.
2. Canon fidelity — issue: `SolidCard` (the wrapper every `IntegrationCard` uses) takes no `tone`/glow prop at all and only ever renders flat `surface-warm` — none of the spec-mandated per-state semantic glows (orange on Connected, muted orange on Pending, none on unconnected) are rendered anywhere on the screen. Glass tiers, color roles, type scale, radii otherwise conform.
3. Data honesty — issue: the "Auto-retrying in background" status line is placed directly under the WHOOP card (already shown "Connected" + "2m ago") instead of under the Fitbit card (badge: "Sync pending"), where the spec's own ASCII wireframe places it. This makes WHOOP read as simultaneously fully connected/synced and actively retrying, while Fitbit's actual pending state ships with zero explanatory copy. The 3-state honesty pattern (Real/Low-confidence/Honest-null) is otherwise correctly implemented for Connect vs Will-sync copy.
4. Cross-cutting — issue: per-provider consent controls (Source, Scope, Freshness, Retention, Export, Revoke, Delete) on the WHOOP card render via `<Chip tone="muted">` in its default non-interactive mode (plain `<span>`, no button role, no focus, no touch target), even though the kit ships a purpose-built `ConsentRail` component that renders the equivalent list as focusable 44px buttons. Export/Revoke/Delete are consent-critical actions and are inert here — same architectural gap independently flagged High on sibling screen S21 (Provenance-vs-ConsentRail).
5. Composition craft — issue: every non-wearable, non-connected service (MyFitnessPal, Cronometer, Lumen, Google Calendar, Spotify — 5 of 9 built cards) reuses the identical generic `Info` glyph in an identical neutral `bg-white/[0.04]` chip, giving them no source-specific visual identity; only WHOOP gets a distinct tinted icon. Otherwise hierarchy, spacing, and card rhythm read clean in the screenshot.
6. Copy voice — ok: sentence case, second person ("your mission pace"), no exclamations, one Tiempos italic emphasis word ("services") on-screen; apart from the leaked route string (defect 2) and known CIA casing (see below).
