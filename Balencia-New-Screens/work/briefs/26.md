# Functional Content Brief: Fitness & Workouts Dashboard

## 1. Purpose
The Fitness & Workouts Dashboard serves as the user's physical command center, surfacing the CIA-generated daily workout plan alongside wearable recovery metrics. It acts as the canonical template for all Domain Dashboards across the app.

## 2. Entry and Exit Paths
**Entry Paths (Arrives from):**
*   Explore Section [18] via stack push
*   CIA Chat [09] via deep-link stack push
*   Home Screen [12] via action card stack push
*   Life Areas Overview [16] via domain tap stack push

**Exit Paths (Navigates to):**
*   **Screen 27 (Workout Detail / Active Workout):** Triggered by "Start workout" CTA, tapping the workout card body, or tapping the "Log workout" FAB (FAB routes specifically to the manual log template).
*   **Screen 09 (CIA Chat):** Triggered by tapping the CIA coaching note card.
*   **Screen 13 (Goals List):** Triggered by "View all" (pre-filtered to fitness).
*   **Screen 14 (Goal Detail):** Triggered by tapping a specific goal row.
*   **Screen 15 (Create/Edit Goal):** Triggered from empty state prompts.
*   **Screen 19 (RPG Character):** Triggered by tapping the Level badge.
*   **Screen 22 (Connected Services):** Triggered by WHOOP connection prompts.

## 3. Information Architecture
**Scroll Behavior:** ScrollView (moderate content length, not a dynamic list). Tab bar visible.
**Layout (Top-to-Bottom):**
1.  **Fixed Header:** Title ("Fitness & workouts"), back chevron, RPG skill level badge.
2.  **CIA Coaching Note Card:** Contextual AI message based on recent activity/recovery.
3.  **Today's Workout Card:** Primary content; workout plan, exercise chips, primary CTA.
4.  **WHOOP Integration Card:** Recovery metrics; 1 hero gauge (Recovery), 2 secondary gauges (Sleep, HRV), and a readiness charge meter.
5.  **Active Goals Section:** Up to 2 goal progress bars.
6.  **This Week Section:** 7-day status dots, 3 KPI tiles, weekly volume comparison bar chart, 6-week activity trend line, and a streak consistency heatmap.
7.  **Overlay:** Floating Action Button (FAB) for manual logging.
8.  **Bottom:** Global Tab Bar.

## 4. Features and Capabilities
*   **AI Workout Plan Surfacing:** Displays the daily AI-generated workout, including type, duration, and exercise preview chips.
*   **Wearable Data Integration:** Reads and displays WHOOP recovery (hero metric), sleep, and HRV data via connected services.
*   **Readiness/Intensity Charge:** Derives a "readiness for intensity" score from the recovery band, displaying it as a charge meter.
*   **Goal Tracking:** Displays active fitness goals with continuous progress bars.
*   **Activity Trend Visualization:** Plots the last 6 weeks of activity as a continuous trend line, including a dashed AI projection for the upcoming week.
*   **Streak Consistency Tracking:** Renders a non-shaming GitHub-style heatmap of historical workout consistency.
*   **Weekly Volume Comparison:** Juxtaposes current week daily volume against the previous week.
*   **Manual Workout Logging:** Allows users to manually log workouts via a FAB.

## 6. Data Fields and Metrics
*   **Recovery (%):** *Source: WHOOP API.* Displayed in the 96px hero gauge. Drives the intensity charge meter.
*   **Sleep Score:** *Source: WHOOP API.* Displayed in a 48px banded gauge.
*   **HRV (Heart Rate Variability):** *Source: WHOOP API.* Displayed in a 48px banded value gauge (no false % target).
*   **Workout Name/Type/Duration:** *Source: CIA AI Workout Engine.* Displayed in Today's Workout card text.
*   **Exercise Preview:** *Source: CIA AI Workout Engine.* Horizontal scrolling list of muscles/groups.
*   **Goal Progress (%):** *Source: User Goals DB (Fitness domain filter).* Continuous fill bars.
*   **Weekly Stats (Workouts, Active Min, Calories):** *Source: Workout completion log + new mock lastWeek data.* Displayed in KPI tiles with WoW deltas.
*   **Daily Volume (Minutes/Workouts):** *Source: Activity logs + mock daily series.* Drives the weekly volume bar chart.
*   **6-Week Activity Trend:** *Source: New `activityTrend` data (6 points + projection).* Drives the Living Line trend chart.
*   **Historical Streak (Session Load):** *Source: New `streakHistory` data.* Drives the trailing 4-week heatmap.

## 7. Screen States
*   **Cold-start / Day 1:** CIA note is motivational. Workout card offers CIA chat link or starter plan. Gauges are ghosted dashed arcs (no-data ≠ 0%). KPI tiles read "0" with "—" deltas. Trend says "Calibrating". Heatmap says "Your streak starts today".
*   **Rest Day (Zero State):** CIA note references rest. Workout card displays rest day messaging with a secondary "Log a workout" button. Recovery data still renders normally.
*   **Loading:** Depth-preserving skeletons that morph into data (e.g., gauge rings draw via radial shimmer, line charts draw L-to-R). Text uses specific CIA loading copy.
*   **Partial (Un-synced/Un-logged):** Missing WHOOP metrics render as ghosted dashed arcs, completely distinct from real low values. Un-logged days on the bar chart render as ghosted dashed ticks, distinct from true zero-height bars.
*   **Error:** Per-section recovery affordances. Failed cards retain depth but show inline text links (e.g., "Could not load WHOOP data. Retry?").
*   **Offline:** Cached data is retained and displayed. Pull-to-refresh is dimmed. Sticky network banner appears at the top of the scrollable area.
*   **Success:** Triggering the primary CTA initiates a brief green glow transition to the next screen (no success toast).

## 8. Interactions, Gestures, and Motion Intents
*   **Gestures:** Tap (cards, CTAs, chips), Pull-to-refresh (full dashboard data sync), Edge swipe (back nav), Horizontal swipe (exercise chips), Long-press (scrub crosshair on trend chart).
*   **Motion Intent (Draw-First Order):** The 96px Recovery gauge ring draws/fills first (520ms), followed by the 48px Sleep/HRV gauges. The KPI numbers count up. The bar chart bars rise from zero. The 6-week TrendChart *draws itself* L-to-R (1200ms, never opacity-fades), with the dashed CIA projection drawing last. The heatmap cells stagger in.
*   **Scroll Intent:** On scroll down, the FAB fades out (opacity + translateY). On scroll up/stop, the FAB fades back in.
*   **Reduced Motion:** All charts appear instantly at their final drawn state. The Living Line trend is rendered fully static with its CIA projection visible. FAB scroll-hiding is disabled.

## 9. Free vs. Premium Gating
*   **Premium Tier Features:** Expanded stat tiles (PRs, weekly volume trend mini-line, streak count), expanded WHOOP data (resting HR, respiratory rate), full list of active goals (instead of max 2), and the `Sparkline` micro-trend rendered under the lead goal.
*   *(Note: Q17 specifies progress photos are private, encrypted, user-deletable, with AI analysis as premium opt-in, though not directly displayed on this dashboard view).*

## 10. Cross-Domain Hooks
*   **AI Insights (CIA Voice):** The CIA Coaching Note at the top of the screen explicitly cross-references WHOOP recovery data ("Your recovery is high today...") or historical workout volume to dictate the daily plan.
*   **Gamification/RPG System:** The header surfaces the user's RPG Skill Level badge ("Lv.12"), linking physical activity to overarching avatar progression.
*   **Template Inheritance:** The architecture, visualizations, and CIA coaching note format established here act as the canonical "Domain Dashboard A" template, directly hooking into and standardizing Nutrition, Finance, Career, Relationships, Spirituality, Learning, and Creativity domains.

### ⚠️ Source Contradictions Flagged
1.  **FAB Routing:** The `## Layout` section states the FAB taps push to *Screen 27 (blank template)*, but the `## Audit Feedback Integration` explicitly overrides this, stating the FAB must route to a *manual workout logging flow* reserving the active workout for the planned workout. The audit integration (manual log) was treated as the source of truth.
2.  **Data Source Liveness:** The spec describes live WHOOP API syncing, but `## Audit Feedback Integration (Q21)` clarifies Data Sources may act as a demo/no-live-sync trust placeholder for prototype acceptance.
3.  **Signature Definition:** The `## Visualization` section refers to the trend line as the "Living-Line hero trend" (implying it's the signature), but `## Design Elevation` explicitly flags a contradiction, relabeling the trend line a *workhorse* and the warm-glow chart system (gauges) as the true *signature*. Design Elevation was treated as the finalized intent.
