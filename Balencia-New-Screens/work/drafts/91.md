# 2. Purpose
A centralized, vertical scrolling timeline where members discover, share, and engage with community updates, milestones, and cross-domain life activities. It serves as the daily social anchor for the Balencia community, enforcing accountability through shared proof rather than vanity metrics.

# 3. Entry & exit
**Entry paths:**
- Primary: Universal search or direct deep-link from the `Me` tab.
- Secondary: CIA Coach text link ("Your Pod just finished a challenge...").
- Push Notification: Tapping a "New Kudos" or "Comment" alert.

**Exit paths:**
- Tap avatar/name -> User Profile (`/profile/[id]`).
- Tap an embedded proof card -> Domain Detail screens (e.g., `/workouts`, `/goals`).
- Tap a Community/Pod tag -> Community/Pod Detail (`/community/[slug]`).

# 4. Layout anatomy
**Regions top-to-bottom:**
1. **TopBar:** Transparent header with the H1 "Community feed" and search glyph.
2. **Context Selector:** `SegmentedTabs` for switching scopes (All, My Pods).
3. **CIA Contextual Strip:** `CIAInsightCard` condensed to a single-line prompt.
4. **Activity Feed:** Vertically scrolling list of `FeedPostCard`s with pull-to-refresh.
5. **Floating Action Button (FAB):** `BtnPrimary` fixed above the system home indicator for text updates.
6. **GlassNavBar:** Floating pill, tabs (Today, CIA, Goals, Me).

# 9. All states
- **Default:** Chronological list of `FeedPostCard`s intermixed with occasional `CIAInsightCard` strips.
- **Skeleton:** Shimmer blocks (`--surface-3`) matching avatar circles, 3 lines of text, and a block for the embedded metric card. Charts render as ghost lines.
- **Empty:** `EmptyState` component. Illustration-free. "It is quiet in here." text with a `BtnPrimary` reading "Log a win".
- **Error:** `ErrorState` component. Glyph and text: "We cannot reach the feed. Check your connection." with a `BtnSecondary` to "Try again". Never blames the user.
- **Success:** `XPToast` slides down from the top: "+40 XP · Social" with auto-dismiss.
- **Disabled/Offline:** `OfflineBanner` appears under the TopBar: "offline — showing last sync 2h ago". The feed remains scrollable via local cache. FAB is disabled (40% opacity).

# 10. Motion & interaction
- **Feedback:** Tapping the Kudos icon (heart) plays a 200ms spring scale bounce (0.8 to 1.0) with a 10ms light haptic.
- **Glow behavior:** Tapping Kudos on a post temporarily amplifies the card's inner glow (brightness +20%) for 150ms, tying the visual directly to the action.
- **Scrolling:** Scrolling hides the TopBar title to maximize feed real estate. The bar gains a `.glass-pill` blur backdrop once content passes under it.
- **Reduced-motion path:** Kudos bounce and glow amplifications are replaced by an instant opacity swap to the active color. XPToast fades in rather than sliding.

# 11. Motivation-tier adaptation
- **Low density:** Only shows posts with real metrics (proofs) and direct pod interactions. Hides generic chatter/discussions.
- **Medium density:** Default. Shows all post types, standard spacing (16px margins).
- **High density:** Compresses avatars to 24px, reduces card padding, and stacks comments inline beneath the post body by default, reducing tap-depth.

# 12. Accessibility
- **Contrast:** Paper-100 (#FEFAF3) on `--surface-2` (#211008) exceeds AAA contrast ratios.
- **Targets:** All engagement icons (Kudos, Comment, Share) and the FAB strictly adhere to the 44px minimum hit target, visually padded by transparent bounding boxes.
- **Screen-reader:** Glyph-only controls possess hidden `aria-labels` (e.g., `Button. Give kudos. 24 kudos.`).

# 13. Premium checklist
1. **Connects:** Cross-domain metrics (Fitness, Career) appear seamlessly inside the social feed via embedded data cards.
2. **Honest:** The feed respects the 3-state data invariant, muting unverified metrics posted by users rather than blindly trusting their manual text input.
3. **Premium:** Selective solid cards over heavy glass ensures the timeline feels native, fluid, and highly legible—avoiding the "washed out" look of typical social apps.
4. One hero color per surface (Orange FAB, Green verified PR).
5. One BtnPrimary (FAB).
6. One Display/Hero moment (H1 Title).
7. Sentence case, no exclamations (corrected brief copy).
8. One Tiempos italic emphasis per CIA moment (*small*).
9. Tab cap respected (Social is moved to the `Me` hub to avoid violating the 4-tab rule).
10. Semantic glow applied with strict meaning (Green for done, Purple for CIA, None for low-conf).
11. 60/30/10 color ratio maintained.
12. Reduced motion path specified.
13. 44px minimum touch targets enforced on all glyphs.
14. Honest empty and offline states designed with care (no dead ends).
