# 83-social-buddy-profile

## 1. Header
- **Screen ID:** 83
- **Name:** Social Buddy Profile

## 2. Purpose
A trusted-person detail screen serving as the anchor for Balencia's social layer. It shifts the focus from a public feed to deep, one-on-one accountability. The screen visualizes a "relationship shape" via shared domains, tracks active commitments, and provides clear, agency-driven safety controls.

## 3. Entry & exit
- **Entry:** Stack push via deep links from Direct Chat `[75]`, Accountability `[46]`, Community `[40]`, Leaderboard `[39]`, Competitions `[47]`.
- **Exit:** 
  - **Back Chevron:** Pops stack to origin.
  - **Avatar Tap:** Opens ModalOverlay for photo preview.
  - **Shared Mission Card:** Pushes Mission Detail `[14]`.
  - **Bottom Actions (Message):** Pushes Direct Chat `[75]`.
  - **Safety Controls (Report/Block):** Pushes Report / Block `[64]`.

## 8. Data & honesty states
*(Every metric ships 3 states. No fabricated numbers.)*
- **Mission Progress (Real):** `68%` (KPI tabular-nums) + `via Mission check-ins` provenance chip.
- **Mission Progress (Low-confidence):** `~35%` (muted 64% opacity) + `estimated · low confidence` caption.
- **Mission Progress (Honest-null):** Blank progress track + `Not enough data yet — log a workout to sync.`
- **CIA Vetting (Real):** `CIA checked in` pill (active purple).
- **CIA Vetting (Low-confidence):** `CIA partial check` pill (muted purple).
- **CIA Vetting (Honest-null):** `No shared data yet` (neutral grey pill).
- **Relationship Radar (Real):** Solid shapes in `#FF5E00` (user) and `#34A853` (buddy) with overlap.
- **Relationship Radar (Low-confidence):** Overlap blurred 40%, tagged `estimated overlap`.
- **Relationship Radar (Honest-null):** Concentric empty rings + `Connect on a mission to map your relationship`.

## 9. All states
- **Default:** Fully populated cards, real metrics, avatars loaded.
- **Skeleton:** Depth-preserving shimmer blocks (`--surface-3` base) for avatar, text, and progress tracks. Fades/morphs in.
- **Empty (Cold Start):** Hero rendered. Missions replaced by `HonestNullState`: `No shared missions yet. Invite Sarah to a mission to train together.` + `BtnGhost: Invite to mission`.
- **Error:** Cached data retained. Failed mission card wrapped in error border, reads: `Couldn't load shared missions. Pull to refresh or try again.`
- **Removed Buddy (Active Agency - *Resolved Contradiction*):** Reconnect banner at top (`You're no longer connected as buddies. Invite them again to reconnect.`). Hero opacity remains 100%. The `Message` button remains fully clickable at 100% opacity, but on tap triggers a toast: `You can message after they accept.`
- **Pending Buddy:** Hero rendered. Status pill reads `Invitation pending`. `Message` button visually dimmed (40% opacity) + tooltip `Your invitation is pending. You'll be able to message once Sarah accepts.`
- **Disabled:** N/A as a blanket state; agency mandates components remain interactable to deliver clear messaging rather than feeling broken.

## 10. Motion & interaction
- **Easing:** Physical, non-linear easing (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`).
- **Timing:** 150-250ms for all UI feedback. Progress bars sweep over 520ms.
- **Entrance:** Hero fades in instantly. CIA card and Mission cards rise with a staggered effect (40ms delay between each).
- **Glow behavior:** Hero `glow-cia` breathes subtly (4s loop, ease-in-out). On mission tap, glow brightens to 100% opacity.
- **Haptics:** Light impact on avatar tap (preview open), medium impact on successful message send.
- **Reduced Motion:** All entrances load instantly at final state. Progress bars render fully filled without sweep animation. Glow breathe is static.

## 11. Motivation-tier adaptation
- **Low Density (Minimalist):** Radar visualization hidden. Progress bars reduced to text percentages. CIA insight hidden to reduce noise.
- **Medium Density (Default):** Radar visualization active. Progress bars rendered. Core CIA insight visible.
- **High Density (Analytical):** Radar visualization displays numerical percentages per domain axis. Mission cards expand to show delta trends (`▲ 12% this week`) and exact historical check-in counts.

## 12. Accessibility
- **AA+ Contrast:** Body text strictly uses `#FEFAF3` on dark `#140A05` surfaces. 
- **Targets:** All list rows, bottom action buttons, and header controls maintain a strict 44x44px minimum touch target. Inert labels resolved into fully wired touch zones (*Resolved Contradiction*).
- **Screen-reader labels:** Glyph-only TopBar buttons (Back chevron, overflow menu) and Domain icons receive semantic `aria-labels` (e.g., "Back", "Fitness mission", "Learning mission").
- **Shapes:** Meaning is not conveyed by color alone (progress bars pair percentages with text framing like "building momentum").
