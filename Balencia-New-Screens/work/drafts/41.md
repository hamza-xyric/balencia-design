### 9. All states
- **Default:** Day view, current date selected, solid synced events, dashed CIA suggestions.
- **Skeleton:** Shimmer blocks over the timeline; ghost lines for the donut; faded `--surface-3` placeholder cards.
- **Empty (Cold-Start):** Connect Calendar `ConsentCard` prominent. CIA starter tasks in unscheduled. Donut in honest-null.

### 10. Motion & interaction
- **Physical easing:** `cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)` for all horizontal swipes (date nav, swipe-to-dismiss).
- **Feedback (150-250ms):** Event cards scale to `.98` on press. Segmented tabs slide active indicator in 150ms.
- **Glow behavior:** The `glow-you` on the Domain Split card and Current Event card "breathes" (opacity 55% -> 80% -> 55%) over 4s to indicate active tracking.
- **Choreography ("Draw-first"):** When entering Day view, the `TimelineGrid` agenda line draws top-to-bottom. The `ScheduleDonut` arcs sweep clockwise simultaneously.
- **Haptics:** Light impact on segment switch. Medium impact on successful drag-and-drop of an unscheduled task.
- **Reduced-motion path:** Draw-first sweeps replaced with standard opacity fade-ins. Breathing glows lock to static 55% opacity.
