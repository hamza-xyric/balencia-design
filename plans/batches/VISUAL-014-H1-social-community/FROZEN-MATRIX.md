- Approved terminology is binding: **Squad** means temporary Mission group of 2–5; **Community** means persistent group. Pods/Circles/Parties/Guilds are not visible product nouns. Partners are named people/permission relationships, not a third group type.

- Social fixtures are bundled/local previews. No UI claims a live server thread, real verified proof, real contact notification, public publication, moderation action, calendar write, file/share/clipboard action, storage mutation, or provider/device operation.

- S64 default is reason-unselected, block OFF, Submit disabled, `0/500`. Report and block are separate actions. Duplicate reports say `You’ve already reported this`; submitted status is `Pending review`; no outcome/punishment is promised.
- S78 Share/Export/Screenshot Guide are local review previews only. Counts/statuses derive from one report model; source failures are separate states.

- S94 uses an absolute bundled-demo schedule/time zone/freshness, exact recording count, local calendar preview, public-URL-only share preview, and reduced-motion-safe status. No private plan/health data is shared.
- S95 tabs are Squads/Communities. Every Squad is 2–5 and temporary; every Community persistent. Membership/counts are exact; route overlap is absent until granular consent and disappears on revoke.
- Asset dispositions: HIFI-39-01, HIFI-40-01, HIFI-47-01, HIFI-91-01, HIFI-94-01, HIFI-95-01 use deliberate privacy-safe code-native initials/abstract treatment or explicit honest-null/hidden-media state. No raster/generated/provider/personal imagery is added. Screens 46/64/78/82 require none.
