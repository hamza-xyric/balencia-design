## Design/source assessment

### Family coherence and premium hierarchy

- The nine screens consistently use the warm dark atmosphere, selective purple CIA moments, orange effort/action, green only for completed/successful states, restrained glass heroes, solid dense-data surfaces, and persistent shell navigation/action placement.
- Default proofs maintain clear above-fold focal hierarchy at 390×844. The paired top/lower proofs show no dead visual zones, clipped modules, hidden bottom actions, or competing primary actions.
- Long screens remain scannable: compact provenance, restrained section density, consistent radii, and repeated source/data-control treatment make the family feel intentional rather than template-like.

### Numeric and source truth

- S30 visibly reconciles `$650 + $400 + $300 + $800 = $2,150`, `$5,000 - $2,150 = +$2,850`, and `$6,000 / $10,000 = 60%`; savings is presented separately from net change.
- S31 visibly separates Budget and Transaction modes, preserves `480 / 620 = 78%`, keeps pending/offline states non-destructive, and names the selected transaction in confirmation.
- S32 uses `1 remaining`, target-10 skills, and the shared premium-preview visual without unsupported XP.
- S33 shows `84 out of 99`, registered Relationships identity, and mutually distinct real/estimated/null treatments.
- S34 uses Spirituality as member copy for the Faith registry identity, bundled-demo/location-consent provenance, and checked-row-derived completion without an invented competing mission.
- S35 cleanly separates `62% book completion` from `9 of 15 · 60%` daily progress and labels Learning `Explore · non-scored`.
- S36 labels Creativity `Explore · non-scored`, differentiates project completion from practice summaries, and never uses done-green for partial progress.
- S37 keeps sensitive authored content separate from system privacy copy and makes CIA analysis explicitly opt-in.
- S38 renders `5 of 8 = 63%` under nearest-integer rounding, uses registered Wellbeing, and labels the sleep/stretch relationship as association rather than causation with window/sample/freshness/confidence.

### State and interaction fidelity

- Honest-null screenshots suppress unsupported metrics and provide constructive starts; low-confidence/error/offline proofs visibly qualify or suppress derived claims instead of merely changing a hidden marker.
- Dialog/sheet proofs are visually isolated by strong scrims and opaque elevated surfaces. Named delete confirmations, consent previews, timer/log flows, and local-only outcomes match the frozen capability boundary.
- Tabs expose materially different panels; the screenshots for S37 Entries/Check-ins and S38 Today/Week/Month are meaningfully distinct.
- Nine-field data controls are consistently visible where personal, derived, provider, voice, financial, or practice data appears.

### RPG/domain and asset fidelity

- The code-native/no-raster decision is appropriate for all nine specs. No fabricated thumbnails, provider logos, personal imagery, or decorative asset claim appears in the promoted proofs.
