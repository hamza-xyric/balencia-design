## 2. Purpose
A browsable, editable knowledge base that makes the CIA's memory of the user transparent and controlled. It stores compiled chapters of personal details, behavioral patterns, and cross-domain correlations, growing into a comprehensive life record over time.

## 3. Entry & exit
- **Entry Paths:** Pushed via the "book of life" quick link on the Me main root screen (Stack depth 1).
- **Primary Exit:** Back button or iOS edge swipe → pop stack to Me main.
- **Secondary Exit:** Tapping a correlation node → switches to CIA Chat via deep link, passing "explain this correlation" as context.
- **In-screen Transitions:** Entering inline edit mode; confirming/deleting an entry via bottom sheets.

## 4. Layout anatomy
**Regions top-to-bottom:**
1. **TopBar:** Transparent header with back chevron, H1 title "book of life", and a 44px target glyph for wiki settings/consent.
2. **Search Region:** Full-width glass pill input for filtering memories across all chapters.
3. **Chapter Tabs:** Horizontally scrolling pills for the 6 core domains (`about you`, `preferences`, `patterns`, `correlations`, `goals history`, `life events`). 
4. **Chapter Meta & Content Area:** Contextual metrics and the primary content list. Dynamically renders either standard `ListRow` cards or the premium correlation graph.

## 9. All states
- **Default:** `SolidCard` entries populate the vertical scroll. Tabs are settled.
- **Skeleton:** Tabs and meta text shimmer. `WikiCorrelationGraph` shows 3 purple pulsing ghost dots. Entry lists show `SkeletonState` blocks matching card geometry.
- **Empty (Day 1):** Warm `EmptyState` component. Graph shows 3-5 ghosted nodes.
- **Search Active:** Tabs dim to 40% opacity and become non-interactive. Results aggregate into a flat list. Each card gains a `ChipDomainTag` badge for chapter context. `X` icon clears query.
- **Edit Mode:** Card border transitions to 1px solid orange. Title/content bodies become multiline `GlassPillInput` fields. Ghost action row swaps to `[cancel]` `[save]`.
- **Success:** Brief 250ms `--glow-done` green border pulse on the saved card.
- **Error:** Brief 150ms soft red flash on card border + haptic. Error copy: `Couldn't save changes. Try again.`
- **Offline:** `OfflineBanner` appears under TopBar: `You're offline — showing your last sync.` Search input is disabled (grayed). Pull-to-refresh disabled.

## 10. Motion & interaction
- **Easing & Timings:** Physical easing curves only (`ease-out` for entrances, `ease-in-out` for state transitions). Chapter crossfade: 160ms out, 280ms in. Bottom sheets spring up in 250ms.
- **Glow behavior:** `--glow-cia` on the hero graph utilizes the "breathe" effect (4s ease infinite loop) to signify live AI processing. Glows dim to 20% opacity when card is not in viewport.
- **Draw-First Graphing:** When the `correlations` chapter mounts, `WikiCorrelationGraph` draws outward from the center hub. Nodes fade in staggered (20ms/node). Edges stroke-draw (520ms). User-confirmed edges draw as solid orange; AI-inferred edges draw as dashed purple.
- **Haptics:** Light selection haptic on node tap. Medium success haptic on saving an edit. Heavy warning haptic when the "delete" confirmation sheet appears.
- **Reduced Motion:** `WikiCorrelationGraph` stroke-drawing and staggered node fades collapse to an instant, fully-settled opacity:1 final frame. Glow "breathe" stops, holding a static 55% alpha.

## 11. Motivation-tier adaptation
- **Low density (Overwhelmed/Fatigued):** Truncated text (title + 1 line only, via `show more` disclosure). Correlation graph simplifies to just 2 nodes and 1 edge. Confidence percentages hidden in favor of simple labels (`low`/`high`). Source indicators hidden to reduce visual noise.
- **Medium density (Default):** Title + 3 lines of body. Correlation graph shows standard 4-5 node web. Confidence meter visible with percentages and `ChipProvenance`.
- **High density (Curious/Analytical):** Full text expanded. Correlation graph reveals secondary "ghost" nodes showing potential future links. Detailed correlation data points surfaced explicitly in the text (e.g., `r=0.82 correlation over 14 days`).

## 12. Accessibility
- **AA+ Contrast:** Text strictly uses paper-100/50. Secondary text uses paper at 64% opacity (passes AA on dark surfaces).
- **44px Targets:** All inline ghost actions (`edit`, `this is wrong`), graph nodes, and tab pills are bounded to a 44x44px minimum invisible touch target. 
- **Screen-reader labels:** Graph SVG nodes equipped with `aria-label` describing the connection (e.g., "Confirmed connection between sleep and spending, 78 percent confidence"). Settings 'gear' glyph labeled "Wiki data and consent settings".
